## 6. Synthesis — the claim in Bashar's words, and what an experiment must measure

1. Bashar's premise (via Anka): "fundamentally, everything exists everywhere at the same time"; "It's all a matter of localizing the pattern" — grounded in the "prime radiant" picture of one particle moving "at infinite speed" that "can appear next to itself over and over… at the same time."
2. The claim proper: "location is not something an object exists in. Location is a vibrational variable in the object itself" — Bashar's session wording: location is "an actual equational vibrational property of the object", so "an object existing here and then existing here are actually literally two different objects" (two "vibrational equations", "very subtly different").
3. Corollary for places: "everything you consider to be a location has a specific signature frequency to it" (Anka, 2026); and for objects generally: "each object has its own vibration, its own signature frequency by which you can recognize it" (Gravity session, c. 1997–2000).
4. The mechanism: isolate the "locational x, y, z variable" of an object at A, "impose" or "bombard, overwhelm" it with the locational frequency of B; then the object "has to stop existing here, blink out, and start existing here instantly" — "no travel has happened".
5. Ship version: "we isolate our ship in an electromagnetic gravitational bubble, unlock it from any particular reality, impose upon it the vibrational locational equation of where we wish to go… pop, we appear at our destination" — asserted to not violate light speed because nothing moves through space-time.
6. Bashar's own proposed test (the only concrete protocol found): a very flat table ≥ 6–10 ft; a thin-walled hollow copper sphere ~3–4 in, as round as possible; strike it at end A and "measure those vibrations as precisely as you can"; move it to end B and repeat; "find the difference in those two measurements."
7. Predicted outcome if the difference is then "simplified and magnified" and the sphere at A is bombarded (acoustically or electromagnetically) with B's tone: "the sphere will start to roll to location B automatically… and once it arrives in that location will stop and go no further" — or, "if not," it will "disappear from here and wind up over there instantaneously."
8. So the measurable quantities are: (i) the sphere's acoustic/vibrational spectrum (resonant mode frequencies, amplitudes, decay) at A vs. B under identical excitation; (ii) whether any A–B difference exceeds instrument noise and the known confounders (temperature, support coupling, table flatness, air currents, striking repeatability, geomagnetic/gravity gradient along the table); (iii) whether driving the sphere with the "B spectrum" produces any directed motion toward B that a control drive (A-spectrum, random spectrum, silence, reversed positions) does not.
9. Bashar himself leaves the key parameter open — "as soon as your instruments are sensitive enough to tell the difference in the vibration of an object in two different locations" — i.e., the claim predicts a position-dependent signature frequency but states no magnitude, so a null result can always be attributed to insufficient sensitivity; any write-up should state detection limits explicitly.
10. Status: the protocol appears only in session Q&A (c. 2012–2014 and the Dec 7 2014 follow-up) and in Anka's retellings (2014, 2025, 2026); no official handout, no replication, and no physics commentary on it was found anywhere online. Treat all of the above as the claimant's statements.
