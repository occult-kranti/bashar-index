Transcript findings and experiments ↗

What the finished transcription holds: hours, seven languages, music and silence among the creators seen before the registry freeze, and the bridge between the two recognizer models. The registry of ten hypotheses is frozen: H3 passed its registered rule, but the rule was not calibrated at creator level, so H3 is not determined at creator level and was withdrawn; H8 was dropped before it ran; the six hypotheses of the first round were taken to their registered tests on the hold-out creators, and none could be determined (too few eligible creators; for H2, no instrument above its reliability floor); H7 and H10 are not run. The exploratory round was released on 26 Nov 2026 (exploratory; not a test of H1-H10): 172 outputs listed, 111 reported, 60 not run, 1 superseded, with a gallery of 231 figures and model-coded labels not checked by a person. The third-round panel's steps 1 to 5 and its entries that read the audio, with the audio addendum, are published (each entry with its question and its reading in its own words), with their corrections. The new-files round, a descriptive persistence check on new recordings of the seen creators (not a confirmation), is published with its 102 rows. Counts only, never transcript text.

These views answer different questions and use different datasets. A listed video is not proof of a saved recording; a saved recording is not a transcript; a candidate statement is not a verified prediction. Each dashboard keeps those distinctions visible.

No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these recordings and how the creators present it.
