# Astrology, calendars and the "permission slip" in the Bashar transcripts

Research note. Transcripts collected 2026-10-30; written up 2026-11-11. Part of the [transcript stage](../../README.md#stage-7-transcript-collection) of this folder. These are reported as claims made in channeled sessions, not as endorsements.

**Question.** Did Bashar say more about astrology/star signs and calendars as a "permission slip" than that any system works?

**Answer.** A little more, but nothing sign-by-sign. He applies the permission-slip idea to astrology, calendars and tarot by name; says his own civilisation does not use astrology; gives one short list of calendar families (lunar, Venus-coded, Sirius-coded) without ranking them; and says the symbols become unnecessary in the end. No sign traits, horoscopes, birth charts, Mercury retrograde, moon phases, or endorsement of one calendar turned up in the transcripts that could be read.

Quotes are kept to 30 words or fewer, as in the rest of this repository. Bashar® recordings and transcripts are © Darryl Anka / Bashar Communications, Inc.; no transcript text is committed.

My own notes, not Bashar's: the Sirius and Venus families match real traditions (an Egyptian Sirius-based year; Maya Venus tables), and a fixed 13-month lunar year is less standard than 12 months plus an occasional extra.

## What was not found

Sign-by-sign traits; horoscopes; Mercury retrograde; moon-phase guidance; birth charts; any claim that planetary positions shape a person; any ranking or endorsement of one calendar. A different question about "astrological events" in an interview was answered with earthquakes and volcanoes, not astrology.

## Method and limits

* Source: auto-caption transcript pages (youtubetotranscript.com) of YouTube re-uploads of full sessions, searched with regex windows around astrology-, calendar- and permission-slip terms, plus sub-agent sweeps that returned matching passages. See the [manifest](../bashar75_manifest.csv).
* Coverage: 55 of the 75 recordings in `data/videos.json` came back; 20 returned `client_error` and were not retried through other routes. Three full sessions are among the unread (*Levels of Mastery*, *Breaking News – March 15, 2026*, *Cybo*). The set skews to recent uploads, so older sessions are not covered.
* A YouTube Short titled "Power of Natal Astrology" turned up in search but could not be opened, so its content is unknown.
* Auto-captions can mis-hear words. Title dates on re-uploads are the uploaders' labels; only *The Silent Season* and *2026: The Year of Disclosure* match official events. Long sessions were read in page-sized chunks and may not have been read end to end.
* The 75 AI digests in `data/digests/` missed some of these passages (including the Mayan-calendar exchange and the astrology Q&A), so they were used only as a secondary index.
