Sources, date precision and scope

The source is the frozen newest-20 index, with optional matching cached platform metadata. The original approximate index date remains in the row-level export when an exact date is available. Unavailable dates stay unknown; invalid dates stop the build. No video page, audio file or transcript is fetched by this report.

Exact means a valid date field was present in the corresponding cached platform metadata; it does not imply second-level time precision. Index dates are explicitly approximate. Differences between a platform upload, a premiere and a publisher’s podcast edition are not silently treated as identical events.

All selected entries are accounted for in the counters and downloadable dataset. Date charts omit undated x-axis positions, while the age/evidence charts, creator table and exports retain them. The selected latest-20 windows differ across creators, so this is a sample distribution rather than a common observation window.
