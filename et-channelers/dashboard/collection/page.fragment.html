What the states, kinds and columns mean
Captured
Every listed piece of work is in the archive as audio or text, and the person's channel has been listed, so "every" includes its videos.
Partly captured
Some of it is.
Planned: source found
A podcast feed, files, pages or archive.org recordings of their own, or an interview episode with live channeling, are registered outside YouTube, and nothing is fetched yet.
Planned: YouTube only
A channel is known and nothing outside YouTube is lined up to download. An official site, or interviews with no live channeling or not yet classified, may be on record. It waits for the owner's decision on YouTube's robots.txt, and then for a home connection.
Planned: no source yet
No channel or site is on record. Interviews with no live channeling, or not yet classified, may be.
One piece of work
A video and the podcast episode or page that carries the same content count once, and are captured when either side is here. "Videos covered by a twin" counts listed videos whose content is here that way.
Text from the source, text made here
Source text includes publisher transcripts and provided timed text, or YouTube captions. Text made here is speech-to-text generated from archived audio. The dated collection summary above reports current availability; the research tables retain their own snapshot dates.
Here, on a source
What the source yields that is in the archive: audio for a YouTube channel, a podcast, an interview show, files on the maker's own site and archive.org; text for text pages. Audio, a text the source itself published and a text made here are counted beside it, each on its own.
Off by rule
YouTube's robots.txt disallows, for every crawler, the paths that channel listings, captions and audio are requested through. The project obeys robots.txt, so those lanes send nothing until the owner decides to turn them on. "From a cloud server" says only whether a lane would work from one: YouTube also refuses audio and caption requests from cloud servers, so those two would need a home connection as well.
Kinds of interview
Live channeling: a reader of the whole transcript found the entity speaking (or, with no reading, its own signature is in the transcript, or the show notes say the session is channeled live). Conversation: the person speaks as themselves throughout, even when retelling what the entity says. Unclear: marks of an entity's voice and nothing that settles it. Commentary or clip: the person is not a guest. Not classified: no transcript is held yet.
Why it counts as theirs
The evidence that a source is the person's own: declared on their own channel page, served from or linked by their official site, or the show's own feed. A name alone never counts: several people called Michelle Robinson each have a podcast.
Candidate, distinct, with a target date
A candidate is a sentence the rules pass kept as a possible statement about the future. Distinct counts near-copies (the same statement posted twice) once. A target date is the window the time expression gives, counted from the date the text was said: "in two years" in 2021 is 2023; "soon" gets no date.
Audio behind a transcript
For an item with text: downloaded means its recording is here or was and is in the private store; on Drive means every part of it is on Google Drive. A web page has no recording. A YouTube video can only be fetched on the owner's computer. An interview is held back when it is a conversation or a clip rather than live channeling.
