      How these counts were made

      • Channels are public YouTube channels whose titles or descriptions name the entry together with a channelling word (channeled, channeling, transmission, “message from”, canalización and others); channels this index already holds as channellers count without that word. Videos (titles) are distinct videos whose title names the entry, counted when the title carries a channelling word or the video’s channel counts as above; descriptions are not counted, because many channels repeat their list of sources in every description.
      • Sources: this index (observations mostly of 23 October 2026), the Channeling Atlas (research edition of 23 October 2026), the discovery of 20 November 2026 (its harvest of public titles) and the title-and-description search of 27 November 2026 (finished 17:53 UTC), with the RSS feeds and channel descriptions read that week. The table was built on 27 November 2026 at 18:09 UTC, from public metadata only; no transcript was read.
      • A channel can count under several civilizations, so the counts do not add up.
      • Re-upload channels inflate some counts: the index’s source lists include 38 channels that re-upload the Bashar material, which lift Essassani in particular.
      • Names are matched by patterns, not read: a title can name a civilization without claiming to channel it (commentary, reaction, music), so each count is an upper bound. “First year in titles” is a lower bound on how long a name has been used on YouTube, not its first use. “First named in the literature” gives the date the cited source gives, without its author. The table names no channel or person for any entry.

      How to read this index

      “Listed uploads” means a channel’s uploads were read during the original indexing pass. “Research notes” means metadata was recorded without that crawl. Neither is a statement about current availability.

      Relative upload dates, view counts and subscriber totals belong to the original observation. Empty fields are unknown. Rows without a recorded video ID remain visible as part of the original sample.

      This page uses public metadata only. Recordings, full transcripts and private contact details are not included.
