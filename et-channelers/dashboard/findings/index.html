Registered tests so far: H3, tested and dropped

H3 asked whether “I”, “me” and “my” are denser at the start and end of a recording than in its middle, once the first and last three minutes are cut. On the hold-out creators the registered test passed, but the interval over creators includes zero and the larger group seen earlier shows nothing, so the owner dropped it. With nothing cut the contrast is large among the creators seen earlier: the surplus sits in the opening and closing minutes. Beyond the registered test, no estimate by position in the recording is shown for the hold-out creators until the labelling procedure is frozen. Dot: estimate in log units. Line: 90% interval over creators.

How to read this page

The corpus is the newest 20 public recordings of each of 101 creators who say they channel non-human beings. A creator's newest 20 can cover three days or fifteen years, so nothing here is a trend. Each language other than English comes from one or two creators, so only counts are given for it and its hours are pooled with the other languages. Every number the run measured rests on five or more creators. Figures on the first five tabs describe the finished run; the last tab describes an earlier snapshot. The hypotheses will be tested with labels made by a language model, which no person has checked.

The data behind this page

Every figure, chart and table is drawn from these files. They hold counts, distributions and cohort-level estimates only; no file has a row per creator or per recording, and every number the run measured rests on five or more creators. Built 2026-11-21.
