## Method and audit counts

Hashtags such as #channeling and #mediums were discovery vocabulary, never
relationship or ownership evidence. Results were filtered toward practitioners'
own sites and original channel descriptions. Commercial first-party sites are
credible for attributing their own claims, but are not independent corroboration.

relationship proof.
