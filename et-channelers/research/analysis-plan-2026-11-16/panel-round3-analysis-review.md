## 2. Exact `raw_format` gap and minimal safe fix

The inspected data flow is unambiguous:

1. [`youtube_captions.py:154`](../../pipeline/youtube_captions.py#L154) returns persistable provenance with `raw_format = spec['ext']`; it does not emit `ext`.
2. [`drive_ingest.py:1133`](../../pipeline/drive_ingest.py#L1133) creates each journal track from that provenance. ZIP `provenance.json` also retains `raw_format`, and the raw member is explicitly `raw.json3` or `raw.vtt` in `caption_bundle`.
3. [`archive_catalog.py:128`](../../pipeline/archive_catalog.py#L128) currently projects `track.get('ext', '')`. A correctly captured native track therefore has a blank projected `ext`.
4. `caption_track_verified` checks canonical destination, positive byte count, hashes, status and Drive ID; it does not infer or validate text format. The projection defect does **not** negate those stored-object proofs.

Minimal compatibility change for the parent: preserve the existing catalog field name `ext`, populate it from nonempty `raw_format`, and fall back to legacy `ext` only when `raw_format` is absent/empty. Permit only `json3` and `vtt`; leave truly missing format explicitly unknown. Reject malformed nonempty values and conflicting nonempty fields through the catalog's existing validation behavior rather than guessing. A later schema revision may expose canonical `raw_format`, but renaming the current field alone would needlessly break readers. Do not mutate historical journals or immutable ZIPs.

Focused future regression cases: native `raw_format=json3`; legacy `ext=vtt`; missing format; malformed nonempty format; conflicting formats. For accepted cases, assert identical Drive ID, remote path, size and hashes before/after projection. The reader still compares projected format with ZIP provenance and member name before parsing; the ZIP provenance's raw hash also permits checking the extracted raw member. These are proposed tests, not tests executed in this review. Recommendation sent to the parent; no code edited here.

## 3. Sampling, human validation and uncertainty

The consolidated sampling register must name each target and its inclusion mechanism. A defensible structure is:

| Panel | Intended purpose | Contract to freeze |
|---|---|---|
| Development | Revise rubrics, prompts, regex, decoding and normalization | Separate reviewed recording/leakage groups; no later recycling into an untouched test. ROUND1's 30-work target is an envelope, not a completed set. |
| Extraction probability sample | Estimate missed and spurious claim mentions in the frozen observed English view | Resolve 120-versus-80 proposal explicitly; retain every window's first- and second-stage inclusion probability, including regex-negative/LLM-negative windows. |
| ASR calibration subset | Compare errors, timing and downstream sensitivity under bounded compute | Six development and eighteen held-out excerpts may nest in the appropriate frames, but their balancing and selection probabilities must be recorded separately. Ninety test minutes are not a population prevalence estimate. |
| Person-held-out / future-period tests | Assess transfer to unseen people or later eligible periods | Separate manifests and contamination checks; neither result follows automatically from a family-hash split. A guest's held-out speech cannot enter training through a host's interview. |

For extraction, ROUND2's weighted TP/FP/FN totals and ratio metrics are appropriate design targets when the stated probabilities and matching rules hold. The neither-detector stratum is essential to recall estimation. Fix the redistribution rule for empty/small strata before sampling. Preserve positive inclusion probabilities, survey strata, family clusters and finite-population information; do not use an ordinary binomial interval on the enriched sample. Report undefined precision/recall when the corresponding denominator is zero, rather than quietly replacing it with 0 or 1.

Human labels must be independent of the system's suggested labels and selection mask. Context/audio access should follow one written protocol for all sampled windows. Record uncertainty, unresolvable speaker identity and inaudible spans explicitly. Adjudication does not erase the original labels; report class-specific disagreement and uncertainty, not just aggregate agreement. Predefine treatment of unscorable evidence and show sensitivity bounds rather than dropping difficult items from the main result. A fixed dev/test boundary applies to rubric changes as well as model changes.

One hundred name mentions in eighteen recordings are not one hundred independent people or independent error trials. Zero observed hallucinations in ninety minutes does not establish a zero rate. The ASR thresholds WER ≤15%, names ≥90% and numbers/dates ≥95% remain proposed tolerances, not achieved results or automatically justified release thresholds. The relevant downstream harm differs: a wrong name, negation or prediction date can reverse attribution despite low overall WER. Exact quotes and consequential claim fields retain source review requirements.

Use a planning calculation after development annotation establishes label prevalence, per-family variability and annotation time. Until then, neither 80 works nor 640 windows supplies a known precision/power guarantee. Report the scope of inference as the frozen observed frame; generalization to unobserved channels or missing-language/blocked-caption material needs additional assumptions or data.

## 4. Ontology, attribution and scientific evidence boundaries

Accepted distinctions must survive machine-readable exports, not just prose caveats:

- **Host, owner and speaker:** Multidimensional Soul/@Akanksshaa has an established collection identity and a sourced self-description. Those facts do not assign every guest's speech or entity to Akanksshaa. Anonymous diarization labels remain recording-local. An embedded interview or testimonial link does not establish ownership; discovery ROUND2 correctly excludes those cases.
- **Person versus collection:** Pia and Cullen are distinct people sharing the `laarkmaa` collection. The primary Quantamo attribution is Pia-specific. A shared channel cannot propagate all person-specific relationships to every participant.
- **Relationship versus event:** A biography, event list or series schedule can support a sourced attribution without proving a speaker/entity assignment for every episode. Keep relationship type and evidence level in the graph. Code/light-language, contact and guidance records are not interchangeable with named spoken-channeling recordings.
- **Concept versus instance:** “Jesus/Yeshua,” “Arcturian” and other comparison families are grouping labels. They neither assert external identity nor establish that two claimants refer to the same instance. Shared-label graphs and instance graphs answer different questions.
- **Measurement versus report:** Proposed fields include `frequency_sense`, `reported_value`, `reported_unit`, `measured_variable`, `measurement_evidence_status` (`not_reported`, `speaker_reports_measurement`, `source_reviewed`, `unresolved`), apparatus/procedure and evidence locator. `source_reviewed` still does not imply valid instrumentation or causal interpretation; record appraisal separately. A speech waveform cannot test an alleged biological/spiritual mechanism.
- **Healing assertion versus intervention evidence:** Separate testimony, case/before–after observation, comparative study, randomized result and synthesis. A linked paper must match population, intervention, comparator, outcome and time before any support relation is assigned. Repeated citations to one underlying study are not replications. Risk-of-bias appraisal concerns a specific result; GRADE concerns a body of evidence for an outcome. Neither a confidence score nor a testimonial produces an efficacy conclusion.

Also retain `not_searched`, `search_incomplete`, `no_matching_evidence_found`, `indirect_evidence`, and `reviewed_result` as distinct evidence-search states. “No evidence found” under a limited search is not evidence that an assertion is false. “A practitioner reports X” can be supported while “X occurred” remains unverified. No human intervention or treatment recommendation is authorized by this analysis plan.

## 5. Meaningful questions, null controls and figure axes

The falsifiable questions in analysis ROUND2 are worthwhile if their operational definitions remain visible. A topic model can test whether language distributions differ under a fixed view; it cannot establish what caused the difference. A bipartite graph can describe reviewed attributed-source overlap; it cannot establish an external communication network.

For graph nulls, preserve the relevant exposure/node-presence mask and relationship type. A constrained degree-preserving swap procedure needs feasible swaps and adequate mixing; a frozen chain cannot generate a meaningful p value. If no defensible exchangeability/null argument exists, publish descriptive effect sizes and sensitivity rather than a significance claim. Repeated parameter selection and preprocessing must be accounted for in any confirmatory test, not treated as fixed after inspecting outcomes. Keep optimization variability, sampling uncertainty and null variation separate.

| Figure | Required axis/denominator semantics | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage heatmap | Named source-person or channel rows, explicit period columns, separate panels for eligible words, known hours and observed/eligible works; unknown states shown separately | Calling channel counts “speakers,” encoding missing as zero, or treating caption time extent as speech coverage |
| Extraction estimates | Precision/recall on 0–1 scale, design-based interval meaning, weighted counts plus actual labeled counts, class and frame labels | A smooth PR curve from one binary operating point; pooled intervals reused for sparse subgroups |
| ASR/retrieval comparison | Paired family/query differences; WER denominator and normalization; time in seconds or RTF; peak RAM/VRAM in explicit units | Cropped magnitude bars; author benchmark speeds presented as local; cross-model panels using unequal prompts/budgets without disclosure |
| Topic dynamics | Period/date basis on x; explicitly labeled normalized NMF weight, cluster share or token prevalence on y; fixed exposure and outlier panel | Calling arbitrary normalized NMF weights calibrated probabilities, connecting unknown periods as zero, or interpreting smoothed continuity as discovery |
| Claimed-source matrix | Human rows, either source-scoped instances or declared comparison labels as columns; binary reviewed relationship presence or distinct recording-family support, never an unlabeled mixture | Treating 101 page relationships as 101 independent entities/utterances; contact/code edges silently pooled with speech |
| Community stability | Co-assignment values 0–1; resolution/coupling axes with exact settings; fixed-node and missing-node conventions | Node position/edge length as measured psychological distance; absent nodes as isolates; forced persistence counted as independent evidence |
| Prediction timeline | Recording/utterance-date interval when known, otherwise labeled publication date; target windows, censoring/maturity and unresolved outcomes | Postdiction scored as forecast; future outcomes marked failure; numerical calibration of unstated probabilities |
| Frequency/healing audit | Claim sense/type versus evidence-search/appraisal status, with denominators and uncertainty in human classification | A medical efficacy leaderboard, a “truth” axis from mention counts, or a forest plot without comparable independent effects and a synthesis protocol |

Magnitude bars start at zero; justified difference plots may use a clearly labeled focused scale. Retain unknowns and uncertainty in exports as well as tooltips. Publish source tables, transformation/configuration hashes, units and figure code with vector PDF/SVG. There are no rendered figures in this review, so visual accessibility, clipping and export behavior remain future checks.
