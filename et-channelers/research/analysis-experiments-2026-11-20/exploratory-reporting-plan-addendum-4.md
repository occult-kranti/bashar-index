## 1. The agnosticism sentence (r3-P35)

- **What the frozen text says.** `hypotheses.md` 14.0, *Terms*: "These follow the creators' own framing; the text takes
  no position on whether any being speaks."
- **Reading.** Every registered hypothesis, exploratory question, verdict and description is read with the sentence the

## 2. Voice definitions centred on the creator (r3-P35)

- **What the frozen texts say.** `hypotheses.md` 14.0: "*Claimed voice*: speech that a creator presents as a channelled
  or relayed voice; within it, *embodied* (the being speaks in the first person) and *relayed* (the creator reports in
  their own voice what a being says)". The registry's amendment A1: "span labels (a being's voice, relayed or embodied,
  against the creator's own voice)".
- **Reading.** The definitions are read with the creator as their grammatical subject. The *first-person claimed voice*
  (the frozen instrument's code V2, "embodied"): the creator speaks in the first person as a named non-human source.
  The *reported claimed voice* (code V3, "relayed"): the creator reports what a source is said to say. The *own voice*:
  the creator speaks as themself. A voice is "presented as" a source's, never "authored by" one: under full trance a
  creator may deny being a conscious author, and filter doctrines claim part of the authorship (programme section 5).
  H4's "embodied claimed voice" reads "the first-person claimed voice (code V2)". The frozen instruments, their codes and
  their prompts do not change.

## 4. H6's own text names the own-voice frame as its mechanism (r3-P35; objection O4)

- **What the frozen text says.** `hypotheses.md`, H6, *Why either outcome matters*: "Keeping the offer in the own-voice
  frame is the mechanism by which this happens, not a confound to remove."
- **Note.** H6's registered text names the own-voice frame as the mechanism of the result it predicts. In this corpus
  the own voice concentrates in openings and closings, so H6's design cannot separate voice from position: "voice and
  position cannot be separated in this corpus" is a printable result (programme section 4.1, O4). Every statement about
  where asks fall by voice (H6, LAB-A, PT-cd, and the programme's r3-P05 and r3-P06) is read as placement that may not be
  separable from position, unless a position-matched row says otherwise. Beside it stands LAB-A's caption fact: the
  known-answer battery scores an own offer inside a claimed passage as own voice and holds no arm with an offer
  presented in the source's voice, so only the programme's arm A (class c, unfunded) could show whether the labeller
  moves offers out of the claimed voice. H6 was not determinable on the hold-out; no verdict changes.

## 5. The registry's novelty statements, narrowed (ruling 13; programme section 3.3)

Each statement keeps "not found in N searches", which means only that. `hypotheses.md` 14.1 is not edited.

- **N2 (the claimed voice against the speaker's own voice).** Narrowed. Close prior work, beside the laboratory voice
  comparison N2 already names (Wahbeh and colleagues 2019): comparisons of trance against waking texts in small groups,
  heteronym stylometry and speaker attribution under recognizer error (the prior work of the programme's N11,
  section 3.1). What remains is the comparison inside naturalistic published recordings, within speaker and across
  many creators; never "the first".
- **N3 (a ledger fixed before the dates pass).** Narrowed to the timing of the freeze (the programme's N21): a
  retrospective audit of published predictions with verdict classes (grey literature) and automated resolution of
  forecasts anticipate the rest.
- **N5 (a) (where in a fixed window invented text falls).** Add Wang and colleagues 2024 (Simul-Whisper, Interspeech
  2024, doi 10.21437/Interspeech.2024-1814), who report words truncated at chunk boundaries. "Documented" stays the
  right word. An informal, uncited practitioner report also proposes that a short final window prompts a closing
  courtesy phrase; the working paper's seam sentence says so since 2026-11-26.

## 8. Nulls are read with their detectable sizes (r3-P35; the lead's step-1 decision 29)

- **Reading rule.** A null that an output of this plan reports is read with its detectable size where one has been
  printed from its interval (r3-P33: 80% power, one-sided 5%, normal approximation). E9's null (phrasing shared within
  title families) could detect only about 2.6 shared runs per pair on 6 creators, so it says little. A null without a
  printed detectable size (among them SD's same-date vocabulary, SD-H, TM-H and rv-L2's label flips across
  recognizers) is not read as independence, as the absence of an effect or as a market. The rule adds a reading; it
  selects nothing.
