# Hold-out contacts of the exploratory round

Exploratory (registry A2); not a test of H1-H10.

The public contact log of the exploratory round (2026-11-21 to 22) and of its two release passes (2026-11-22 and
2026-11-25), assembled 2026-11-25 (UTC, from the clock) by the collector of release pass 2. It is drawn from the
package logs as the round's collector put them in time order (2026-11-22, about 02:00 and 04:40 UTC), from the contact
logs of the release-pass groups, and from the lead's decisions D6, D7, D9, D31, D37, D38, D41 and D42. The same rows are
entered in the registry's hold-out contact log. Times are UTC, from `date -u` or from the scripts' own clocks.

**What a contact is** (plan section 5; D6 as read by D31). Any operation that opens, loads, sends to a model, displays or
derives a value from a hold-out or other-group recording's text, timing by position, page or metadata fields, or model
labels; and any read of a file that holds measured values of the hold-out or the other group at the creator, recording
or span level (text, labels, features, per-creator or per-recording derived values, caches). A search that scans such a
file's contents is a contact even when nothing is displayed. Not contacts: the run ledger's metadata fields (counts,
durations, dates, languages, recognizer model), TABLE's whitelisted columns, matching identifiers as strings, and
reading published aggregates.

**Correction (release pass 2, fix step, 2026-11-26).** Rows 12 to 20 were dated 2026-11-22 in the first
assembly; they ran on 2026-11-21 between 23:41 and 23:59 UTC (the packages' logs and the status table's run
times agree), and row 19 ended at 00:10:50 on 2026-11-22. The rows keep their order, which is now the time order.

**What this log does not print.** No creator, channel, video id, title or quotation; no private path or salted file
name; no per-creator size or count; no recording count of the hold-out that is not already published. Files are named
by their kind.
