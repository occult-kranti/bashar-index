Proposed rule for future external calls (D8):
1. Before a window is sent, drop every unit that the voice map (run locally or by the small model on seen creators only) labels V4 (another participant's turn), and every unit of a recording whose claimant is guest_or_client or several_people, unless the hypothesis needs it and the owner approves that instrument in writing.
2. Recordings whose format is audience_session or conversation are sent only as the creator's own units (V1-V3 of the host or sole speaker).
3. Health, belief and personal-circumstance statements of clients and audience members are never sent, even when they sit inside a creator's unit: a local pattern list (illness, diagnosis, medication, bereavement, a named private person) blanks such units first, and the count of blanked units is logged.
4. The ledger logs, per request, the recording hash and the number of words sent, so that the inventory above can be exact.
5. Zero data retention is requested from the vendor before any further transcript is sent; until it is granted, Section 8 says that inputs may be kept up to 30 days in abuse-monitoring logs.
6. Batch input and output files are deleted after collection and the deletion is logged (a files_deleted row per job); the open self-test batch is closed and its file deleted.
Model-coded labels decide what is withheld; their errors are reported as such (the pilot share above is model-coded and has no human check).
