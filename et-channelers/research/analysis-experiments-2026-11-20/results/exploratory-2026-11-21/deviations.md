# Departures from the exploratory reporting plan in the exploratory round

Exploratory (registry A2); not a test of H1-H10.

The public deviation table of the exploratory round (2026-11-21 to 22) and of its release passes (2026-11-22 and
2026-11-25), assembled 2026-11-25 (UTC, from the clock) by the collector of release pass 2 from the round's collected
departures (2026-11-22, about 02:00 and 04:45 UTC), the release passes' records and the lead's decisions D1 to D70. Every
row gives its decision. The same rows are entered in the registry's table "Deviations from the frozen registry" (rows 174 to 176,
release pass 2's first three fix steps of 2026-11-26, since the third fix step, D64); rows 177 (the fourth fix step (fix5)) and 178 (the fifth fix step (fix6)) are entered here, and the registry's row of 2026-11-26 04:45 describes both in general terms. **No
departure below changes a registered verdict:** the first-round tests had run and been published before the round
began.

**Direction words** (as in the registry's deviation table): *deviation*, a departure from a rule, with less reported or
a different procedure; *disclosure*, a privacy rule that hides values and chooses none; *post hoc*, chosen after a result
was seen, and labelled so; *added*, a choice the plan left open, fixed in code before the first run and labelled "added
after the exploratory reporting plan"; *neutral*, no effect on what is estimated; *correction*, a wrong statement or
figure corrected (plan P6), or a factual correction made by addendum 3. "Recorded; no lead decision needed" marks a
departure that the package recorded and labelled as the plan requires, on which the lead took no separate decision.

**Still open.** The deletion of private files with hold-out text (D10) is still to be made. Four agreement figures
were not recounted by creator; each is withheld or no longer printed (row 167). Owner's items: the registry's
decision row of 2026-11-21 19:30 quotes the R-hold voice gate A share (a registered row, not edited), and the
creator-count pairs that were public before the round are listed in addendum 3 item 9. The nesting of H3's seen
sets (61, 64) against seen groups of 57 to 66 creators needs a contact to verify (D66). R5 (HOLD2's scope cuts)
is accepted as a reported departure (D64).

Rows R1 to R7 apply to the whole round; the numbered rows follow by output, then the departures from the contact rules,
the release-review edits, the factual corrections of addendum 3 and the decisions of the release passes.
