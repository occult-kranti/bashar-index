# Package LAB: first-round labels on seen creators as model-coded descriptions

Exploratory (registry A2); not a test of H1-H10. Model-coded descriptions of first-round labels on seen creators (exploratory reporting plan, section 6.8).

Exploratory reporting plan, section 6.8 (LAB-V, LAB-A, LAB-F, LAB-S, LAB-T, LAB-K). Seen creators only; no hold-out contact; no model call. Every label is model-coded and not checked by a person; agreement between models is agreement, not a measure of correctness. Distributions over creators only: no creator, recording, title or quotation appears. The voice label responds to punctuation and case.

## Printing rules

- Seen creators only (71 creators, 1,293 recordings); no hold-out or other-group file was opened, so no contact. No model was called. Code written after the first round on frozen labels (status L; the plan's freeze-2 code rule concerns F2 outputs only). Intervals: 95% creator-bootstrap (2,000 draws; the plan fixes no level; rv-L1 used 95%). Small-cell rules: rows under 5 creators withheld; statistics only with 5 or more creators carrying a non-zero value; cells that add up to a known total (codes of a partition, sides of a voice, classes of a creator's asks) are pooled by the plan's section 3 rule, never suppressed one by one; a row whose difference from a printed row, or from the sum of two printed rows, rests on 1 to 4 creators is not printed (addendum 2, item 9); creator counts lying 1 to 4 from a count they may be nested with are withheld or printed as 'all but fewer than 5'; outer quantiles only for rows of 20 (10) or more creators, and any quantile only where it is 0 with 5 or more creators at 0 or has 5 or more creators with a non-zero value at or below it and 5 or more at or above it. Every statistic cell is entered in a private ledger and lab_check.py recomputes from it every cell a reader could solve from the printed cells (it fails if one rests on 1 to 4 creators) and applies the pairs rule to all printed cells. Agreement figures in the description lines are those of the paper's measurement table rv-D5, the results file or the registry, or recounted under the registry 22:33 rule (as applied to G at 00:13); figures failing it read 'not published (fewer than 5 creators)'. These printing rules were tightened at build after some renderings were seen (and again after the verifier's review of 2026-11-22); they hide values and choose no estimate.
