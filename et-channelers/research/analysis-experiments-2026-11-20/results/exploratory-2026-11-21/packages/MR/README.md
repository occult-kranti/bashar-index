# Package MR: label-error checks of the span contrasts (MR-18c, MR-18d, nlp-R7)

Exploratory (registry A2); not a test of H1-H10. Added after the exploratory reporting plan (second wave of the exploratory round, lead's decision D4 of 2026-11-22).

Seen creators only (71 creators; 1,284 recordings of the English-only recognizer model). No hold-out or other-group label, text, feature or derived file was opened; run-ledger and bridge-ledger metadata were read for seen rows and channel names only. Voice and ask labels are model-coded: voice labels model-coded; verified by a third model of the same vendor (kappa 0.79 on the VER-3R sample), a reader of another vendor (VG-6 kappa 0.91) and known-answer texts (claimed recall 0.93 relayed, embodied not published: misses from fewer than 5 creators; an upper bound); not checked by a person; spoken asks model-coded; verified on known-answer texts (found 0.98, kept as pay-or-enrol 0.97; an upper bound) and by reliability gates on R-hold; not checked by a person (H6). Agreement between models is agreement, not a measure of correctness. Intervals are 90% creator-bootstrap intervals unless a file says otherwise. No figure, cell or statistic rests on fewer than 5 creators, and no share is printed whose complement (drops or misses) comes from 1 to 4 creators; counts of nested groups 1 to 4 apart are stated against each other. The registered specifications are the paper review's (MR-18 parts c and d; R7); the choices they leave open were fixed before the first result.

Notes:

- MR-18c's rate is a recognizer-output measure (the recognizer's words and word times), not a direct measure of speaking. Its R-squared is that of a regression within recordings (the review's text says within-creator).
- MR-18d's post hoc diagnostic was chosen after the result (plan P4) and is labelled so in its file; its H1 rows run the same permutation on constructed values, not data. Times of the choice (UTC, 2026-11-22): chosen after MR-18d's first permutation table (02:29:40) and first written at 02:42:31; its claimed-run rows and H1's constructed rows were added after the correctness review (code last changed at 04:10:57); run of record 04:17:04 to 04:18:44.
- nlp-R7's interval classes say only where each interval lies against the conditions of the amended version of the reading rule (for example 'includes the threshold 0.95' for H1, 'wholly above the threshold 0' for H4, 'wholly below the threshold 1' for H6), without the Holm step; every row says that it is a description of seen creators, not a test, and that the registered verdicts are the first-round results of 21 November (for H1, H4 and H6 not determinable). Worded under the lead's decision D36 of 2026-11-22 by `code/release_d36.py` (added after the exploratory reporting plan; wording only). Its gap columns are recognizer-output gaps.
- The figure `nlp-r7-boundary-jitter.png` was redrawn in the release pass with the H1 panel's 'smallest effect' label moved clear of the bars (same values, from the run of record).
- Smallest effects of interest drawn in the figures: H1 ratio 0.95 (log -0.051), H4 0.5 SD units, H6 ratio 0.5 (log -0.693).
