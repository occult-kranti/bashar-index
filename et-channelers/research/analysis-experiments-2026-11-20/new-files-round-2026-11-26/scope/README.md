# New-files round: scope

Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.
New recordings of seen creators: a descriptive persistence check, not a confirmation and not a fresh-creator wave.
Rule-based and recognizer-based; not checked by a person; agreement, never accuracy.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these recordings and how the creators present it.


Written 2026-11-27T05:38:02Z (UTC, from the clock) by the new-files round's computing agent (a model agent working for the lead; not a person).

`public/data-scope.csv` gives the recordings and creators of the round in band form, by source. It was built from ledger and queue fields only, with no transcript opened for it.

- **Who is kept.** Seen creators only. A video in both sources is kept once, as its new-uploads copy (decision 119.3).
- **Who is dropped.** Hold-out, other-group and unlisted recordings are dropped before any text is read. They are never opened (decision 119.4: they stay behind an allow-list).
- **Bands.** The bands are those fixed in the plan (section 1.3). Each leaves 5 or more admissible values. Recordings are not creators.
- **Two sources, two directions in time.** The new uploads are later than the latest-20 capture. The latest-50+ batch sits mostly at older listing positions (21 to 50).
- **Language.** Language was set to English by the transcription jobs (channels whose earlier recordings were all English). It was not detected per recording.
