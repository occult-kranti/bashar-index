## 4. Source: et (round 3, section 1.3, P48: et-3's estimand): the smallest effect and the negative-control prediction fixed now

I adopt the window version as the estimand. I keep the smallest effect fixed on substantive grounds and do not raise
it to look feasible.

- **Units.** Windows of 150 words centred on each body-text mention of a valence-A family (A windows) and on each mention
  of a federation or star-nation family (F windows), from any recording. Control terms are counted outside a core of
  ten words either side of the name (den-meth). E11's frozen list is used after P41's disjointness rule, with
  suppression terms removed.
- **Primary.** A Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio over creators of the share of windows holding a control term, A windows
  against F windows. The interval is a 90% creator-cluster bootstrap. Creators need 5 or more windows of each class. A
  pooled ratio avoids the zero-count problem stat raised for creator-level log ratios (C16). The creator-level mean
  difference of window shares is a row.
- **Smallest effect: a risk ratio of 1.5, fixed now**, before P70 is computed. My reason: in the genealogical
  literature the adversaries are the agents of control. If the strands are distinct in speech the contrast should be
  large, and a ratio below 1.5 would mean the strands are not told apart by control talk.
- **Telling a fusion from no difference.** If the ratio is near 1, the absolute creator-balanced rates of the A and F
  windows decide what it means. Both high, with a large fusion share, reads as fusion (the federation as a protagonist
  of control talk, emic's 2020s reading). Both low reads as no strand difference. Consciousness-shift vocabulary in the
  same windows is the negative control; I predict a ratio near 1 there.
- The recording-level version (classes A, M, F, N) stays a description. Registration only if P70 finds the window
  version feasible on a collection the harvest can supply. Otherwise it stays a description with its detectable size.
