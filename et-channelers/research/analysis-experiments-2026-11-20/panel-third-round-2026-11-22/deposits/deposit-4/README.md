- **Reference rows.** Years named in a plainly biographical frame, showing what the cue families do on ordinary
  biography. 1844 and 1954, the literature's archetypes in deposit 3's list L15, are described only.

## How a statement is placed

By the position of its stated target relative to the recording's upload date:
- a target already passed at upload goes to r3-P40;
- a target still open at upload goes to r3-P38;
- no findable target means unclassed.

## What is printed

Cue families near target years, by voice, per the programme. The ordered cue classification is shown only as "the first
matching cue family", never as a frame. Rows on fewer than 5 creators with any classified cue family are withdrawn. The
wording never says that a date failed.
