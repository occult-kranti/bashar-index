# The lead's default margins, readings and intervals for the class-a programme (2026-11-26, about 02:05 UTC)

Fixed by the lead before any class-a entry has run, and before any of their outcomes exists. The programme
(`programme.md`, revised 00:56 UTC) states no smallest effect for any of its 56 distinct primaries and names reading B
without defining it (step-0 report). This file sets both, by measure type, for every class-a primary whose entry states
no margin. Where an entry does state one, the entry's own value holds. It is deposited with deposit 2, before any run.
Exploratory: a reading is a description, never a test of H1 to H10, and never a statement about whether any non-human
source speaks.

## Smallest effect of interest (margin), by measure type

| Measure type | Margin |
|---|---|
| Ratio of rates or durations, claimed against own voice or another contrast | outside 0.80 to 1.25 (absolute log ratio of 0.223 or more), the band E1 used |
| Difference in shares or probabilities | 5 percentage points |
| Standardised difference | 0.20 between-creator SD (the unit BR-GS used) |
| AUC or a probability of superiority | 0.10 away from 0.50 (H3's registered smallest effect was 0.10 on its U scale) |
| Correlation or agreement coefficient (kappa, alpha, r) | 0.10 |
| Count of creators or recordings moving a bin | not a margin quantity: described only |

## Readings

Each primary is read on its interval at the chosen level, against its margin, after the admissible references the entry
names (for the voice contrasts these are r3-P04's cross-creator transplant matched on format and the position-matched
reference; for instrument checks they are the entry's own placebo or reference).

- **Reading A ("beyond structure"):** the interval lies wholly beyond the margin, in the entry's named direction, under
  every admissible reference.
- **Reading B ("accounted for"):** the reference-adjusted contrast's interval lies wholly inside the margin under every
  admissible reference. The contrast is explained, within the margin, by the structure, position or instrument that
  the references hold fixed.
- **Undetermined:** anything else. This covers an interval that crosses a margin, admissible references that disagree,
  and a reference that does not hold its size at the count (r3-P30).
- **Not computed:** fewer than 5 eligible creators.

## Intervals, tiers and multiplicity

- **Interval.** 90%, by the method r3-P30's calibration battery selects for the design class at that creator count. Until
  r3-P30 has run, the t interval on creator means is used, the one CAL shows near nominal coverage, and the caption says
  so.
- **Tiers** (r3-P31). A data-tuned rule with 20 or more outcome-blind eligible creators is read on the sealed half; with
  10 to 19, by two-fold cross-fitting; with fewer, only as a description. Lists written blind from the literature run
  once on all non-development creators.
- **Multiplicity.** No family-wise adjustment is applied to exploratory readings. Each output prints the number of
  primaries (56) and the half-effect disclosure: if every true effect sat at half its margin, about 0.05 × 56 ≈ 2.8
  readings of A would be expected by chance at the 90% level's one-sided 5%. A reading A is never called a finding.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.
