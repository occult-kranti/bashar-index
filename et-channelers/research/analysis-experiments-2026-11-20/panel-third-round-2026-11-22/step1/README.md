# Third-round programme, step 1: the entries

Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10. Seen creators only. Model-coded labels, not checked by a person; agreement, never accuracy.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

Step 1 of the third-round panel's [programme](../programme.md) ran on 2026-11-26 (UTC), after
[deposit 2](../deposits/deposit-2/README.md) was merged. It has 17 entry folders: r3-P30, the run of step 0's
calibration method card, and 16 entries that cover all 23 step-1 programme ids; r4-P01, r4-P02, r4-P04, r4-P05, r4-P06,
r4-P09 and r4-P11 are carried inside their r3 partners, as each entry's README says. Every entry was written by a model
agent working for the project lead (not a person). No model or API call was made in step 1 and nothing was spent. No
entry made a hold-out or other-group contact (each entry's `contacts.md`). This index was assembled at
2026-11-26T08:08:54Z (UTC, from `date -u`) from the entries' public files, updated at 2026-11-26T12:18:38Z with decision 64, at 2026-11-26T12:07:57Z with decision 63, at 2026-11-26T11:54:44Z with decision 62, at 2026-11-26T11:06:01Z with decision 59, at 2026-11-26T10:54:34Z with decision 56, at 2026-11-26T10:22:27Z with decision 53, at 2026-11-26T09:36:39Z with decision 50, at 2026-11-26T09:24:29Z with decision 49, and at 2026-11-26T08:41:45Z with the
lead's decisions 42 to 47.

**Multiplicity** (deposit 2). No family-wise adjustment is applied to exploratory readings. The programme has 56
primaries; if every true effect sat at half its margin, about 2.8 readings of A would be expected by chance at the
deposited one-sided 5%, and up to about 6.1 for the share, standardised, AUC and rank-correlation classes (r3-P30's
simulations, not any outcome). A reading A is never called a finding.

**Reading words** (deposit 2): A, the interval lies wholly beyond the margin in the named direction; B, it lies wholly
within the margin; undetermined otherwise. Descriptions and acts carry no reading. Several entries use their own words
for A and B, quoted below.

## What each folder holds

- `README.md`, `status.json` and `contacts.md` of the entry, and its prespecification: `prespec.md` (with
  `prespec-record.md` where the entry wrote one). The prespecifications of r3-P30, r3-P02 and r3-P04 (whose
  prespecification covers r3-P05 and r3-P06) were fixed in the entries' private code as `code/prespec.json`. r3-P02's is
  published verbatim as `prespec.json`; those of r3-P30 and r3-P04 are published as redacted copies named `prespec.md`
  (a header, then the JSON body). Each of those READMEs states when the file was last written and when its hash was
  recorded. r3-P01's prespecification is its tolerance-arm record, published as
  `tolerance-arm-record.md`.
- `public/`: data CSVs with a commented header, readings tables, and each figure as PNG (200 dpi, no text chunks) and
  PDF (no document information).
- Not published: each entry's `code/` and `private/` folders (per-creator values under anonymous indices, and code that
  carries local paths) and any copy made before a change.

**Hashes.** Each `status.json` keeps the SHA-256 that the entry recorded for its private files and adds a section
`published_copy` with the SHA-256 of every published file. The two differ only for the files it lists as changed for
publication. Six private paths were replaced by neutral descriptions: in r3-P30's README, in r3-P02's `contacts.md`, and
in the prespecifications of r3-P04, r3-P20, r3-P30 and r3-P32. Those four prespecifications are published as redacted
copies, with a note at their top: the hash recorded at hashing time is kept privately. SHA-256 values printed in the
entries' READMEs and prespecification records are those of the private files as written; the SHA-256 of each published
file is in its entry's status.json (`published_copy.published_sha256`).
For publication only, three READMEs (r3-P02, r3-P04 and r3-P30) gained a paragraph on their prespecification's timing,
and the contact logs and prespecification records gained the label line and the agnosticism sentence where they lacked
them (the lead's step-1 decision 47). The other prespecifications are published unchanged. The files of this folder are
published byte for byte, line endings included (`.gitattributes`), so that every printed hash can be checked against the
file.
