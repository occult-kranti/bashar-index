Written 2026-11-26T00:22Z (UTC, from the clock) by a drafting agent working for the project lead (a model agent; not a
person). The lead moderates the panel and adopts the programme. Until the lead adopts it, every "decision" below is a
recommendation, built on the members' rankings, vetoes, attacks and concessions over four rounds. Exploratory
throughout: nothing here is a finding, a test of H1 to H10, or a statement about whether any non-human source speaks.
Revised 2026-11-26T00:56Z (UTC, from the clock) by a fixing agent (a model agent) after a review; the two files as they
stood before the fix are in `backup-before-fix/`.

**Privacy.** No creator, channel, title, id or quotation appears here. Results are cited by output id and described in
words (direction; whether an interval includes the null). The only numerals are design constants (sample sizes fixed by
design among them), panel data (Borda points, member counts, search counts), members' own cost estimates and the
publication years of prior work. Authors named are authors of published prior work, never people in the corpus. Any figure proposed here rests on 5 or more
creators and shows distributions over creators only.

**Ids.** Round 3's pooled list (the final numbering of rounds 1 to 3) is written **r3-P01** to **r3-P73**. Round 4's
novelty-round pooled list is written **r4-P01** to **r4-P33**; its proposals mostly sit inside round-3 entries, and

## 0. In brief

- **Twelve role-playing model agents, four rounds, one moderator.** Six method members, three domain and emic members,
  three denialists. No practitioner, ethics member or sociologist of religion was seated.
- **The panel's programme is a sequence, not a result.** Before anyone reads a claimed-voice difference as a property
  of speech, four checks must run on the seen creators with frozen code: the decoding grid (r3-P02), the recognizer by
  voice (r3-P01), position (r3-P04, r3-P05) and the labeller's own-offer rule (r3-P06). These four took most of the
  Borda points, together with two acts that must come first: the forecast-ledger freeze (r3-P39) and one hashed
  register of margins and readings (r3-P29).
- **One act cannot wait.** The ledger hash must be deposited, first and alone, before the largest group of pending
  windows matures at the end of this December (TL-7.9). Resolution of any forecast stays the owner's act and is
  unfunded.
- **Class counts.** Of the 106 pooled proposals (73 of round 3, 33 of round 4): 88 are no-cost work on the seen
  creators (class a), 9 register for a future wave (class b), 4 have an unfunded primary part (class c; their
  preparatory parts are no-cost and are written or hashed now; the unfunded arms of many a and b entries come besides), and 5 are set aside (class d).
  Nothing in class a runs before step 0's deposit, which needs the owner's permission (rulings 1 and 2, section 2.6).
  Of the 88, 49 need nothing more (a.1): step 0's eight ids other than r3-P35 are written now and deposited on the
  owner's act, and
  the other 41 run in the order of work once that deposit is made. 39 wait for a further ruling or a stored fact
  (a.2): 28 for the lexicon ruling (11), r3-P35 for the ruling on frozen or released files (13), and 10 for the audio,
  package, storage, reporting or provenance rulings or a stored fact. Most round-4 entries sit inside a round-3 entry
  (section 2.5), so the distinct pieces of work are fewer than the ids.
- **Novelty.** 48 claims were made, attacked and pooled into 31 surviving wordings; one claim was withdrawn and one
  awaits an attacker. What survives is designs, not findings: checks of the study's own instruments, a few field
  designs that use one feature this corpus has (one speaker who alternates, inside one recording, between their own
  voice and a voice they present as another's), and applications of named methods to this target.
- **The denialists' strongest point (O1) is shared by everyone,** in the form of the agnosticism sentence the panel
  agreed: "No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in
  these recordings and how the creators present it." (den-mat's own wording of O1 is attributed in section 4.)

### 1.5 Novelty searches and attacks (round 4)

- **The rule** (den-meth's, accepted by eleven members and followed by the twelfth): no "first", "novel" or "unique
  research question" in any output; each claim carries its search log, the paper's sentence that "not found means only
  that", and the closest prior work. Members added: name the tools, the date and the indexes not reached; mark reading
  depth (abstract, record or full text); deposit the logs with r3-P29; strike a claim when covering work is found and
  never reword it upward; write "the method is theirs; only the target is this corpus's" where the method exists.
- **Searches.** Members searched free scholarly indexes and paper search tools and named the ones they could not reach.
  The indexes reach the humanities, monographs and non-English scholarship poorly, so a "not found" is weakest exactly
  where the domain claims sit.
- **Attacks.** Each attacker asked, in order: has it been done; is it trivial (would every account predict it, or does
  the measure build it); can these data show it; otherwise it survives. den-soc added five standing attacks of its role
  (A1 to A5, section 4).
- **Coverage was uneven.** The lists given to three attackers were cut off inside one claim, so stat, den-mat and
  den-meth attacked the sixteen claims of stat, ling, css and meas; den-soc attacked the other members' claims too. So
  sixteen claims have four verdicts, 28 have one (den-soc's), and den-soc's own four have none. A second attack pass by
  a methods attacker is requested (section 2.6).
- **Withdrawal rule.** A claim is withdrawn as a novelty claim when most of its attackers refute it, or its author
  withdraws it. Duplicate claims were pooled into one wording per design, credited to every member who claimed it.

### 1.6 Limits of the process

- Members wrote in parallel inside each round, so some questions were answered in memos the asker had not read; the
  syntheses routed them.
- Every member is a model agent of one family; their agreement is agreement among agents, not a check by people.
- The members could read only public files and their own reasoning; codebook facts, stored-label facts and roster
  provenance were not visible to them and are listed as questions to the lead.

## 2. The programme

### 2.0 How the classes are used

- **(a) Do now.** No-cost: seen creators, stored fields and local computation, within one desktop's limits (at most two
  heavy jobs at a time, at most 4 workers that are passed paths, never the corpus). Exploratory. Nothing in class a
  runs before step 0's deposit, which needs the owner's permission (rulings 1 and 2 in section 2.6). Split into
  **a.1** (needs nothing beyond that: step 0's entries are written now and deposited on the owner's act; every other
  a.1 entry runs in the order of work once the deposit is made) and **a.2** (no-cost, but waits for a further ruling
  or a stored fact: the Tier 0 and Tier 1 audio ruling with the owner's archive consent, a package ruling, a storage
  question, a reporting ruling, roster provenance, the lexicon ruling (11) or the ruling on frozen or released files
  (13)). Every a-entry except r3-P35 (text only) names the plot it yields.
- **(b) Register for a future confirmation wave.** Needs a new collection, which only the owner can open. Each card
  gives the hypothesis worded as a prediction, its measure, its decision rule and what would refute it. A wave of the
  same roster tests persistence only (rv-R2); claims about creators like these need fresh creators.
- **(c) Unfunded.** Model calls (estimates at batch prices unless marked) or human coders. No money is left; any spend
  above 2 USD must be reported to the lead before it is sent.
- **(d) Set aside,** with the reason.

Many entries have parts in more than one class. Each entry's class is that of its primary part, the part that answers
the entry's own question (for r3-P22 and r4-P08 the unfunded arm; for r3-P54 stage B by people; for r3-P13, r3-P72 and
r4-P19 the registered test; for r4-P16 its audio part). Work in another class that runs before or beside it (a
specification or draw hashed now, templates, projections, a seen exploration, a filter rule) is listed beside it. The
rule is not "the part that runs first": every class-b card is hashed in step 0, so that rule would leave class b empty.
Shared rules bind every a-entry:
- **Floor rule** (stat): no reading may be looser than "the near 90% creator-interval bound lies beyond half the
  smallest effect"; reading A also needs every named control row in the same direction; estimators or references that
  disagree give "undetermined". Smallest effects are fixed in r3-P29 on substantive grounds and never lowered later.
- **Agreement, never accuracy.** Every label is model-coded and not checked by a person; only r3-P25 could give an
  accuracy word.
- **Wording.** Form (b) of the agnosticism sentence; creator-centred voice definitions; "first-person claimed voice"
  and "reported claimed voice" in public text; "creators who describe their practice as X"; never "failed" for a
  date.

**Order of work** (agreed by all twelve members, updated in round 4):
1. Step 0, before any run: the ledger hash alone (r3-P39), then one external, time-stamped deposit of r3-P29 with
   r3-P30's method card, r3-P31's split, r3-P34's cells and r4-P33's novelty logs, citing the ledger hash (both
   deposits need the owner's permission, rulings 1 and 2); r3-P35's text corrections (for frozen or released files,
   after ruling 13).
2. Step 1, instrument checks on the seen creators with frozen code, each on the base its stored data hold (the
   grid-shift and bridge rows rest on BR-GS's 40 and rv-R3's 35 seen recordings, one per creator; see r3-P01 and
   r3-P02): r3-P02, r3-P01 (families A to D and the tolerance arm), r3-P04 with r3-P05 inside its prespecification,
   r3-P06, then r3-P03 and r3-P07; r3-P33 after r3-P06 and r3-P30; r3-P32 last.
3. Step 2, after the lexicon ruling (11) and before any lexicon count: r3-P41, then r3-P42.
4. Then the descriptions that read meaning from a by-voice difference or a lexicon (steps 3 to 5 below).
5. After the audio ruling, heavy jobs one at a time with free memory checked first (a.2).
6. Design for any collection: fresh-creator rows that need no comparison stratum, then r3-P66's cells, then r3-P70.

**Spending order if money ever appears** (all unfunded now): r3-P22's arm A first (about 2 to 5 USD); then small arms
that each remove one named dependence (r3-P03's stripped relabel if stripped labels are not stored; r3-P56's precision
read; r3-P24's faithfulness arm); then r3-P25's minimal person check as the first large sum.

### 4.3 What would change the denialists' minds

- **den-meth:** for the corpus, a fresh collection in which a frozen, label-free statistic passes a preregistered test
  against a no-source comparison stratum; or three results together (a low false-positive rate of the voice instrument
  in the no-source stratum, fresh channellers' contrasts beyond the no-source and persona strata, and person-coded labels
  at the registered floor). "It would still be nothing about a source." For novelty: a design that survives an attacker
  searching the neighbouring fields and has a result beyond its own instrument's differential; the grid check (N02) is
  closest, and it is a check of the pipeline. On the seen creators (round-3 memo, section 9): if a headline contrast
  keeps reading A through every row of its artefact cascade (r3-P33) and inside the panel-wide band (r3-P29), den-meth
  concedes a description of the seen creators that position, the recognizer, register and the labeller's measured priors
  do not explain (still model-coded, not distinctive, nothing about a source). On its own attacks: it drops the
  labeller-prior reading of LAB-A if r3-P06 shows no island concentration; drops D4 if r3-P01's part E or another
  recognizer family shows no differential deletion; drops the multiplicity attack if r3-P29's panel-wide band leaves most
  readings unchanged; and drops the claim that the seen descriptions were tuned into the instruments if r3-P27 shows no
  difference between development and check creators.
- **den-mat:** per proposal, a named criterion (for example, a position-matched contrast as large as the unmatched one;
  a channelled persona outside the declared-persona range on several primaries). On the source question, no outcome of
  these designs can move den-mat, and the member says so once and attacks only wordings that slip toward granting one.
- **den-soc:** retirement rules fixed in advance: brand safety retires if the visibility gradient falls inside its band;
  the funnel retires on an odds-ratio upper bound below 0.8 with no congruence, or on a client-service share equivalent
  between the two kinds of recording (inside 0.8 to 1.25); "platform-timed" retires if sky anchors are at least as

## 5. What the domain and emic members contributed to codebooks and categories

These are readings by non-holders (historians and an emic reader of conspiracy culture), offered to inform codebooks,
categories and hypotheses about meaning. None is published as a fact about any community, and none is an endorsement.
No practitioner was seated.

**Voice codebook and public wording.**
- Creator-centred definitions: the creator speaks in the first person as a named non-human source, or reports what a
  source is said to say; public names "first-person claimed voice" and "reported claimed voice" ("embodied" is an
  insider term). "Presented as", never shortened to "authored by": under full trance a creator may deny being a
  conscious author, and filter doctrines claim part of the authorship.
- The agnosticism sentence in form (b), with emic's amendment, so that "produce" cannot be read as "compose". An
  earlier form set "contact" against "performance", which emic read as a false binary: filter doctrines, higher-self and
  blended channelling and psychological accounts are neither.
- Wording rules: "claimed extraterrestrial source"; the historians' name for the 1950s movement only as a historical
  name, never for creators; self-claims stated as self-claims; the outsiders' label for conspiracy belief avoided for
  people; the scholarly term for the fusion of conspiracy and spirituality marked as an etic category; "persona voice"
  withdrawn because it presupposes performance; "channelling mediums" replaced because it presupposes mediation.

**Modes of mediation** (r3-P53, N30). Classes from the tradition's own historical categories: full trance (self-
described), conscious or blended, dictation, relay, higher self or Source, received text read aloud, recitation of
another receiver's message. A term that names trance in Afro-Brazilian religions is not used as a general label; an
identity claim of a replaced soul leaves the mode list; wordless vocalisation is named "vocalisation without
recognisable words, self-described as light language". The passage into a claimed voice is described in the record as
taught and developed, so "learned" is common ground with the materialist account; occult predicts a ramp at onset and a
step at offset, as a hypothesis to test.

**Asks and offers** (r3-P05, r3-P06, r3-P22, r3-P55, r3-P56). Not asking is not standing outside commerce: in the
historical record, messages presented as a source's endorsed the medium's (historically so called) work, and appeals
were presented as dictated by a source; "the human handles the money" is the norm the record shows, not "the source
never speaks of the work" (occult, et). The insider idiom frames payment as exchange or gift, which an ask codebook may
miss; asks to share a message and invitations to a role of service are ordinary in the claimed voice, while a claimed
voice that sells directly is read, in the milieu's own discernment talk, as a sign of an unreliable channel (emic). Invitation classes: to practice, to a gathering, and a
pay-or-enrol ask only when an offer is named; synchronised practice at a stated time as a free, service-framed
tradition; a free live stream as a platform ask. These forms became arm A's formula types, so the battery tests exactly
the forms history makes likely.

**Forecasts and the ledger** (r3-P36 to r3-P40). The energy almanac (monthly or yearly readings of the time) is not
heard as world prophecy and must not be scored as a failure; hedging is done by cosmology ("certain outcome, open
timing") rather than by modal verbs; conditional prophecy is a doctrinal form, so qualifier families are named
descriptively and no escape or motive is presumed. The resolvability classes (resolvable as worded; not resolvable,
energetic predicate; not resolvable, unbounded or only time-hedged; mixed) with flags for conditional statements (met
only as stated by blind resolvers from public record) and date refusals, and a mass-observable event class, were drafted
by et and emic. A timing rule: a statement that an event has already taken place on an energetic level goes to the hedge entry if
the target was open at upload and to the retrospective entry if it had passed. Four anchor classes keep civil-calendar
numerology apart from sky and festival events.

**Dated vocabularies** (r3-P43, r3-P44, r3-P46). "Draws on vocabulary first current in period X", never descent;
"imported" never judges sincerity. Names circulate as affiliation, not descent; shared source names are rival claims,
not ties; name classes (open, franchise-like, tied to one channel, and unnamed sources as a class of their own) come from
the literature. The post-2012 layer is split, so that non-political end-time hopes are not joined with political fusion
(which would impute an alignment many in the milieu reject); institutional disclosure is a narrow layer of its own;
timeline language is older than it looks; devotional names are undated; Christian end-time, New Thought and
anti-Masonic layers were added, the last kept apart as imported; evacuation lore with ship collocates stays in the
contact layer and is never on one list with rapture lore. Credential and commerce words of healing modalities are
offers, not doctrine.

**The control register** (r3-P48 to r3-P51, N26 to N29). A term count cannot tell asserted, reported, defanged or
rejected talk, and the lightworker norm against fear-based content makes defanging and rejection common (emic). Control
talk may be coded or voiced through adversary beings that the lists left out, so E11's null may come from its lists
(emic, et, occult). The benevolent federation strand and the adversarial strand of the lore should point in opposite
directions (et). "Neutralised" splits into inner means and an outside agent, which imply opposite readings (emic). An
unanchored "they" is mostly generic; in the claimed voice a third-person "they" for humanity's leaders is distancing
deixis, not coded speech. "The source does not name names" is a recent norm of the present idiom, not a constant of
channelling (occult; emic accepts). Polarity cosmology and doctrinal history terms are read as cosmology by default, and
as a bridge or trope only by adjacency to human groups. Windows next to senses that parts of the literature document as
carriers of the antisemitic world-conspiracy trope or the hidden-bloodline trope are never counted, totalled or shown by
family in public in the lexicon entries (et's condition, which round 3 scoped to r3-P43, r3-P44 and r3-P48, carried to
r4-P18, r4-P27 and r4-P29); they go only to r3-P54's private screen after the owner's ethics act. The one exception is
r3-P54's own display after stage B: the creator-equal share of recordings with a window confirmed by people, by pooled
family, in cells of 5 or more creators, or "fewer than 5 creators".

**Grammatical person and delivery** (r3-P52, r3-P14, r3-P17). Plural "we" is ambiguous (inclusive or exclusive);
emic's reading (a shared genre style across the milieu) and occult's (a mark of particular lineages) are opposed
hypotheses, kept out of result sentences. Many guided meditations in this milieu are led in a claimed voice, so guided
practice is split by voice (occult). A received text read aloud is a respected mode; "read-like" would be heard as
"scripted", and a synthetic-narration flag as an accusation, so neither may be printed as a reading of any creator
(emic).

**Platform and economy** (r3-P57, r3-P60, r3-P61). The free message and the paid sitting predate the platform; marketing
devices have devotional twins, hence the priced-or-unpriced rule; links to alternative platforms mostly mean reach, and
the main hedge destination is a messaging-app class; "a link is a link, not a verdict".
