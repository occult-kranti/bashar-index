## What was checked, and how

- **Scope.** Every work that `verdicts.md` (this folder) names and that the programme's section 3.1 did not already name
  for the same wording: the final wordings' closest-work lists, the narrowing sources, the works named in the evidence
  and notes, and the unopened leads of section 5 (existence and metadata only). 161 rows: 159 distinct works, two of
  them judged once for each of two wordings (Cunningham 2012 for N10 and N11; Zarei et al. 2025 for N26 and N28).
  Three works already in the paper's verified bibliography were checked for the claim only (Klimo 1987, Brown 1997,
  Hua et al. 2022).
- **Questions.** For each work: does it exist; is it findable (a DOI, arXiv id or stable URL that was opened, or whose
  catalogue record was opened); does what was read say what the wording claims. Reading depth is stated per work.
- **Who and when.** Six search helpers (model agents of the paper-update agent) under one written brief, by group:
  A1 (N01 to N03), A2 (N04, N06, N07, N14), B (N10 to N13, N15, N30), C (N19 to N24, N31), D1 (N16, N17, P32),
  D2 (N25 to N29). Checks ran from 15:52 to 16:30 UTC on 2026-11-26; each row carries its own time.
- **Engines.** WebSearch and WebFetch; the Crossref, OpenAlex and arXiv APIs; Consensus; PubMed; Firecrawl's paper
  index and paper reader (Firecrawl web search was out of credits); the Internet Archive / Open Library index for books.
  No model or API call other than literature search and page fetches; no OpenAI call; no money spent; no corpus data
  read. Python ran only in the guarded venv.
- **Raw files.** The helpers' JSON and BibTeX are in a session scratch folder (not published); they can be cleared
  on reboot. Everything needed to audit the check is in the table below.

## Rule for the paper

A work goes into the paper only if it passes. PASS (full text or passages read) and PASS-ABSTRACT (the abstract or
record supports the claim; full text not read) pass. A PARTIAL passes only where the description the final wording
gives still holds and the discrepancy lies in a detail of the attacker's evidence, a year within one or an author
count; otherwise it fails as worded. FAIL-NOT-FOUND and UNNAMED fail. A wording prints only if every newly named work it
cites passes; N02 and N03 print no novelty sentence (decision 96.10). The paper rewords nothing: a wording with a
failed work is held, not narrowed.
