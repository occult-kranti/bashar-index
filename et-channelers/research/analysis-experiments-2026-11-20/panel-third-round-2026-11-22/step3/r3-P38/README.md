# r3-P38 / r4-P24: qualifier clauses at speaking time (cosmological hedges v2)

Third-round panel programme, step 3 (forecasts and time), class a.2 (ruling 11). Run on 2026-11-26 (UTC, every time from
`date -u`) by the step-3 agent for r3-P38 and r3-P40, a model agent working for the project lead (not a person). r4-P24 is
carried: its primary is r3-P38's (programme 2.5); its registered test (card b8) needs fresh creators and a secular forecast
genre and is not run here.

**Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.** Tier L: the 23 non-development seen creators of deposit 2's split, run once with no
split. The forecast statements are LAB-F's model-coded candidates in the frozen ledger (deposit 1), not checked by a
person; the qualifier families are those of the ledger's frozen rule coder (deposit 2), rule-based and not checked by a
person; cue counts, sense not resolved, precision unknown. Comparisons between instruments are agreement, never
accuracy. The families are named descriptively and no motive is printed. Conditional prophecy is also a doctrinal form:
a conditional is never presumed to be an escape.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

## Results in words

- **Forecast items.** 578 ledger statements of the 23 creators had a frozen window still open at upload (deposit 4's
  placement: 568 closed windows ending on or after the upload day, 4 of the contradictory lane, 6 open-ended). 577 were
  matched to length-matched non-forecast sentences of the same recording (545 within a length ratio of 1.25, 32 by the 5
  nearest; one had no candidate with its number of units). They come from all but fewer than 5 of the 23 creators.
  19 statements had passed at upload and went to r3-P40; 86 dated statements without a findable window and 2,134
  undated ones are unclassed.
- **No family reads A** (an excess beyond 1.25 with the control rows on the same side). Excess ratio, forecast over
  length-matched non-forecast sentences (creator mean of log((F + 0.5) / (E + 0.5)), 90% t interval):

  | time qualifiers | 1.22 (0.93 to 1.61) | undetermined | undetermined (term-dependent): leaving out a single form moves the estimate across 1.25 (3 forms do so) |
  | probability and open-future wording | 1.48 (1.11 to 1.97) | undetermined (the interval crosses 1.25) | undetermined (term-dependent): leaving out one form moves the estimate across 1.25 |
  | collective-choice conditionals | not computed | | too few terms or creators (2 coder forms used by 5 or more creators) |
  | certainty and completion wording | 1.00 (0.79 to 1.26) | undetermined | undetermined |
  | energetic predicates | 1.02 (0.84 to 1.22) | B (inside the margin) | undetermined (term-dependent): leaving out any one of 4 forms changes the reading |
  | prevention and obstruction wording | not computed | | too few terms or creators (no coder form used by 5 or more creators) |

  Under emic's band of plus or minus 20% (0.80 to 1.20; card b8) every computed family reads undetermined. The control
  row NF2 (non-forecast sentences taken from everything outside the ledger's statements, finder candidates included)
  reads undetermined for every computed family; for certainty wording and energetic predicates its estimate lies on the
  other side of 1 from the primary's (1.00 and 0.95).
- **What the shares show.** Probability and open-future wording is carried by 7.1% of forecast items against a
  length-matched expectation of 1.7% (creator-balanced); time qualifiers by 2.3% against 1.3%; energetic predicates by
  21.7% against 19.7%; certainty and completion wording by 1.8% against 2.0%. The ratio column is smaller than the share
  ratios because each creator's counts get 0.5 added before the log, which pulls rare families toward 1; this is the
  prespecified estimator (r3-P30's class H).
- **S1** (decision 54 item 2): no forecast item's prevention match lies on a span of a register term flagged for trope
  adjacency; the prevention family is thin, so S1 is not printed either.
- **Quotation coding** (the ledger's frozen fields, coded on LAB-F's stored quotation): agreement with the unit coding
  ranges from 0.96 (energetic predicates) to 0.99 per computed family (creator-balanced share of items); the quotation-coded
  ratios lie lower than the unit-coded ones (for example energetic predicates 0.82, 0.67 to 1.01). Described, not read.
- **Predictions**: the deposited account-prediction cells of r3-P38 and r4-P24 are silent in every column; nothing is
  scored. Card b8's prediction (emic: denser in dated forecasts than in matched sentences and than in a secular genre) is
  for fresh creators and is not tested by this seen run.
- **Multiplicity** (deposit 2): no family-wise adjustment. The register holds 56 primaries; about 2.8 readings beyond a
  margin are expected by chance if every true effect sat at half its margin. No reading here is called a finding.
