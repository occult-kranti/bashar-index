# Third-round panel, step 3: forecasts and time

Five entry folders of the third-round programme's step 3 (forecasts and time), carrying nine programme ids. Each folder
holds its README, prespecification and hash record, contact log, status file (with the SHA-256 of every published file)
and public CSV files and figures. Shared code and the step's private records are kept privately and cited by SHA-256.

Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10. Seen creators only. Model-coded labels, not checked by a person; agreement, never accuracy.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

## The lead's decisions, in their public wording

- **r3-P38's four family rows stay printed.** Their interval method (a t interval on creator means) was fixed in the
  prespecification, not chosen by the count, and across the printed band it carries no count information.
- **Sets printed against the 23.** r3-P38's eligible creators and r4-P21's creators with a closed window lie, by
  definition, inside r4-P23's creators with ledger statements; all three are printed against the same 23, and the joint
  check of steps 3 to 5 found that no printed form narrows another.
- **r3-P40's class 3.** The class-3 rule deposited by the lead (deposit 4) yields no controls on this roster: controls
  are possible only where a creator's uploads lie 2 or more calendar years apart. This is a defect of
  the lead's deposited rule, found on application. Class 3 prints as "not computed (the deposited class-3 rule yields no
  controls on this roster)". A corrected rule would need a new deposit before any count; it goes on the fourth panel's
  agenda with a run on a larger tier.
- **Thin tier-L results** print as not computed. A count on more creators would be a new registration.
- **Tier counts.** Eligible counts print as "all 23", "all but fewer than 5 of 23" or a band leaving 5 or more values.
  Eligible counts within the exploration side or the sealed half are not printed (privacy rule); the tier letter prints.
- **Fixed 90% t intervals** in place of r3-P30's count-chosen methods are accepted: they were fixed before any outcome,
  and are flagged "not calibrated by r3-P30" where no class fits. Where the fixed t interval over-covers, it is
  conservative.
- **r3-P37's tier** stays S as run; changing it after the result is not allowed. For later entries, a date or anchor
  table written blind from public sources and hashed before any count counts as tier L.
- **Slopes** read "undetermined" until a calibrated precision-weighted slope method exists; that method and N20's
  decomposition on a larger tier go on the fourth panel's agenda.
- **r3-P37's word-anchored row** uses the frozen rule coder's event-anchor pattern, because no list was assigned for it;
  this is a stated departure.
- **Joint staging.** Steps 3, 4 and 5 were staged together, with one nesting check across the three steps and one
  independent check before publication.
- **The strict nesting standard binds** for steps 3 to 5: where one printed creator set lies inside another, each printed
  count must keep 5 or more possible values even for a reader who knows that the two sets differ. Some cells were
  therefore changed (privacy rule), and r3-P61's priced shares of classes that are not
  computed are not printed (privacy rule). No number changed.
- **Shared prespecification files** of steps 4 and 5 are cited by hash and not published; if they are ever published,
  private paths in them are redacted first.
- **A fix pass after the independent check (the lead's decision 99).** In r3-P36 (with r4-P21), r3-P37, r4-P23, r3-P38
  and r3-P40, some cells were changed (privacy rule); r3-P40's
  year-mention totals are folded; privacy notes and the note on fixed t intervals are worded in general terms; r3-P40's
  class-3 condition is restated as above. No number changed; the changed figures were redrawn by each entry's own code.
- **The nesting rule's scope (the lead's decision 103).** Where two printed creator groups nest within 1 to 4 of each
  other, no statistic of the same measure is printed on both, and the smaller group's count is never printed exactly
  beside the larger's. Banded forms ("all but fewer than 5 of N") are not exact. For the 11 voice-eligible creators the
  stricter rule of step 5 stands. Decision 103 sets the scope of the
  step-2 standing rule for steps 3 to 5.
- **A second fix pass after the second independent check (the lead's decision 103).** Under that rule, some cells were changed
  (privacy rule); counts below 5 read "fewer than 5" (0 to 4, as each file's header says); change notes are worded in general terms; settled notes to the lead are marked. No number changed;
  the changed figures were redrawn by each entry's own code.
- **A correction to step 1 (the lead's decision 100).** A count that step 1's r3-P33 printed exactly takes a band form,
  with a dated correction, in r3-P35's publication.
- **After the first fix pass (the lead's decision 101).** r3-P15's claimed-claimed rows keep their intervals: across the
  possible counts of their band, r3-P30's table chooses the same method for every design class, so the interval carries
  no count information. r4-P21's month share is not printed with its decade share (privacy rule).
- **The paper (the lead's decision 102).** The paper's programme section copies values with their source named; the
  novelty-search record is published with the paper after an identifier scan; wordings resting on works not yet
  checked are held until they pass the bibliography check; the paper goes in one pull request after steps 3 to 5.
- **A third fix pass after the third independent check (the lead's decision 104).** Rows that were not computed and
  rows that are not printed carry one label ("not computed or not printed") in their count, method and status cells
  and are listed together; no change note says why a row is not printed. Every staged count was checked against sets
  whose counts the repository's history keeps; none needed a change. File headers say "0 to 4" where they mean it.
  No number changed; the changed figures were redrawn by each entry's own code.
