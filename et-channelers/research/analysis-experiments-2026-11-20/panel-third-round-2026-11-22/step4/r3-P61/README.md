# r3-P61: persuasion and monetisation devices, with a priced-or-unpriced rule (pilot)

Third-round panel programme, step 4 (class a.2, ruling 11), a pilot, tier L. Run on 2026-11-26 (UTC, from `date -u`) by
the step-4 agent, a model agent working for the project lead (not a person). Prespecification `prespec.md` with
`../common/prespec-shared.md`, hashed 14:07:09Z (`prespec-hash-record.md`).

**Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.** Deposit 3's L14 (addendum 2b): device families (urgency, scarcity, social proof,
reciprocity, commitment, authority, discount, guarantee, unity) with commercial forms and devotional twins apart, and the
price cue that defines "priced" (a PRICE cue in the same label unit). **A pilot with no reading of its own:** without a
business-matched stratum the device counts have none; it is an instrument for the comparison set only. Cue counts: sense
not resolved, precision unknown. Full coding (15 to 30 USD) and reliability (40 to 60 coder-hours) are class c.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

## Tier and units

Tier L: the 23 non-development seen creators, once, no split. Hits: step 2's re-matching of r3-P42's extract by the shared
matcher (totals equal to r3-P42's counts). Position: tenth of running time by each label unit's midpoint over the
recording's summed unit durations (403 of the 410 recordings; the other 7 by unit index). Rate: creator-balanced per 10,000
tokens in the cell. Ladder fixed in advance: tenths if every cell has 5 or more creators with a hit, else fifths, else
halves, else not computed.

## Results in words

- **Twelve of the fifteen classes are too thin:** every urgency, scarcity, reciprocity and commitment form (commercial and
  devotional), commercial social proof, discount, guarantee and the devotional unity class read "not computed (too few
  terms or creators)" (fewer than 3 forms each used by 5 or more of the 23 creators), as r3-P42 found.
- **Three classes place in a recording:**
  - the price cue (by fifths): 2.81 per 10,000 tokens in the first fifth, then 1.07, 0.75, 1.14 and 1.11; densest at the
    opening;
  - devotional social proof (by fifths): 1.37 in the first fifth, then 0.73, 0.40, 0.70 and 0.82;
  - the credential (authority) cue (by halves): 0.56 in the first half, 0.18 in the second.
- **Priced or unpriced:** no class's priced share is printed (privacy rule), as in step 2's r3-P42 file.

No account prediction was deposited; no reading is made (a pilot).
