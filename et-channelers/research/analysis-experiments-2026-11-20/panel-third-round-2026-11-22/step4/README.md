# Third-round panel, step 4: the platform and the audience

Five entry folders of the third-round programme's step 4, carrying six programme ids. Each folder holds its README,
prespecification and hash record, contact log, status file (with the SHA-256 of every published file) and public CSV
files and figures. The step's shared rules, shared code and private records are kept privately and cited by SHA-256.

Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10. Seen creators only. Model-coded labels, not checked by a person; agreement, never accuracy.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

Creator counts print as "fewer than 5" (1 to 4), "all but fewer than 5 of N" (N-4 to N-1), "K and fewer than 5 more"
(K+1 to K+4), "withheld (5 or more of the 18)", or a band; values that could narrow a banded count are not printed.

true effect sat at half its margin.

## The lead's decisions, in their public wording

- **A change to step 2's files.** Some rows of a step-2 file are not printed (privacy rule); the change is made in step
  2's publication.
- **r3-P65's tab shares by audience band** move from class b to a.1, because the tab counts are stored; they run in a
  later batch.
- **r3-P57's congruence half** and other rows that cannot be computed on the 23 go on the fourth panel's agenda with a
  registration on a larger tier.
- **Tier counts.** Eligible counts print as "all 23", "all but fewer than 5 of 23" or a band leaving 5 or more values.
  Eligible counts within the exploration side or the sealed half are not printed (privacy rule); the tier letter prints.
- **"withheld (5 or more of the 18)"** is accepted as a floor form.
- **r3-P58's claimed-voice share with views** is cited only as "outside the permutation-maximum band but undetermined
  against the 0.10 margin (association at capture)". It is never cited as a finding.
- **Joint staging.** Steps 3, 4 and 5 were staged together, with one nesting check across the three steps and one
  independent check before publication.
- **A fix pass after the independent check (the lead's decision 99).** r3-P60's funnel-depth shares and r3-P61's
  priced shares follow step 2's published rules (some values not printed, privacy rule); r3-P58's views row is worded
  everywhere only as above; one count in r3-P65's table moves out of the band columns; notes to the lead that were
  decided are marked as settled. No number changed; the changed figures were redrawn by each entry's own code.
- **The nesting rule's scope (the lead's decision 103).** Where two printed creator groups nest within 1 to 4 of each
  other, no statistic of the same measure is printed on both, and the smaller group's count is never printed exactly
  beside the larger's. Banded forms ("all but fewer than 5 of N") are not exact. For the 11 voice-eligible creators the
  stricter rule of step 5 stands. Decision 103 sets the scope of the
  step-2 standing rule for steps 3 to 5.
- **A second fix pass after the second independent check (the lead's decision 103).** Change notes are worded in general terms,
  and settled notes to the lead are marked. No number changed.
- **A correction to step 1 (the lead's decision 100).** A count that step 1's r3-P33 printed exactly takes a band form,
  with a dated correction, in r3-P35's publication.
- **After the first fix pass (the lead's decision 101).** r3-P15's claimed-claimed rows keep their intervals: across the
  possible counts of their band, r3-P30's table chooses the same method for every design class, so the interval carries
  no count information. r4-P21's month share is not printed with its decade share (privacy rule).
- **The paper (the lead's decision 102).** The paper's programme section copies values with their source named; the
  novelty-search record is published with the paper after an identifier scan; wordings resting on works not yet
  checked are held until they pass the bibliography check; the paper goes in one pull request after steps 3 to 5.
- **A third fix pass after the third independent check (the lead's decision 104).** Rows that were not computed and
  rows that are not printed carry one label ("not computed or not printed") in their count, method and status cells
  and are listed together; no change note says why a row is not printed. Every staged count was checked against sets
  whose counts the repository's history keeps; none needed a change. File headers say "0 to 4" where they mean it.
  No number changed; the changed figures were redrawn by each entry's own code.
