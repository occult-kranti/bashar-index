Third-round panel programme, step 2 (class a.2, ruling 11), a recall probe of LAB-A, in one pass with r3-P55. Run on
2026-11-26 (UTC, from `date -u`) by the step-2 description agent, a model agent working for the project lead (not a person),
in one run with the other six step-2 description entries (shared code and run record in `../r3-P48/`; prespecification
`prespec.md`, hashed 12:46:51Z with the shared rules of `../r3-P48/prespec.md` section 0).

**Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.** The 23 non-development seen creators (tier L), run once with no split. Cue counts:
sense not resolved, precision unknown. Voice and asks labels are model-coded and not checked by a person; comparisons are
agreement, never accuracy. Offers and asks are described as the creators make them, neutrally. Absence is uninformative (a
recall probe).

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

## Results in words

- **The insider idiom** (payment worded as exchange, gift or support) is thin: no form is used by 5 or more creators. Its
  recall probe ("per voice, the share of idiom-hit units coded as asks") is "not computed (too few terms or creators)".
- **Dissemination asks** (asks to pass a message on) pass the thin rule. Units holding a dissemination phrase that the asks
  instrument codes as an ask are found for fewer than 5 creators in every voice, so the recall probe's shares are not
  printed. (The frozen asks codebook pools sharing into its platform class; step 1 could not count dissemination asks.)
- **Dissemination against pay asks per hour, claimed against own voice** (the 11 voice-eligible creators): the three
  contrasts (dissemination hits, pay-or-enrol asks (K1), and the difference of the two ratios) are not printed (privacy
  rule): a contrast prints only where each voice side is carried by 5 or more of the 11 voice-eligible creators, checked
  from the private data (the lead's step-2 decision 86), and the difference row only where both contrasts do. The
  own-voice rate of pay-or-enrol asks is 9.4 per hour; the other per-hour rates by voice rest on fewer than 5 creators
  (not printed).
- A4 with L11 (dissemination hits on tokens that also count on L11): not printed (privacy rule).

Multiplicity (deposit 2): no family-wise adjustment; 56 primaries, about 2.8 readings beyond a margin expected by chance at
half-margin effects. A reading beyond the margin is never called a finding.
