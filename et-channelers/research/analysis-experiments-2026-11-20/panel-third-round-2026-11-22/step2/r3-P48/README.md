# r3-P48 with r3-P49 / r4-P18 and r3-P50 / r4-P18: the control register v2, the concentration of control talk, and page text against speech

Third-round panel programme, step 2 (class a.2, after the lexicon ruling 11), run as one package as the members asked.
Run on 2026-11-26 (UTC, every time from `date -u`) by the step-2 description agent, a model agent working for the project
lead (not a person), after deposit 3 (merged 11:14:53Z) and r3-P42 (12:17Z). This folder also holds the shared code of the
seven step-2 description entries (`code/desc_common.py`, `code/extract_desc.py`, `code/analyse_desc.py`).

**Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.** Seen creators only: the 23 non-development seen creators (deposit 2, tier L), run
once with no split. Every count is a cue count: sense not resolved, precision unknown, until a reader is funded. Voice labels
are model-coded and not checked by a person; comparisons between instruments are agreement, never accuracy. A reading is a
description, never a finding. Control talk and adversary beings are described neutrally, as vocabulary in speech: a count
is not a belief, a stance or an endorsement.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

## Results in words

**r3-P48 (the control register v2).** Nothing can be computed. Every sub-register of L05 (adversary beings of negative or
ambivalent valence, polarity cosmology, an institution named as controlling, a technology or programme of control, an
ontological or spiritual control system, the population described as controlled) has fewer than 3 expanded forms used by 5
or more of the 23 creators: "not computed (too few terms or creators)". CR-A (a hidden controlling group) prints "not computed
until r3-P54 (waits for the owner's ethics act)". The statuses equal r3-P42's.

**The deposited predictions** (`r3-P48-predictions.md`): et's A-window estimand (risk ratio, smallest effect 1.5), its
negative control and its rule for telling fusion from no difference all need the valence-A family, which is set aside: each
prints "not computed until r3-P54 (waits for the owner's ethics act)". F windows are not described alone (no residual after
the fact). emic's precision ranges are design inputs of C4 and are applied where a set is computed (r3-P49, r3-P50). meas's
scorecard rows: every sub-register row is "not computed (too few terms or creators)"; no row carries a mark. **No
prediction is scored.**

**r3-P49 / r4-P18 (concentration).** The control set pools L05's 21 counted terms after every set-aside, CR-A left out
(4 of its forms are used by 5 or more creators, so the pooled set passes the thin rule; no sub-register does). Its
concentration over creators, phi = 3.25, lies above the band of 200 matched placebo sets (0.10 to 1.13): **"excess over
matched placebos"**. It holds without any one term (C2), at every precision value including emic's ranges (C4), without
the forms the ledger's rule coder shares (A1: 3.18, band 0.16 to 1.07) and without the hits that also count on L01 (A4:
2.55, band 0.03 to 0.81). In the binned Lorenz curve, the lowest three quarters of creators by rate carry about a tenth of
the summed rates, against about 0.28 to 0.49 for matched placebo sets and more under a homogeneous-Poisson null. This is a
description of cue counts: control vocabulary sits with a minority of these creators more than matched vocabulary of the
same frequency and spread does. It cannot be split by sub-register. Under r3-P42's convention (a list's whole is the union of
its computed classes) the control set is **not computed**; it prints beside the pooled set as a sensitivity row (decision 69). The shift set (L06) shows no
excess over matched placebos (phi 0.51, band 0.03 to 0.80) and reads "undetermined (term-dependent)": "ascension" carries
more than half of its count (r3-P42 found the same).

**r3-P50 / r4-P18 (page against speech; placement, never routing).** Placebo-adjusted log ratio of page rate over speech
rate: shift vocabulary 3.28 (band 1.79 to 4.89), "page denser than speech" on the band, holding without coder-shared forms
and without L01-shared hits, but **"undetermined (term-dependent)"** because one term ("ascension") carries more than half of
the speech count. Control vocabulary 1.40 (band 0.08 to 3.12): undetermined, and "undetermined at unknown precision" (the
0.3 weights change it). Dissemination asks (L12 DISSEM): the page side rests on fewer than 5 creators ("not printed"); the
idiom class and both suppression halves of L13 are thin; L13's link classes are page-only ("not applicable"). By third of
control-talk rate, shift vocabulary reads the same way in the middle and highest thirds (term-dependent); every other cell
rests on fewer than 5 creators or a thin class. The third direction registered by N17 (page denser than speech) is what
the shift row shows on its band, but it is term-dependent.

**The crossed outcome table** (`data-r3-P48-outcome-table.csv`) puts the two entries' readings side by side, as hashed.

Multiplicity (deposit 2): no family-wise adjustment; with 56 primaries about 2.8 readings beyond a margin would be expected
by chance if every true effect sat at half its margin. These rows use placebo bands (fixed methods), not r3-P30 intervals.

## Privacy

Every printed value rests on 5 or more creators who carry it; otherwise the cell says so. No per-creator row or point: the
Lorenz curves are binned by rule into bins of 5 or more creators and printed as cumulative shares of the 23. Terms are named
only from the deposited lists and only when 5 or more creators use them. Creator counts printed: the 23 (deposit 2's set);
the thirds of control-talk rate print as a band ("5 to 9"), because a count near a published count is banded. Bands are fixed
methods (matched placebos, a Poisson null); no interval here is chosen by the creator count. Checks run: the identifier scan
(0 hits in CSV, PNG, PDF and the prespecification), PNG without text chunks, PDF without document information; zsolve,
qscan_all2 and whole_position_scan over the seven entries' public files (0 rows tested or pinned; the Lorenz rows trace to
the printed base of 23); the s1_* solvers (they read step 1's files; unchanged results; no step-2 row has a banded count with
a count-chosen interval, so decision 62 removes nothing); `code/nest_check.py` over every banded count of the seven entries
against step 1's stored-membership register (0 left with 4 or fewer values).

## Departures and readings of the prespecification

1. **Checks added after the first analysis run (12:59).** The prespecification (0.3) requires C2, C4, A1 and A4 on every
   computed reading. The first run carried them for r3-P49 but not for r3-P50's rows (and r3-P56's). They were added at
   13:02 to 13:04 (with `code/extract_page_shared.py`, which reads page text again to find page hits that overlap L01's
   tokens, for A4), after the first-run base readings had been seen. No rule was changed; the readings changed only by the
   prespecified checks (the shift row became term-dependent, the control row precision-dependent).
2. **Placebo term order.** The re-matched placebo draws (A1, A4, link-bound) followed Python's set order, which varies
   between runs; from 13:07 the terms are sorted, so the draws are reproducible. Readings were the same in either order.
3. **Page text.** Web and e-mail addresses are removed from descriptions before matching (run record, before the analysis).
   Titles' word-class placebo matching was not built separately; the link-bound row applies only where L12 is printed (it
   is not).
4. **Lorenz bins** (5, 6, 6 and 6 creators, fixed by rule) are printed as cumulative shares of the 23 creators. No
   statistic is printed for a difference between two groupings of the creators printed in this entry (privacy rule).
5. **Thirds of control-talk rate.** The prespecification fixes their sizes by rule; the CSV prints each third's count as a
   band under the count rule. The prespecification is published as written (decision 69), as a redacted copy with one
   private path replaced (decision 70).
6. The count register stores the membership of every printed set (private), so that `code/nest_check.py` can test them.
