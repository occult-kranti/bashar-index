Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10. Seen creators only (tier L: the 23 non-development seen creators, deposit 2's split policy). Model-coded labels, not checked by a person; agreement, never accuracy.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

**Multiplicity** (deposit 2). No family-wise adjustment is applied to exploratory readings. The programme has 56
primaries; if every true effect sat at half its margin, about 2.8 readings beyond a margin would be expected by chance at
the deposited one-sided 5%. A reading is a description, never a finding.

**Reading words.** Rows read against matched placebos, pseudo-strata or a null use the band's words ("no excess over
matched placebos", "outside the placebo band", "no stratigraphic structure beyond placebos"). Where no direction was
deposited, a margin row reads "beyond the margin (...)", "within the margin (B)" or "undetermined", never A. No reading A
occurs in step 2. "Term-dependent", "undetermined at unknown precision" and "not separable from the ledger's coded
families" are the readings of the checks C2, C4 and A1 (r3-P42's README).

**Privacy.** Every printed value rests on 5 or more creators who carry it; otherwise the cell says "not printed". No row
or point is per creator, and no quantile of creator values is printed. Terms are named only from the deposited lists and
only where 5 or more creators use them. Counts near printed counts are banded or not printed. No interval on a row whose
creator count is banded was chosen by that count (step-1 decision 62); placebo, permutation and null bands are fixed
methods. A contrast between voice sides prints only where each side is carried by 5 or more of the voice-eligible
creators (checked from the private data; decision 86); terms used by fewer than 5 creators are pooled only where together
5 or more creators use them; a share that is the sum of printed and withheld shares prints only where every part of it
prints.
