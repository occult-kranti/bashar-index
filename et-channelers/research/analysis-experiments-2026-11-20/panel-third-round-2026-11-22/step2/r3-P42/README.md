Run on 2026-11-26 (UTC, every time from `date -u`) by the r3-P42 agent, a model agent working for the project lead (not a
person), after deposit 3 was merged to main at 2026-11-26T11:14:53Z.

**Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.** Seen creators only: the 23 non-development seen creators of the split (deposit 2,

accuracy. A reading is a description, never a finding.

## Privacy

Every printed value rests on 5 or more creators who carry it; otherwise the cell says "not printed". Terms are named only
from the deposited lists and only when 5 or more creators use them. The only creator counts printed are the 23
non-development creators (deposit 2's count, the same set) and the 11 voice-eligible creators. For that set, its nesting with
other printed groups was checked; any pair 1 to 4 apart carries no statistic on the smaller group. Printed counts that carry no
membership cannot be checked for nesting without a contact; none is near it by set. Counts near printed counts are not
printed. Bands come from fixed methods only (placebo, pseudo-strata, permutation and curveball bands, and the prespecified
creator bootstrap), never from a method chosen by the creator count (step-1 decision 62). No group means, no quantiles of
creator values, no per-creator row. Group sizes of the profile contrasts are not printed. Zero and 1 to 4 carriers read
alike ("not printed"). Under the lead's step-2 decision 86 (after the independent privacy check): a voice contrast, and
every value read from one, prints only where each voice side is carried by 5 or more of the 11 voice-eligible creators
(checked from the private data); terms used by fewer than 5 creators are pooled only where together 5 or more creators
use them; a funnel depth prints only where every link class at that depth prints; the survival strip's rank correlation,
L14's priced shares and the pseudo-strata band endpoints of L10 and L14 are not printed. No number changed.

Checks run before finishing: the identifier scan (0 hits over CSV, PNG and PDF); no band phrase and no creator count other
than 23 and 11 in any CSV; PDFs carry no document information. The eight `s1_*` solvers, `zsolve.py`, `qscan_all2.py` and
the four new solvers were run (see status.json). No row of this entry prints a banded or withheld creator count, so no
solver route applies: 0 rows tested, 0 narrowed.
