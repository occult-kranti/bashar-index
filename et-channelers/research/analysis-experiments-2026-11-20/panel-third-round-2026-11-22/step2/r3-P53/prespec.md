## 1. Modes and groups

- L10's classes: MODE-TRANCE, MODE-CONSCIOUS, MODE-DICTATION, MODE-RELAY, MODE-HIGHERSELF, MODE-READALOUD,
  MODE-RECITATION, MODE-LIGHTLANG ("vocalisation without recognisable words, self-described as light language"). Status
  by the thin rule.
- **Mode group X** (multi-label): creators with 2 or more cue hits of X in their speech (any voice side). Sensitivity: 1
  or more hit. A group of fewer than 5 creators prints "fewer than 5 creators".

## 2. Statistics (computed modes only)

- **Creators by self-described mode**: the count of each group (shared 0.4's count rule) and its share of the 23.
- **LAB-V's claimed share per mode group**: per creator, claimed-voice tokens over all tokens of the creator's recordings
  inside the primary voice definition; the creator-balanced value in group X and in the other creators with such
  recordings, and their difference, with r3-P30's class B interval (a between-group difference of creator means; class B
  is the nearest class, named as such). Group means only where both group counts print exactly.
- **Where the voice code files the cues (r4-P30)**: for each computed mode, the creator-balanced share of its cue hits in
  claimed-voice units, own-voice units and units with no voice side, over creators with 1 or more hit in recordings inside
  the definition (5 or more needed). A description: no correct filing is defined, so no misfiling rate is read; the
  programme's concern (blended or conscious channelling, dictation and received texts read aloud may be misfiled) is
  printed beside the shares of those modes, which are thin.
- The shift by mode (r3-P16) is not computed here.
