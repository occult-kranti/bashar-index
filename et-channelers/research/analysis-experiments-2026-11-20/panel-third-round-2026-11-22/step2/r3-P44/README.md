## Results in words

**Primary (all 23 creators).** Of the 122 pairs of the binding pair list, 15 layer pairs are computed: every pair of the
six computed layers S02 (Spiritualist), S03 (Theosophical), S04 (New Thought), S06 (contact and federation), S13
(timeline language) and S14 (devotional names). Their co-use lies between -1.5 and 1.9 null standard deviations of the
curveball null blocked by audience band; **none lies outside both the fixed-margin null and the placebo band**, so no
pair shows co-use structure beyond chance. One pair (S03 with S06) lies below its placebo band but inside the null. 77
pairs are "not computed (too few terms or creators)" and 30 are "not computed until r3-P54 (waits for the owner's ethics
act)".

**By stratum** (den-soc's audience-capture reading): with the null drawn within each stratum (audience under 10,000;
10,000 or more; client offers; no client offers), **no pair lies outside both bands in any stratum**. Stratum rows print
their readings only (a privacy rule; no value or reading changes).

**r4-P27's fusion test, the decisive pair P000** (federation strand against adversarial strand): "not computed until
r3-P54 (waits for the owner's ethics act)": the adversarial side loses its trope-adjacent terms under decision 51 L1,
and no residual is built. **The registered predictions** (et: at or below chance; occult and emic: at or above chance in
the 2020s) **are not scored.** The conspirituality pair P001 (L05 against L06) is "not computed (too few terms or
creators)": every L05 class is thin on 23 creators.
