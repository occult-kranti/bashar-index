**Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10.** Every count is a cue count: sense not resolved, precision unknown. Labels named
here are model-coded and not checked by a person; comparisons between instruments are agreement, never accuracy. A
reading is a description, never a finding.

> recordings and how the creators present it.

## How the 48 were selected, and what was not read

The published chains read deposit 2's split draw only to select the 23 non-development creators (and to copy their
audience band and half). The rerun takes the 48 from the registry's development list instead, recomputes the audience
band from its definition (the median stored subscriber count), copies no half, and builds a fresh extract; the split
draw, the X+T folds and r3-P42's stored caches were never opened. No by-voice row, no row
reading ask, forecast or TIME-role labels and no X+T row was computed; every token was tallied on the all-speech row.

## Privacy

No cell rests on fewer than 5 creators; no per-creator row or point; creator counts print as bands of their set (or
"all 48"); values that could narrow a banded count are not printed; terms are named only from the deposited lists and only
where 5 or more creators use them; group sizes of strata and terciles are not printed; no fold or half value; no name,
id, title or quotation.
