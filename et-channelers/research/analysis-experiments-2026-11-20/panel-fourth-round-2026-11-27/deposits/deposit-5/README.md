The programme's order of work is instruments first, then one deposit, then the readings (decision 130.4). Deposit 5a,
already public, registered the forecast entries. Deposit 5 registers every other class-a and a.2 entry: f4-Q01 to
f4-Q06 and f4-Q08 to f4-Q13. It is hashed and published before any count, so that no primary, margin, forbidden cell,
seed or rule can be chosen after an outcome is seen.

It has three parts:
- **Part 1, the stored facts** (`stored-facts.md`): the four outcome-free checks of decision 131.7, answered from code,
  file listings and published metadata.
- **Part 1b, the lists** (`lists/`): the new blind lists, the extended collision register, the codebook-overlap census,
  the historians' date table, the matcher extension and the second author's materials, hashed in `hash-record-lists.md`.
- **Part 2, the registration** (`registration.md`): for each entry, its inputs by hash, its design and tier, **one binding
  primary** with its margin and reading words (decision 131.2 made the drafter's primaries provisional; this deposit binds
  them), its expected decidability, every account's forbidden cells (decision 130.3), its sensitivity rows, its figures and
  tables, its guards, its preconditions and what it does not compute; and the shared parts (the two matching passes, the
  seeds, the order of runs, the memory and worker rules, the updated half-effect disclosure).
