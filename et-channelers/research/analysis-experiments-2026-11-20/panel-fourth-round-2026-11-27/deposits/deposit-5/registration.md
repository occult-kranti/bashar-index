adds it merges into `main` (decision 131.9).

**Status.** Exploratory; not a test of H1-H10. Every voice, ask, forecast, time-role and title label named here is
model-coded and not checked by a person. Every comparison between labels, models, instruments or authors is agreement,
never accuracy. Every lexicon count is a cue count: sense not resolved, precision unknown. A reading A is never called a
finding.

> No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
> recordings and how the creators present it.

## 2. Rules common to every entry

### 2.1 Margins, reading words, intervals

Deposit 5a's sections 2.1 (margins and reading words) and 2.2 (intervals) apply unchanged:
- **Margins** (deposit 2): ratio outside 0.80 to 1.25 (absolute log ratio 0.223); share difference 5 percentage points;
  standardised difference 0.20 between-creator SD; coefficient (kappa, correlation) 0.10. Where a card registers its own
  margin or band, the card's holds.
- **Readings:** A (beyond the margin in the named direction under every admissible reference, with every named control row
  in the same direction); B, "accounted for" (inside the margin under every admissible reference); "beyond the margin,
  opposite to the named direction"; for a row with no named direction, "more" or "less beyond the margin" or "equal
  within the margin (accounted for)"; otherwise "undetermined". Printed labels: "not computed (too few terms or
  creators)", "not printed (privacy rule)", "undetermined (the tuning-pool guard does not pass)". Band-read rows use
  their band's words ("outside both bands", "inside both bands", "no excess over matched placebos").
- **The floor rule** binds: no reading looser than "the near 90% creator-interval bound lies beyond half the smallest
  effect". Smallest effects are never lowered to make a reading reachable.
- **Intervals:** 90%. Share differences and log ratios between creators: the t interval on creator values, fixed in
  advance, as deposit 5a and the third panel's entries used it (counts print as bands, so a count-chosen method would be
  withheld). Where a card names its own interval (crossed bootstraps, permutation bands, HKSJ), the card's holds and is
  fixed below. r3-P30 version 2's choice table prints beside each row; where version 2 would choose another method, that
  prints as a limitation and never replaces the fixed interval (f4-Q09 (ii)).

### 2.2 Tiers, worded as decisions 129.12 and 130.5 require

- **Label-free list counts** (voice-free prevalence, rates and composition): the 48 development creators are the primary
  row, worded **"a second description on a disjoint set"**, never "replication" or "confirmed". The published 23 print as
  **the published reference**. The 71 are **described only**.
- **By-voice and other label-based rows:** the larger voice-eligible set of the 71, under decision 129.6's check (section
  5) and the private guard (section 2.3), each carrying the note that the voice prompts were developed on the 48.
- **Tier X+T:** two-fold cross-fitting by creator, the folds stratified by development membership, deposit 2's halves and
  roster vintage, the fold seed in section 3.2, fold values never printed (decision 90).
- No row prints by development membership, fold or half (decision 129.3).

### 2.3 The private tuning-pool guard and its one label

Every reading that rests on labels whose prompts were tuned on the development creators (voice, ask, forecast and TIME-role
labels) is guarded: a reading beyond the margin, in either direction, also needs the same sign of the point estimate in the
development part and in the rest, computed privately from the split draw and never printed (f4-Q05b's wording). Otherwise
the row reads **"undetermined (the tuning-pool guard does not pass)"**, its one label. No row of the development part minus
the rest prints under any name (decision 129.3).

### 2.4 Account cells

Every entry gives each account's forbidden cells before any count. An account that forbids nothing prints "forbids
nothing". After the count, each account's cell in a row is marked as deposit 5a's section 2.4 sets out: **miss** (the
decided outcome lands on a forbidden tile), **hit** (it lands on an allowed tile, in a row where the account forbids a
direction), **absorbed** (the account is silent on the row or allows both directions), **not readable** (the row reads
undetermined or not computed). No account is called confirmed or refuted. Accounts not named on a card are not entered.

### 2.5 Privacy rules for every figure and table

- No cell, bin, bar, tenth, step, rung or plotted group rests on fewer than 5 creators; no per-creator row or point; a row
  whose creator values are non-zero for 1 to 4 creators is not printed (the dominance rule).
- No histogram or bin table over a banded set: a creator-balanced mean, its 90% interval and a share in the printed form
  of its count instead (decision 129.10).
- A share of creators takes the printed form of its count; values on the 48, the 23 and the 71 print together only where
  all three counts are exact; otherwise they are banded or not printed (decision 132.5).
- Counts in the project register's printed forms (`pubrules.Register`), registered against every printed group (the 71,
  the 48, the 23, the 11, every printed nested set), so that no printed count narrows another; every new count within 1
  to 4 of a printed count takes a banded form.
- A list term or formula is named only where 5 or more creators use it (decision 129.10). No statement text, title or
  description of any creator prints.
- Every public file of a later reading passes the identifier scan, a search for private paths and the name screen
  against the people directories before it is published.

### 2.6 Wording

"Cue counts, sense not resolved, precision unknown" in every lexicon caption. Families are named by what is said, never as
a reply or a sign of anything. "Self-attributed switches"; "delivery around boundaries in published edits"; "a shared
claimed-voice register", never "a shared source"; "self-described awareness", never a clinical or memory claim; "the date
passed", never "failed"; "specificity of address" as the only name of that construct; "music bed present", never a mark of
an edited programme. No novelty word: "closest prior work" only (decision 129.15).
