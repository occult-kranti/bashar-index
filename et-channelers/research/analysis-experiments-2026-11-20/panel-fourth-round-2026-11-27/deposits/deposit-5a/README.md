## What is registered, and why now

Deposit 5a is the forecast part of the fourth panel's deposit 5. It goes first and alone (decision 131.9), because the
largest group of the frozen ledger's windows becomes first checkable in January 2027 (TL-7.9). Once those windows close,
the creators' later uploads and the world's events exist, and a design fixed after that could be shaped by them. Fixing
it now, by hash and in public, keeps the design blind to both.

`registration.md` registers two entries of the programme (`../../programme.md`):

- **f4-Q07, the frozen ledger.** The scorability funnel (seven steps from model-found forward statements to statements
  scorable as worded, with undated imminent statements as a named exit), the contact class and its pooling rule, the
  agreement floor at which a step is read, r4-P23's rows 0 to 4 on the larger eligible set with a new row 5 (voice), the
  calendar-unit slopes under r3-P30 version 2's precision-weighted class, and den's calendar-habit prediction, described
  only. **One primary:** row 0, the within-creator contact-minus-world-event difference in bounded share; margin 5
  percentage points; named direction negative.
- **f4-Q19, after the window.** The January 2027 group of windows, the anchor rule that says when a creator returns to a
  passed window, the placebo anchors and a second reference (conditional on the owner's act), the listing-only snapshot
  dates (subject to the owner's go-ahead), the horizon, five columns (return, framing of a return, cadence, offers,
  availability at equal age) and den's account table, with every account's forbidden cells as den reviewed and amended
  them. **One primary:** the return column, a within-creator risk difference against the placebo anchors; margin 5
  percentage points; no direction named. An outcome equal to the placebo reads "accounted for", never "unnoticed".

For each entry the file gives the frozen inputs by their published hashes, the unit, the steps or columns, the primary
and its margin, the reading words, the tier and the expected decidability, every account's forbidden cells, the
sensitivity rows, the figures and tables, the privacy guards, and what happens when the windows pass.
