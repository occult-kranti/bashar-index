## 2. Five tier-A layered research questions

Each question has a main question, sub-questions with their indicators on stored data and the figures and tables that
answer them, the entries that feed it, and the debates it speaks to. Every figure shows distributions over creators: no
cell, bin, bar, tenth or group rests on fewer than 5 creators, no point is per creator, and no histogram or bin table is
drawn over a banded set. A share of creators takes the printed form of its count, and values on the 48, the 23 and the
71 print together only where all three counts are exact; otherwise they are banded or not printed (decision 132.5). Where a design in a round-1 memo drew such a histogram, it is replaced here by the
creator-balanced mean with its 90% interval and the share of creators above zero in banded words. Figure ids (F-A1a and
so on) are used again in the cards of section 3.

Data codes: T, stored transcripts with word times and word probabilities; V, the model-coded voice labels (first-person
claimed voice, reported claimed voice, own voice, other); A, the stored per-recording audio features of the 895
processed seen recordings; M, page metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, upload dates, chapters) and the model-coded
title and kind labels; L, the frozen forecast ledger and its rule coder.

### RQ-A. Who speaks, and on whose authority?

**Main question.** When one speaker alternates within a recording between their own voice and a voice they present as
another's, how is the authorship of the presented voice marked, how is the passage between the voices marked, and where
is the authority of what is said placed?

**A1. Is the claimed voice marked as self-evident or as perceived?** (f4-Q01)
- *Indicators (T, V).* The pooled evidential-frame rate per 1,000 words in first-person claimed-voice span interiors
  (10 s inside each span end) over the same creator's own-voice rate, within creator; the same by family (perception
  and vision, merged with the perceptual-modality family; hearing and being told; sensing and receiving; reportative and
  quotative; inferential; certainty boosters); the perceiver's person (first singular, first plural as a form only,
  third, unexpressed) within each family.
- **F-A1a, family forest.** y: frame family, pooled set first. x: claimed-over-own log rate ratio with its 90% creator
  interval; the band of plus or minus 0.223 shaded; the matched-placebo band in grey; the cell that each account forbids
  outlined (Du Bois's: an excess in claimed-voice interiors; emic's: a perception-report excess in the first-person
  claimed voice).
- **F-A1b, perceiver person.** x: frame family. y: creator-balanced share of frames by perceiver person, stacked, one
  bar per voice; any segment from fewer than 5 creators merged into "other" by a rule fixed in advance.
- **T-A1a, family status.** Family; source in the reviews; terms used by 5 or more creators (a count); creators with any
  hit (a band); thin-rule status; codebook-overlap status in words (f4-Q01's census).
- **T-A1b, construction check.** The reported claimed voice is defined partly by reportative frames, so its rates print
  in a separate, labelled block and are never read.

**A2. Does the own voice assign authorship around the claimed spans?** (f4-Q01, with f4-Q06's filter family)
- *Indicators (T, V).* Own-voice attribution, disclaimer, filter ("the message passed through me") and awareness or
  memory frames in the 60 s before an entry and the 60 s after an exit, against own-voice units at matched positions
  60 s or more from any boundary (r3-P04's position matching); the share of exits that the edit cuts within 10 s of the
  last claimed unit, printed beside.
- **F-A2a, framing around spans.** x: seconds before an entry (-60 to 0) and after an exit (0 to +60), in bins merged
  until each rests on 5 or more creators by a rule fixed from boundary positions only. y: creator-balanced rate per
  1,000 own-voice words, one line per frame family, with the position-matched band; Szuchewycz's forbidden cell (a rate
  at or below the matched units) outlined.
- **F-A2b, filter frames at the boundary.** One row per frame family. x: within-creator difference, boundary windows
  minus matched own-voice windows, as the creator-balanced mean with its 90% interval; the share of creators above zero
  printed beside in banded words.

**A3. What marks the passage between the voices, in delivery and in wording?** (ling-speech's sub-question; f4-Q05b,
f4-Q01, f4-Q12)
- *Indicators (A, T, V).* At cue-free and cue-opened entries and exits (r3-P09's boundaries: seam-free, 60 s on each
  side, gated recordings, no environment change): the sustained shift (10 to 60 s after minus 60 to 10 s before) minus
  matched non-boundary edges, for long-pause share, relative F0 level and intensity variability, and articulation rate
  once f4-Q05a's deposit releases it; the early-minus-late shape (5 to 15 s against 30 to 60 s); the own-range ladder
  (D_co minus D_oo, with the own-halves negative control); in the same windows, the own-voice framing-cue rate of A2; at
  each onset, the label of the preceding unit (own voice, another participant, other material, recording start).
- **F-A3a, the boundary from two sides.** Small multiples on one time axis. Columns: entries, exits. Rows: each delivery
  feature, and one row of own-voice framing-cue rate. x: seconds from the boundary, -60 to +60 in 10-s windows. y: shift
  relative to matched edges in margin units (log units for rate, SD units fitted by fold for F0 level and intensity
  variability, share difference for long-pause share, rate per 1,000 words for the framing row), creator mean, with the
  90% permutation-maximum band shaded. Every curve rests on all gated eligible creators (12 or more under A8, or it does
  not print).
- **F-A3b, shape.** x: feature. y: early-minus-late shift in margin units, as dot-intervals, entries beside exits, with
  the band's interval drawn behind.
- **F-A3c, own-range ladder.** x: rung (D_oo; D_co; the own-halves negative control; D_co minus D_oo). y: SD units with
  90% intervals; the 0.20 SD margin drawn as a line.
- **T-A3a, readings.** Boundary kind by feature: creators (exact or banded), estimate, interval, margin, reading, the
  battery cap and A8 status applied to the row. No row by development membership, fold or half.

**A4. Where is authority placed: in the listener, the source or the creator as filter?** (f4-Q06)
