## The panel's design, in brief

- **The owner's order** (2026-11-26) asked the panel to improve on the experiments, to study occult and ET theories and
  documentaries, and to deliberate over three rounds toward more meaningful analysis, plots, tables, experiments and
  tier-A layered research questions.
- **Phases.** Phase 0, three literature reviews with real searches (occult and channelling scholarship; ET and UFO
  contact theories and their scholarship; documentaries); round 1, proposals; round 2, cross-review; round 3, convergence
  and vote; then the lead's synthesis, published after an independent check.
- **Members.** Eight model agents playing roles, not people: occult, et, doc, ling-speech, stat-meas, css-llm, emic and
  den (a denialist and sceptic attacker).
- **Limits.** Members read only the public files on main and the reviews; no model or API call on the spent budget; no
  commits by members; every time from the clock; memos stay private, and only the lead's checked programme is published.
- **Searches.** Real searches were allowed for the reviews, never with a creator's name, a channel or a title, and no
  documentary or work is ever tied to a roster creator.

## Decision 126: the occult review (07:27 UTC)

- The review holds 106 works: 4 read in full text, 63 as abstracts and 39 as catalogue records.

  Where the project's files cite those voice measures (the paper's related work, the cohort draft and the r4-P15 files),
  they say only that the readings "differed", for one story read in both states. That holds under both readings, so
  nothing is corrected. Any later citation that gives a direction for the voice measures takes it from the results and
  says that the study measured read text.

  existence was confirmed, its content could not be checked here, and it carries nothing more.

## Decision 129: rulings on the conflicts raised in round 2 (08:05 UTC)

These bind round 3 and the programme. *Why, in general:* each resolves a conflict between a proposal and a privacy rule,
a standing veto, the spent budget or a reporting rule.

1. Ruling 7's one recognizer of another family is used at word level, for the recognizer dependence of the voice
   contrast. The phone-level uses proposed for wordless vocalisation become unfunded work (class c).
2. Embedding arms need a package ruling and a download, so they are class c and the owner's act; the lexical arms stay
   no-cost.
3. No row may print, under any name, an estimate withheld under the acoustic eligibility rule (A8): no row of the
   development part minus the non-development part, and no eligibility band of the non-development part. A private
   tuning-pool guard caps a reading under one label instead.
4. The proxies of the set-aside speaker-embedding entry stay barred: no within-creator regularity of pause or
   vocalisation timing, no per-creator read-like score, and nothing pooled from them.
5. The grounds of the set-aside feedback-contingency entry stand: two sub-questions that conditioned on another
   participant's turn, or that carried that entry's construct, are dropped.
6. Every by-voice row on a larger set first passes a hashed, outcome-free check that its voice-eligible set lies 5 or more
   from the published voice-eligible set and from every printed nested set; otherwise it reads "not printed (privacy
   rule)". The standing rule, in public text: no statistic is printed on any subset of a printed group that lies within 1
   to 4 of it.
7. The label-free voice proxy is not read on the sealed half, which served as a fitting fold earlier. It is read by
   cross-fitting on the larger set, printed as "proxy-coded (local classifier on stored labels)", never as a voice label
   of record, and it never admits audio time.
8. No new rows on files already opened: persistence designs run only as operating-characteristic analyses on published
   values, or on recordings never opened. Transcribing the latest-50+ recordings not yet transcribed is the owner's act;
   a later snapshot needs a new collection.
9. No creator groups come from model-coded title labels, in any new registration.
10. No histogram or bin table over a banded set (a mean, its interval and a share in banded words instead); a list term or
    formula is named only where 5 or more creators use it.
11. One primary per entry, named in the new deposit; lexicon contrasts are read only for classes that pass the thin rule.
12. Label-free list counts: the 48 development creators are the primary row, the published 23 the reference, and the 71
    are described only; by-voice rows follow item 6. (Decision 130.5 sets the wording: "a second description on a
    disjoint set".)
13. No registered rule is loosened after its result: the rate-slope band stays 0.9 to 1.1, with the deflated-margin rule
    as the fallback.
14. A family of replies to medicalisation is dropped. *Why:* it would count creators' statements about their own mental
    health.
15. Novelty: only "closest prior work" lines, never "first", and no "not found" without a counted search.
16. Disclosure events: a blind table of official acts inside the stored upload range, hashed before any count, replaces
    the three dated reports of 2021 to 2024 alone. *Why:* those reports predate most stored uploads, and choosing events
    after a feasibility look would be a forking path.
17. The wording of how the gate sample G was drawn is settled at synthesis from the published files (see decision 131.10).
