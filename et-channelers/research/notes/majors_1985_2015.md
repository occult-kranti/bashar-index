# Major extraterrestrial channelers, c. 1985–2015 (the post-Ra, pre-YouTube generation) — with non-ET vocabulary-shapers as context

Researcher scope: ET channelers active roughly 1985–2015, plus seven non-ET context channels (dates only) and three key events. Stance: neutral documentary; every claim below is reported as a claim made by the person, their publisher, their followers, or (where noted) by critics/scholars. All URLs accessed 2026-10-23. Dates without a URL are marked "unverified".

Access notes: Wikipedia has NO article for Barbara Marciniak, Barbara Hand Clow, Laura Knight-Jadczyk or Blossom Goodchild (all 404 on 2026-10-23); "Sheldan Nidle" redirects to the "Ground Crew Project" article. WebSearch budget was exhausted near the end, so a handful of secondary dates (flagged) could not be re-verified. Firecrawl was rate-limited; most primary pages were read with WebFetch.

Important correction to the brief: Sheldan Nidle did NOT die in 2020. PAO's own site, Legacy.com and GalacticChannelings.com all give 26 August 2025 (sources in his block).
