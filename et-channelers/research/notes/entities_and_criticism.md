# ET channelers directory and timeline — entity catalogue and criticism dossier

Research notes compiled 2026-10-23 (all URLs accessed 2026-10-23). Stance: documentary/neutral. All "claims" below are claims made by the channelers or their publishers; all criticisms are attributed to named authors. Items marked **[unverified]** could not be confirmed against a fetched source in this session (the shared WebSearch budget was exhausted near the end; Firecrawl search was rate-limited; several sites block fetching). Primary sources (channelers' own sites) are used for first-appearance dates where possible; Wikipedia pages are used mainly for their underlying references, which are named inline where the page gave them.

Summary takeaway: the ET-channeling corpus has a traceable genealogy. The 1950s contactee era (Van Tassel's Ashtar 1952; Dorothy Martin's Clarion/Sananda 1954; George King's Aetherius 1954) supplied the "space brother", "command/fleet" and "evacuation" templates; the 1963–1985 non-ET channels (Seth, Abraham) supplied the "you create your own reality" idiom; the 1974–1984 L/L Research material (Hatonn, Latwii, Ra) supplied the density vocabulary, "harvest", "Wanderers", "Confederation" and "Orion group" that later channels (Cassiopaeans 1994, Nidle 1994–96, Bashar, Scranton, Danaan) recycled; and since 2015 the field has merged with "secret space program" and QAnon-adjacent narratives (Goode, Danaan, Love Has Won). Documented failed dated predictions run from 1952 (H-bomb) and 1954 (Clarion flood) through 1994 (Cole), 1996–97 (Nidle), 2003 (ZetaTalk), 2008 (Goodchild), 2011–2012 (Ra "harvest"/ascension) to open 2026–2027 windows (Bashar). Peer-reviewed work on channelers (Wahbeh et al. 2018–2023) finds channelers are psychologically similar to population norms and does not test or establish that channeled content is accurate.

## Entity: Ra (the Ra Material / Law of One)
- **Type:** collective ("social memory complex").
- **Claimed origin:** evolved on Venus; member of the "Confederation of Planets in the Service of the Infinite Creator"; said to have visited Egypt ~11,000 years ago (Egypt claim per L/L Research/Schiffer, see below).
- **Claimed density/dimension:** sixth density — "I am sixth density with a strong seeking towards seventh density" (Ra, session 14.19) — https://www.lawofone.info/c/Ra?su=Ra%E2%80%99s+History ; Venus origin at 41.26 (same URL).
- **Description claimed:** non-physical; speaks only through a trance instrument in "narrow-band" transmission (L/L Research's description) — https://www.llresearch.org/channeling
- **First appearance:** 1981-01-15, through Carla L. Rueckert (instrument), Don Elkins (questioner), Jim McCarty (scribe), Louisville, Kentucky; 106 sessions to 1984-03-15 — https://www.llresearch.org/channeling . (A Schiffer Books anniversary post gives "January 15, 1980", which conflicts with L/L Research's own date and with McCarty's statement that he joined in December 1980 and contact began "three weeks later" — https://schifferbooks.com/blogs/redfeather/the-ra-material-s-40th-anniversary .) Rueckert said she received Ra in 1981 after asking "for the highest and best contact" — https://www.northatlanticbooks.com/blog/wisdom-how-carla-rueckert-found-channeling-and-found-the-truth/
- **Later channelers:** none official; L/L Research states only the 106 Ra sessions used trance and that no later Ra channel is recognised — https://www.llresearch.org/channeling . RationalWiki notes David Wilcock "once claimed to have channeled Ra in 1998" and that L/L Research distances itself from him — https://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Ra_Material
- **Distinguishing claims:** seven-density octave; "harvest" (graduation to fourth density) — Ra: "This sphere is at this time in fourth-dimension vibration" (6.16); the disharmony "shall continue unabated for a period of approximately … thirty [30], of your years" (6.17); asked if harvest occurs in 2011, "This is an approximation" (17.29); elsewhere a transition of "between 100 and 700 of your years" (40.8) — https://www.lawofone.info/c/Harvest ; "Wanderers" (~65 million higher-density souls incarnated on Earth) and the negative "Orion group" "as an empire" (session 7) — https://www.lawofone.info/s/7 ; https://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Ra_Material
- **Change over time:** Elkins died in 1984 (RationalWiki: by suicide) and the trance contact ended; Rueckert continued conscious channeling (Hatonn, Latwii, then Q'uo) — https://www.northatlanticbooks.com/blog/wisdom-how-carla-rueckert-found-channeling-and-found-the-truth/ ; https://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Ra_Material . The 2011–2012 "harvest" expectation passed without the predicted shift (RationalWiki: "It didn't") — https://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Ra_Material
- **Sources:** llresearch.org; lawofone.info; northatlanticbooks.com; schifferbooks.com; rationalwiki.org (all above).

### Psychological and academic explanations
- **Cognitive dissonance (Festinger, Riecken & Schachter, *When Prophecy Fails*, 1956):** covert observation of Dorothy Martin's Seekers; after the 1954-12-21 flood failed, the group "briefly increased its public outreach" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_Martin_(spiritualist)
- **Fantasy-prone personality (Wilson & Barber 1983) and glossolalia:** Joe Nickell (Skeptical Inquirer, 2013-10-07) applies both to "extraterrestrial communicator" Cassandra Vanzant's "ET language", comparing it to Hélène Smith's 1894 "Martian" that "proved syntactically to be like French"; he finds the ET texts "indistinguishable from her own New Age speech" — https://skepticalinquirer.org/newsletter/messages-from-star-familiesin-the-et-language/
- **Cargo-cult analogy:** Andreas Grünschloß (Marburg Journal of Religion 3(2), 1998) describes "cargoistic hopes" in UFO religions; Wikipedia's Ashtar and Ground Crew articles summarise his view that much Ashtar channeling is "akin to cargo cults" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_Crew_Project (the Marburg PDF itself could not be fetched: https://archiv.ub.uni-marburg.de/ep/0004/article/view/3771/3587 ).
- **Ethnography — Michael F. Brown, *The Channeling Zone: American Spirituality in an Anxious Age* (Harvard UP, 1997):** ~4 years of participant-observation in New Mexico, Arizona and upstate New York; argues channeling has roots in American spiritualism and reflects individualism, self-improvement and commerce; "Neither a debunker nor an advocate" — https://sites.williams.edu/mbrown/the-channeling-zone-1997/
- **History of religions — Wouter Hanegraaff, *New Age Religion and Western Culture* (Brill 1996; SUNY 1998):** treats channeling as a primary source of New Age religion and notes Seth's influence on later authors — https://wouterjhanegraaff.net/newagereligion ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Material .   - Wahbeh, Carpenter & Radin (2018), *Journal of the Society for Psychical Research* 82(3): five full-trance channelers, Mt. Shasta, 2017-03-19/22; personality, dissociation and psychotic-symptom scores "similar to general population norms"; a room random-number generator showed a "modestly significant" difference (z = 2.250, p = 0.024) described as exploratory; no test of information accuracy — https://noetic.org/publication/a-mixed-methods-phenomenological-and-exploratory-study-of-channeling/

### How to read this directory
Each entry records what a channel or contactee claimed, when the claim first appeared in print or recording, and who repeated or revised it; it does not assert that any entity exists. Scholars who have studied these movements describe them as a modern religious genre with recognisable mechanics: Festinger and colleagues documented how a group responds when a dated prophecy fails (1956) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_Martin_(spiritualist) ; Helland and Melton describe the "spiritualisation" of failed landings into inner work — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ground_Crew_Project ; Brown approached channelers as "Neither a debunker nor an advocate" and tied the practice to American individualism and commerce — https://sites.williams.edu/mbrown/the-channeling-zone-1997/ ; the IONS studies find channelers are psychologically similar to population norms but do not test whether their content is accurate — https://psi-encyclopedia.spr.ac.uk/articles/helane-wahbeh/ . Readers can use the dated-prediction table to compare stated dates with outcomes, note where entities' origins, densities or descriptions changed between channels (Ashtar, Hatonn, Pleiadians, "Galactic Federation"), and treat vocabulary such as "density", "harvest" and "5D" as terms of art that originated in specific 1970s–80s texts rather than as measurements. L/L Research's own advice is to accept only what "resonates" — https://rationalwiki.org/wiki/Ra_Material — which critics regard as unfalsifiable and adherents regard as discernment; this directory records both positions.
