# The Ra Material (Law of One) as timeline anchor, the pre-1981 prelude, and the academic framing of ET channeling

Researcher notes. All sources accessed 2026-10-23. Stance: neutral/documentary; every statement about entities, origins, densities, etc. is a *claim made by the channel or group*, not a finding. "Confidence" refers to how well the *date/attribution* is sourced, not to the truth of the claim. Items marked **unverified** could not be confirmed from a fetched source in this session.

Format note for the compiler: blocks use the required headings `## Person:`, `## Entity:`, `## Timeline events`, `## Framing`. A final `## Gaps and unverified items` section lists what could not be sourced.

## Takeaway (read first)

- The Ra contact is unusually well documented from primary sources: 106 trance sessions, first on **1981-01-15**, last on **1984-03-15**, with Carla L. Rueckert as "instrument," Don Elkins as questioner and Jim McCarty as scribe/"battery" (roles per L/L Research; see Person blocks). Session-by-session dates are published at https://www.lawofone.info/sessions.php and https://www.llresearch.org/channeling/ra-contact.
- The contact's prehistory is also primary-sourced: Elkins' research began in **1955**; a Detroit contactee group's "Brown Notebook" reached him in **1961/1962**; a channeling experiment with ~12 physics students began in **1962**; Carla began channeling in **1974**; *Secrets of the UFO* was written in 1976 and **first printed May 1977**; L/L Research was formed in **1970**.
- Don Elkins died **1984-11-07** (L/L epilogue; third-party sources describe a self-inflicted gunshot); Carla died **2015-04-01**; L/L continues conscious channeling of "Q'uo" (first archived session **1986-01-12**) with Jim McCarty, Gary Bean, Austin Bridges, Trisha Bean and others, and had sessions dated 2026 on its site.
- The pre-1981 prelude divides into (a) 1950s "Space Brothers" contactees (Van Tassel/Ashtar 1952, Bethurum 1952, Angelucci 1952, Williamson 1952, King/Aetherius 1954–55, Klarer 1954–56), (b) non-ET trance channels that built the vocabulary of 1970s–80s channeling (Roberts/Seth 1963, Rodegast/Emmanuel 1969, Pursel/Lazaris 1974, Knight/Ramtha 1977), and (c) Billy Meier (1975), a physical-contact "contactee" rather than a trance channel.
- Scholars (Brown 1997, Hanegraaff 1996, Klimo 1987, Partridge 2003, Bader 2007, Palmer 2004) treat channeling as a modern descendant of Spiritualist mediumship and UFO religion as a post-1947 variant of theosophical esotericism; skeptical/psychological literature invokes dissociation, absorption, cold reading, fantasy-proneness and cryptomnesia.

## Framing

Short, quotable definitions (≤30 words) and positions, each with URL. Quotes are verbatim from the cited page; where a scholar is quoted second-hand, the intermediary is named.

### Definitions of channeling
- **Jon Klimo (1987), *Channeling*:** channeling is "the communication of information to or through a physically embodied human being" from a source said to exist on another level of reality (Klimo 1987: 2, quoted in Petryk 2016 and Miller & Wahbeh 2018). https://ojs.zrc-sazu.si/jz/article/download/6983/6572 ; https://noetic.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/WahbehMillerTermspaper1_2020.pdf . Klimo distinguishes mental, automatism, full-trance and sleep-and-dream channeling, and dates the grassroots spread of channeling to "since 1986" (2nd ed. 1998: xix). Book record: https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/928317.Channeling (2nd ed. North Atlantic Books 1998: https://northatlanticbooks.com/?p=45508 ).
- **Wouter Hanegraaff (1996/1998), *New Age Religion and Western Culture*:** channelers receive "information from sources other than their normal selves" (p. 23); channeled texts are "articulated revelations" (p. 26); channeling includes trance-less "inner dictation" (p. 24) (as quoted in Petryk 2016). https://ojs.zrc-sazu.si/jz/article/download/6983/6572 . Hanegraaff holds that Seth's ideas influenced later New Age authors who adopted Roberts's terminology. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Material . Book record: https://humanitieshub.sdsu.edu/omeka/items/show/660
- **Michael F. Brown (1997), *The Channeling Zone* (Harvard UP):** channeling is "the practice of serving as a vessel for the voices of ancient or otherworldly beings," whose practitioners say they are possessed by angels, aliens and "ascended masters"; the quest "finds its roots in traditional American notions of individualism and self-perfection." Fieldwork: four years, ~40 channelers, New Mexico/Arizona/upstate New York. Reviews: Zaleski (NYT) — "a largely artless, anti-analytical phenomenon"; Hermes (Utne) — "a positive tradition of renegade spirituality that dates back to the birth of the nation." https://sites.williams.edu/mbrown/the-channeling-zone-1997/ ; review record: https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/church-history/article/channeling-zone-american-spirituality-in-an-anxious-age-by-michael-f-brown-cambridge-mass-harvard-university-press-1997-xiii-236-pp-np/F8BDDD4AA65976734DFE9D5C0F347FA1
- **Arthur Hastings (1991):** messages from "a personality source outside the conscious mind" (quoted in Petryk 2016). https://ojs.zrc-sazu.si/jz/article/download/6983/6572
- **Wikipedia, "Mediumship" (Channeling section):** channeling is the modern New Age form of mediumship in which messages from a teaching spirit, Ascended Master, God or angel pass through the channel's waking consciousness or "Higher Self." Lead: mediums "allegedly mediate communication between spirits of the dead and living human beings." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediumship ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_channelers
- **Encyclopedia.com, "Mediums and Channelers" (Gale):** "channeling" became a more popular name for mediumship in the 1970s; channelers were imagined as human "television channels," replacing the 1930s radio-receiver image. https://www.encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/mediums-and-channelers
- **Miller & Wahbeh (IONS preprint, Oct 2018):** propose "channeling" as the umbrella term with mediumship (communication with the deceased) as a subcategory; note Edgar Cayce first used "channel" for his work; term chronology: "medium" 1861, "mediumship" 1919, "channeling" 1937, "trance channeling" 1989. https://noetic.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/WahbehMillerTermspaper1_2020.pdf
- **Carla Rueckert's own definition (1987 *A Channeling Handbook*, cited by Miller & Wahbeh):** words or sounds expressing concepts that come from outside one's own conscious mind. https://noetic.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/WahbehMillerTermspaper1_2020.pdf
- **Don Elkins (1984):** "Our research group uses what I prefer to call 'tuned trance telepathy'." https://www.lawofone.info/Intro

### UFO religion / contactee framing
- **Christopher Partridge (ed., 2003), *UFO Religions* (Routledge):** presents "UFO religion as a distinct variant of new spiritualities"; places UFO religion within theosophical esotericism that became "UFO religion" after 1947; notes pre-1947 contactees did not involve UFOs; disputes Melton's claim that "I AM" Activity was the first UFO religion. https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/4101 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_religion ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contactee ; publisher page https://www.routledge.com/UFO-Religions/Partridge/p/book/9780415263238
- **Wikipedia, "UFO religion":** "any religion in which the existence of extraterrestrial (ET) entities and communication with them is a core belief"; Stephen Hunt calls the Aetherius Society "probably the first and certainly the most enduring UFO cult"; "The 1947 Roswell incident was a key point in time within UFO spirituality"; beliefs are millenarian. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_religion
- **J. Allen Hynek (via Wikipedia "Contactee"):** contactees receive repeated messages of "cosmic importance" from "generally benign beings whose ostensible purpose is to communicate." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contactee
- **Christopher Bader (2007):** "Contactees tend to view alien encounters positively and often imbue them with themes of the sacred" (vs. abductees' involuntary, negative accounts). https://digitalcommons.chapman.edu/sociology_books/1
- **Susan J. Palmer (2004), *Aliens Adored*:** Raëlian belief is a response to secularization; reviewer John von Heyking: "a cult of technology that, despite its weirdness, clearly expresses the aspirations of our technological society." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliens_Adored ; publisher https://www.ubcpress.ca/aliens-adored
- **Wikipedia, "Space Brothers (myth)":** "Space Brothers is a term used for extraterrestrials in several narratives involving alien contact"; "physically handsome, spiritually evolved beings who have moved beyond the problems of Earth people"; in popular press by 1954; supplanted by malevolent "Grays" by the late 1980s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Brothers_(myth)
- **Michael Barkun (on Adamski):** his Venusian messages "sounded suspiciously like his own earlier occult teachings." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contactee
- **Christopher Helland (on Ashtar):** belief rests on "faith in an extraterrestrial celebrity"; failed prophecies shifted emphasis from a physical fleet to a theosophical Ascended-Master figure. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran
- **Andreas Grünschloss:** compares much Ashtar channeling to cargo cults. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran
- **Mikael Rothstein / John Saliba / David Barrett / Roy Wallis (on Aetherius):** Rothstein — syncretic, mainly theosophical; King's biography is hagiography; Wallis (1974) — King a "religious virtuoso"; Barrett (2011) — membership in the thousands. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aetherius_Society ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_King_(religious_leader) ; Saliba's WRSP profile https://wrldrels.org/?p=3129
- **Catherine Albanese (on Seth):** *The Seth Material* (1970) "launched an era of nationwide awareness" of communication with other-than-human entities. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Material
- **Elkins' own comparative claim (1977):** "The same general message permeates over 90% of the millions of words received by thousands of people around the world." https://assets.llresearch.org/books/secrets_of_the_ufo.pdf

### Psychological and skeptical explanations
- **Dissociation / absorption / hypnosis:** Miller & Wahbeh summarize dissociation as a continuum from absorption to dissociative identity disorder, and the "absorption hypothesis" (focused attention with reduced self-awareness). https://noetic.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/WahbehMillerTermspaper1_2020.pdf
- **Dissociative identity disorder:** Wikipedia "Mediumship" notes trance mediumship has been linked to dissociative identity disorder. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediumship
- **Cold/hot reading and fraud:** "Cold reading also explains why psychics have consistently failed scientific tests of their powers."; "Belief in psychic ability is widespread despite the absence of empirical evidence for its existence." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediumship
- **False memories / fantasy-proneness:** Susan Clancy attributes contact claims to "false memories" from fantasy-proneness, memory distortion, culturally available scripts, sleep hallucinations and scientific illiteracy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contactee ; Thomas Streicher on Klarer: "generally poorly substantiated," possibly fantasy-prone. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Klarer
- **Cryptomnesia (contested):** Paul Cunningham (2010) on Seth: "fraud and cryptomnesia are highly improbable explanations"; James Alcock: "there seems little need to consider the involvement of any supernatural agency." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Material
- **Parsimony (Sagan on Ramtha):** "the simplest hypothesis is that Ms. Knight makes 'Ramtha' speak all by herself"; "all we are offered are banal homilies." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Z._Knight
- **Vallée:** "No serious investigator has ever been very worried by the claims of the 'contactees'." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contactee
- **David M. Jacobs:** older contactees backdated their first encounters to claim greater authenticity. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contactee
- **Randi (on Aetherius):** King's claims "denounced as pseudoscience by some skeptics." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_King_(religious_leader)
