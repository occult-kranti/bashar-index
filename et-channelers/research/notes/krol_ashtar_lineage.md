## Person: George Van Tassel

**Aliases/handles**: George W. Van Tassel; founder, Ministry of Universal Wisdom / College of Universal Wisdom; "Brotherhood of the Cosmic Christ" (1948) (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/).
**Entity channeled**: "Ashtar" — self-described in the first message as "Ashtar, commandant quadra sector, patrol station Schare, all projections, all waves" (https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/ashtar); "I am Ashtar, commandant, station Schare" (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/). Also messages from "Solgonda" of Venus and others from a planet "Shanchea"; Earth called "Shan" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Van_Tassel; https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/ashtar; https://www.cdamm.org/articles/extraterrestrial). Early messages reference "The Council of the Seven Lights" (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/). Density: described by Wikipedia as "extraterrestrial and interdimensional" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran).
**Channeling began**: Jan 6, 1952 first telepathic messages; July 18, 1952 first Ashtar message (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/; https://www.cdamm.org/articles/extraterrestrial; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran). Encyclopedia.com says messages began January 1952 with the Ashtar message "in July" (https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/ashtar).
**First public/known**: *I Rode a Flying Saucer* (New Age Publishing, 1952; 2nd ed. 1955) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Van_Tassel; https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/).
**Location**: Giant Rock, Mojave Desert, near Landers, California (leased from BLM 1947) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Rock). Born Jefferson, Ohio, March 12, 1910 (Wikipedia) — WRSP gives March 10 (discrepancy); died Feb 9, 1978, Santa Ana, CA (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Van_Tassel; https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/).
**Platforms**: Giant Rock Interplanetary Spacecraft Conventions, annual from 1953 — end year 1971 (Wikipedia) vs 1977 (WRSP) (discrepancy); peak attendance "as many as 10,000" in 1959 (Wikipedia) vs "up to 11,000" (WRSP) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Van_Tassel; https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/). *Proceedings of the College of Universal Wisdom* newsletter 1953–1978 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Van_Tassel). *Life* magazine coverage May 27, 1957 (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/).
**Method**: telepathic reception during weekly group meditations in Frank Critzer's dug-out rooms under Giant Rock; 1958 on-stage channeling of "Knut" (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Rock).
**Core claims**: Ashtar's forces exist to "save mankind from himself"; hydrogen-bomb research threatens life on Earth ("when they explode the hydrogen atom") (https://hatch.kookscience.com/wiki/Ashtar; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran). Nov 1, 1952 H-bomb test passed without catastrophe; later messages claimed Ashtar's forces helped the planet survive (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/). **Distinguishing claims and how the identity changed over time**
1. 1952: patrol-station commandant warning about the hydrogen bomb; "The Council of the Seven Lights"; ships and "stations/vortices" stabilizing Earth; failed H-bomb and landing prophecies (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/; https://encyclopedia.com/science/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/ashtar).
2. 1957: Christianized chain of command — Ashtar commands "ten million" spacemen and reports to Jesus (Hill) (https://www.cdamm.org/articles/extraterrestrial; https://sacred-texts.com/ufo/idtc/index.htm).
3. 1950s–70s Germany/Italy: "Ashtar Sheran", World Teacher and commander of an "Intergalactic Light Fleet" appointed by Christ/Michael, from Metharia; Italy: "commander of the confederated worlds" (sources in Speer and Siragusa blocks).
4. 1977–1985 (Tuella): physical commander of "millions of space ships", second to Sananda, "highest in authority for our hemisphere"; three-phase evacuation of Earth ("Project World Evacuation") (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/; https://www.cdamm.org/articles/extraterrestrial).
5. 1986–1994 (Cole): destruction/evacuation dated to 1994 → failure → "lift-off experience"/"Pioneer Voyage" reinterpreted as consciousness transfer (https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran).
6. Mid-1990s onward: Helland — the movement became "noticeably more spiritually focused and less concerned with extraterrestrial spaceships and visitors"; Ashtar as an "Ascended, Immortal and 'Christed Master'" linked to Sananda and Michael; twelve guidelines (Ashtar.org, 1998–2005) classify failed prophecies as deception by "negative space beings"; ships intervene only in WWIII or "astrophysical catastrophe"; lift-off can happen "during meditation or sleep" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashtar_Sheran; https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/). Helland's chapter title summarizes the arc: "From Extraterrestrials to Ultraterrestrials: The Evolution of the Concept of Ashtar" (2003) (https://www.cdamm.org/articles/extraterrestrial).
7. 1996–2026 internet era: Ashtar absorbed into "Galactic Federation of Light" discourse (commander/leader of the GFL or its "Fifth Fleet"); mass syndication via Ning community Ashtar Command Crew (ashtarcommandcrew.net, "Powered by Ning", "The Awakening Movement", © 2026; founder/founding year not stated on site — gap; commonly attributed to "Ben-Arion", unverified), Facebook "Ashtar Command Tribe", eraoflight.com, sananda.website, Telegram (not verified) (https://www.ashtarcommandcrew.net/; https://wrldrels.org/2016/12/05/ashtar-command/). 2020s messages are heavy on "5D", "ascension", "disclosure", Covid-era conspiracism (Velazquez 2021) and year-specific prophecy (Gabriel 2026).
8. 2025–2026 individualized/psychologized Ashtar: "middle manager", "first among equals" (Substack 2025); Król: "Pleiadian-connected galactic being" with "more of a spirit guide energy", no fleet/command, focused on "light leaders" and business/soul-mission coaching (https://channelings.substack.com/p/ashtar-an-interview; https://youtubetotranscript.com/transcript?v=VhKNfikzcKA).
