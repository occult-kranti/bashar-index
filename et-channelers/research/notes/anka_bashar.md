**Entity channeled:**
- Name: Bashar. Melton's encyclopedia glosses it as "commander" [reference] (same URL as above); Japanese Wikipedia gives "commander / being / messenger" https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%80%E3%83%AA%E3%83%AB%E3%83%BB%E3%82%A2%E3%83%B3%E3%82%AB [secondary].
- Claimed species/collective: a "physical E.T, a friend from the future" https://www.bashar.org/about [official]; a human–Grey (Zeta Reticuli) hybrid of the Sassani/Essassani civilization — "a hybrid of human and 'grey' DNA" https://www.cesnur.org/2012/el-baroni.htm [reference]; "a multidimensional, physical extraterrestrial hybrid being from a parallel reality" and "First Contact Specialist" https://www.bialikbreakdown.com/episodes/aliens-are-making-contact-3000-years-ahead-warning-were-making-big-mistakes-darryl-anka-bashar [press, 2026-03-24]. - Claimed density/dimension: no official page retrieved states a density for Bashar/Essassani. ## Key questions — takeaways, inferences and gaps (researcher summary)

### Exact start dates
**Takeaway:** Class and first channeling 1983 (official bio, Melton, Wikipedia); regular public sessions 1984 (Melton; bashar.org/about "since 1984"; Baroni). First book 1987 (New Metaphysics), preceded by 1985/1987 booklets. Bashar 2.0 is a 2012 session (not 2016). First Contact is a 2016 film (not 2026). BasharTV launch date not established (operating by mid-2024; app by Feb 2025).
**Inferences:** The 1983/1984 discrepancy reflects class-year vs. public-session-year; anniversary events (30th in 2013) count from 1983, while CLE/Bialik copy ("37 years" in 2021; "41 years" in 2026) counts from 1984–85.
**Gaps:** No primary document for BasharTV's launch; no official statement of the Bashar 2.0 recording date beyond the 2012 category.

### Bashar's self-description
**Takeaway:** Physical hybrid ET from Essassani (500 ly toward Orion, parallel reality, ~300 years ahead), "first contact specialist," a future self of Anka; the collective voice of his society. Density is never stated officially; "fifth density" appears only on fan sites.
**Gaps:** No full-session transcript was accessible to quote Bashar directly on Essassani's density or on the five races' names; official highlight clips omit that material.

### Yahyel/Shalanaya and hybrid children
**Takeaway:** Bashar (2009) named the Shalinaya/Yahyel as the first open-contact civilization and (2018) ranked the Sassani third and Yahyel fifth of five hybrid races, with humanity sixth; by May 2025 the official Q&A reflects a shift toward Pleiadians as "most likely" first contact. Hybrid children discussed since at least 1990; "Dear Mom and Dad" (2016) gives the teenagers/333 details.
**Gaps:** Race names for the 1st, 2nd and 4th races are withheld or unavailable; the "2033–2050" arrival window is third-party only.

### Audience footprint
**Takeaway:** As of 2026-10-23: YouTube ~229K (stats sites conflict, 190K–258K), Instagram 439K, Facebook ~240K; up from ~20K Facebook likes in Jan 2014. Most YouTube reach is via third-party clip channels.
**Gaps:** TikTok count; official view totals; BasharTV subscriber numbers.

### Criticisms and commercial model
**Takeaway:** Academic (Baroni: esoteric roots, failed 1990 predictions), skeptic (Skeptoid 2014; RationalWiki; Ross & Carrie 2022–23), press (Boing Boing 2015; Toronto Sun 2011; Bialik 2026; IBTimes 2026), Medium critiques (2024–25). Prices: $12 cassettes (1990), $300+$100 private sessions (2014), $45–95 expo tickets (2023–), $35–39.99/month streaming and $73.95–129.95 live bundles (2026). Anka's own framing: "I can't prove any of this"; predictions are "probabilities" contingent on human choice.
**Gaps:** Toronto Sun article text (blocked); current private-session price (unlisted); any mainstream investigative piece (LA Times/Vice/Guardian) — none found.
