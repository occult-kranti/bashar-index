# Research and collection history

The historical Bashar Index, the Channeling Atlas and the latest-20+ audio archive are three related datasets with different evidence and denominators. This register follows the code and published research through the November 20, 2026 merge of the local CUDA batch. It records what the team tried, which outcomes have receipts and which questions remained open at each observation. [Frozen repository revision](https://github.com/occult-kranti/bashar-index/tree/4a2ac5f0f0e0d6c4956a808573e01822e26c01fe)

## Reading the counts

| Count | What establishes it |
|---|---|
| Listed recording | A canonical video ID appears in a dated listing with its creator and source tab. The cumulative index also holds older selections, Shorts and completed streams. |
| Verified audio | A complete audio object and its provenance pass the checks for their edition, and the archive observes the remote object IDs, sizes and MD5s. Local SHA-256 identifies the accepted bytes. A recording counts once across overlapping editions. |
| Text | An archived caption, publisher transcript or local speech-recognition result carries a separate receipt and edition. Published captions, original automatic captions, machine translations, untimed documents and author-written companions preserve their distinct labels. |
| Source observation | A listing, player response, HTTP status, byte prefix, caption declaration or button describes only that observation. A missing worker report leaves the corresponding source activity unknown. |
| Estimated completion | A reported rate has a named workload and a measured interval. First-pass decisions include failures. Only verified pairs contribute to a verified-audio completion rate. |

The November 19 regular-upload study selected 1,976 recordings from 101 channels. Five of those channels together contributed 56 available regular uploads. Every date and duration was eligible. Earlier 70- and 74-channel snapshots used earlier source lists and sometimes counted different tabs. For a status comparison, use a common selection, observation time and definition of saved audio. [Selection and completion rules](https://github.com/occult-kranti/bashar-index/blob/4a2ac5f0f0e0d6c4956a808573e01822e26c01fe/et-channelers/pipeline/COLLECTION_PROCESS.md) · [#78](https://github.com/occult-kranti/bashar-index/pull/78) · [#83](https://github.com/occult-kranti/bashar-index/pull/83)

## Later checks recorded in private archive receipts
