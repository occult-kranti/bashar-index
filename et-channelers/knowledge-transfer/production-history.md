# Acquisition operations: factual production chronology

This records the finite latest-20+ acquisition campaign and its production experiments. The primary target is 1,976 observed public regular Videos across 101 creator channels. Older Shorts, streams, and other saved artifacts remain indexed and retained. Publication date and duration are not selection cutoffs. Latest-50 acquisition is deferred until the requested post-collection work.

## Source and receipt categories

- **YouTube native original track:** a creator/channel-bound, completed recording with a provider-labelled original audio-only offer. Complete selected bytes, source identity, codec/container quality, and paired Drive objects must validate.
- **YouTube qualified AAC extract:** complete selected MP4 delivery followed by packet-preserving AAC stream copy and quality verification. It preserves source and derivative byte counts separately. Original-track, speaker, spoken-language, and edit identity are not invented.
- **Independent publisher edition:** a creator-owned full episode established by an exact crosslink or sufficiently specific creator, title, guest, date/order and duration evidence. Edition differences remain labelled. This is not a claim of byte identity with the YouTube edit.
- **Text and caption companions:** written messages, transcripts and caption documents retain their own identity and count rules. A text-only companion does not satisfy or block missing selected audio.

Contact, metadata extraction, a captured offer, complete local bytes, quality verification, and a verified Drive pair are separate stages. Only the final eligible receipt supplies archive coverage.

## Production transitions

1. **Reusable ordinary worker and native handoff.** The regular path validates expected channel, video and completed delivery before selecting media. Fresh metadata supplies the offer; an old cached offer is not silently substituted. Explicit original audio can be handed to the existing native worker without a selected-media request in the metadata worker. Source labels survive the handoff.
2. **Qualified batch H7 and Native17.** The 53-item qualified batch closed with 36 paired extracts and 17 native handoffs. The native follow-on archived 16 pairs and retained one actual item refusal. Its shared source allocation was released before subsequent acquisition.
3. **Fresh regular intake and early rolling owners, H1–H3.** A data-driven compiler replaced creator-specific actors. A 5 GB future reservation was reduced to 4.8 GB for a small cohort while preserving the managed-project ceiling and physical spare. Actual local redirect/parser failure stopped the first fresh cohort after seven pairs; 21 unsubmitted rows remained in the durable queue. The unchanged regular path then closed the 21-row continuation with 19 pairs, one native handoff and one actual refusal. Rolling ownership, finite scopes, automatic clock wakes, receipt deduplication and native/ordinary alternation were added around the existing workers.
4. **Mixed public inventory, followed by the regular-Videos cutover.** An initial 74-creator mixed inventory contained 1,458 positions, including Shorts and streams. Public tab observations resolved unknown classifications. The user subsequently clarified that the primary latest-20 criterion meant regular Videos. Fresh public regular listings, restricted-slot accounting and public fill-ins formed the primary queue; older artifacts stayed supplementary. Scope expanded additively to 79, 98 and then 101 verified creator channels, reaching 1,976 primary identities. Canonical channel/video/tab identities and ancestors were preserved.
5. **Adaptive transport health.** The original 24-hour fallback came from local policy reacting to three distinct item-specific 403s without Retry-After. Thirty-one intervening complete transfers showed that a whole-provider pause was not supported. A separate operational health view replaced that artificial wait: one bounded fresh-item canary when required, followed by the normal pool after a verified complete transfer. Unexpected fresh-item failure waves use bounded exponential delay from 60 seconds to 30 minutes. Raw refusals, denied-resource history and genuine global/429/Retry-After controls remain intact. Qualified success is not rewritten as a native HTTP counter reset.
6. **Owned draining and H4–H8 fixes.** A paused parent could strand a child waiting before media permission. A successor cancels only proven owned media-unstarted work, lets already-permitted healthy delivery drain, and records a parent cancellation rather than fabricating a child result. A Windows mutable-progress stat/read race was subsequently reproduced and fixed: an unavailable owned observation briefly parks new permission, while unrelated immutable-file failures still stop. Fresh-offer and local-reconsideration views were bounded to exact authority, actor, capture, identity, closure and history evidence.
7. **Widened resource-aware execution.** The project moved from two-source operation through four to six ordinary source workers, at most two staggered metadata bootstraps, eight outstanding rows, one publication owner and three Drive file transfers. Actual RAM/commit, known response length and byte backpressure determine admission. The managed ceiling moved from 16 GB to 18 GB, and later to 23 GB; the settled physical free-disk reserve is 12 GB. Existing sealed actors retained their own settings until a real closed/released boundary.
8. **H9 proof and deduplication improvements.** A receipt detected before source submission can be skipped only after strict paired-proof verification; malformed or text-only records do not become audio coverage. Native transport health uses the last contiguous protocol-EOF clock, exact bytes/hash, closed ownership, quality and canonical receipt. A later quality/upload timestamp is not evidence of a later source transfer. An independently invalid or redirected pair supplies no direct-health proof without discarding other independently valid direct pairs.
9. **H10/H11 native grouped publication.** An initial grouped native preparation stopped before source ownership because settings allowed eight outstanding rows while a second grant validator still expected seven. Both real validators were aligned and exercised against retained real inputs before the first grouped production run. The grouped publisher batches immutable pairs into one copy and readback, keeps each pair independently accountable, retains originals on partial failure and preserves the existing Rclone quota journal. A full code/lease guard runs immediately before each real group copy. An earlier missing nested batch-stat delegation was repaired; 13 already-complete files were published offline instead of downloaded again.
10. **H12 operational recovery.** A same-response read idle allowance increased from 20 to 60 seconds while retaining the total deadline, same client/connection and zero request retries. Exact old and new transport capabilities are accepted alongside old receipts. Three former local failures then completed qualified pairs; the fourth took a newly captured original-audio route and completed natively. Startup-only missing input/API-export issues were retained as source-zero evidence. The restart health check was corrected to validate native EOF evidence with the native verifier and reject an obsolete positive window after a newer refusal.
11. **One four-file Drive trial.** A separately bound native actor allowed four file transfers only for an adequately sized group, under the same six-source/eight-outstanding/one-publisher and 23 GB/12 GB controls. All 15 items archived safely. The size-matched comparison did not show a reliable gain, so three remains the default.
12. **H13 shared-journal contention.** The child and parent both record an observed refusal. Their finite HTTP-journal lock was nonblocking, allowing an ordinary lock collision to become a generic parent failure and stop unrelated work. Two real Windows process fixtures reproduced the old failure and validated a bounded acquisition wait for this one lock: the commit body executes once, duplicate refusal stays one outcome, and a genuine global STOP is still written before waiting. The running successor uses this correction. A typed pre-media retry requires the corrected code and closed media-unstarted evidence; it cannot exempt a current refusal or replay accepted bytes.

## Retry, recovery and reconciliation

- An actual current refusal remains a refused offer. Publication restriction, provider-wide challenge, Retry-After, an expired locator, a local timeout and a never-started media request are distinct facts.
- A retained complete source is taken through offline quality/publication; it is not fetched again. This recovered closed Hce, tH81, Ba4 and ZBU bodies. A further complete five-megabyte source was retained when a late controlled boundary stop interrupted local processing.
- A partial is neither completion nor an automatic resume opportunity. Retained early partials lacked a strong validator/If-Range proof for safe splicing.
- Ordinary metadata/format failures and native local preparation failures do not consume unrelated queue entries. Source-zero startup does not permanently spend an item or intent.
- A single bounded Drive metadata inventory found 15 validated historical orphan pairs, followed by one additional exact pair. Their canonical registrations required no new media delivery.
- Creator-owned RSS/enclosures and official episode pages supplied independent editions. Julie's 20 completed editions also supplied 20 declared English documents/texts. Ten Natalie and twenty Mariya editions were separately verified. A Dallisa association explicitly shares one existing publisher audio file between two selected listings while counting unique files honestly.
- Negative research is retained: a matching written message is not audio; a conference invitation is not the later talk; a similar interview with a materially different publication history is not automatically the selected edit.
- A boundary watcher was found to lag behind an already-admitted next turn. Its old proof was retained and the actual later closed/released turns reconciled without bypassing head equality. Future coordination needs an admission fence that does not become a new in-flight worker stop.

## Current completion contract

The source owner dispatches only still-eligible primary V9 members. It cannot fall through into historical supplements or Latest50. Every source/native turn must actually close and release before another provider actor owns the lane. New denials, unknown/global responses, actual resource pressure and Drive cooldown remain effective.

A private completion signal requires strict receipts for all 1,976 identities and their per-channel extents. A held or exhausted-ready queue is not completion. A completion-only owner can recheck late publisher receipts without a new media request. Post-collection work additionally requires actual collector exit, empty owned jobs/released source reservations and an exclusive queue-owner lock. Current totals belong in the dated disposition exports rather than this historical record.
