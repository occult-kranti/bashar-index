# Paper draft: build notes, sources of numbers, and checks before submission

This folder holds an anonymous draft for ICWSM in the AAAI two-column format. It was written on 2026-11-20 while the transcription run was still going. Every number computed from transcripts is provisional.

**In this repository.** The folder holds the paper, its references, its numbers, tables and figures. The scripts that generated them (`work/`, `work2/`) and the downloaded e-print (`style-source/`) are kept in the project's private archive, not here, so the rows for them below describe the private copy. `build.sh` here needs only `pdflatex` and `bibtex`; the tables in `floats/` are already split. The draft is anonymous, but this repository is not: decide where the draft lives before a double-blind submission.

## How to build

MiKTeX with `pdflatex` and `bibtex` on the path, and Python 3 for the table split.

```
bash build.sh
```

or by hand, from this folder:

```
python -I work2/split_floats.py findings/tables.tex floats
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

Packages used: `times`, `helvet`, `courier`, `url`, `graphicx`, `natbib`, `caption`, `booktabs`, `array`. No package was installed by hand. The last build gave no error, no undefined citation or reference and no overfull box; the log has some underfull-box notes only. MiKTeX prints "unsupported version of Windows" on this machine; it does not affect the output.

After a build, run the checks:

```
python -I work2/check_paper.py <private per-creator table> <public failed-links table>
```

The two optional arguments let the script read creator names so that it can confirm none of them occurs in the paper. It prints counts only.

## Where each number comes from

1. **Measured numbers** (cohort, run, snapshot, distributions, variance shares, correlations, artefacts, reading sample, term families, dates): macros in `findings/numbers.tex`. The comment after each macro names the source file and field. `main.tex` uses 300-odd of them and never types such a number directly.
2. **Collection facts and design parameters** (objects verified, reasons for held recordings, worker settings, sample sizes of planned experiments, gates and margins): macros in `setup/setup-numbers.tex`, each with its source: the pre-analysis checkpoint, the transcription setup document, the experiment catalogue, section 13.7 of the analysis plan, or the private plan-and-data audit.
3. **Throughput** (595.3 audio hours in 10.15 hours, 58.7 per hour; 87.5 of 710.1 hours without speech at the last progress row): computed from the progress table, saved in `setup/throughput.json`.
4. **Numerals typed directly in `main.tex`.** These are thresholds and labels quoted from the sources, not measurements. `setup/check-report.json` lists every one with its context. They are:
   - 13:00 UTC, the time of the snapshot (header of the plan-and-data audit);
   - HTTP 403 (failed-links table); MD5 and SHA-256 (names of digests);
   - "27 to 30 seconds" into a decoding window, "at most three words", "under a second of gap" (reading notes, section 2.4, as worded in the artefact table);
   - 30 seconds (quiet stretches) and 30 days (window bands), 50 words, 150 words, 300 words, 500-word windows for the moving-average type-token ratio (the definitions behind the corresponding fields of the descriptive summary and the reading notes);
   - 95% intervals and the 95th percentile;
   - rule numbers R1 to R16 and "part 1 of 2";
   - thresholds written as words: five or more transcripts, at most three finished transcripts and at least ten still to come (hold-out), ten or more recordings in another text kind, one minute of speech, five minutes, an hour, two years, seven measures, about a hundred creators, about one eighth.

- **Section 5.6:** the transcripts of hold-out creators in the ledger (322 at 17:15 UTC).
- **Section 6 (all of it),** Tables 5 to 8, Figures 1 and 2, appendix Figures A2 and A3.
- **Section 7:** the snapshot coverage (70% of files, 68% of hours), the 518, 393 and 27 of the unfinished part.

When this README was written the ledger held 1,651 lines (1,649 distinct audio files), more than the 1,641 audio files of the 17:15 UTC count that the paper states with its time stamp (`setup/ledger-count-at-final-check.json`).

### Steps

1. Re-run the panel's descriptive script on the closed ledger, then the pilot and the pattern passes if their numbers are kept. The artefact, reading and term numbers are literals in `work/build_findings.py` and have to be edited by hand.
2. Decide first whether the hold-out is still held. If the registry is not frozen, give `work/build_findings.py` a fixed list of the 71 creators already seen; otherwise files of hold-out creators enter the figures.
3. Run `work/build_findings.py` (rewrites `numbers.tex`, the figures, their CSV files).
4. Re-run `work2/throughput.py` on the final progress table and update the four throughput macros in `setup/setup-numbers.tex`.
5. `bash build.sh`, then `work2/check_paper.py`.
6. Read every caption and every sentence with "snapshot", "provisional" or a time stamp, and the abstract.

## What the owner must check before submission

**Status and honesty**

1. The paper says the registry is not frozen, no experiment has been run, no transcript was checked by a person, and the bridge, the replication wave and the comparison set are approved but not collected. Update each statement if it has changed.
2. Tables 5 and 6 include 37 audio files of creators with fewer than five transcripts, 32 of them from hold-out creators, because their source numbers were computed before the hold-out was declared. The paper says so in three places. Decide whether to recompute these tables without those files.
3. The pilot behind "inside a recording" (1,093 recordings, 89 creators) includes creators outside the 71. The paper says so.
4. The sources disagree in places: the other-text breakdown sums to 216 against a total of 214 (the paper gives the total and four kinds); the audit says one recognizer model, the catalogue two (the paper follows the catalogue); "at most 3 transcripts" alone gives 30 creators, the hold-out rule gives 23.
5. The stock cut-off of -1.0 was not confirmed in the recognizer paper; the text says so.
6. The moving-average type-token ratio is defined in the text and has no citation, because no reference for it was verified.

**People, review and disclosure**

7. Section 5.1 describes "a panel of 4 roles" and Section 6 "one reader". The sources do not say whether these were people or AI assistants. State it. The analysis plan requires that AI use in coding, design and drafting is disclosed.
8. Section 8 says that the literature studies used an automated extractor and that this draft was prepared with an AI writing assistant. Confirm or complete the statement, and check the venue's policy on it.
9. Section 8 says that no ethics review decision is recorded. Replace it with the real status.
10. The paper says creators are not named in the paper or in analysis outputs. The project's public collection tables list recordings by creator and title. Make sure the two statements are consistent with what is public, and that nothing public breaks the anonymity of the submission (the earlier single-channeler study is mentioned once, in Section 3).
11. The platform is not named in the paper ("one video platform"). Decide whether to name it.

**Literature**

12. Statements about cited papers come from the two literature digests, which were made by automated extraction and abstract-level search. Check each sentence of Section 2, and the citations in Sections 1 and 5, against the papers (rule R16). The paper quotes no number from any cited paper.
13. `attanasio2025effects`: the arXiv title says "Algorithmic Visibility" where the proceedings title says "Mainstream Visibility". `gerard2024modeling` and `habib2021reddit` also carry arXiv titles that differ from the proceedings titles. For six ICWSM entries the venue comes only from the digest. `jo2025metaharm` was known to the digest from a snippet, and the paper describes it from its title only.
14. `wahbeh2026semantic` is cited for its existence and stated design only; the digest read a summary page, not the paper.
15. The ICWSM 2018 paper by Samory and Mitra has no arXiv version and is not cited; `samory2018government` is their CSCW paper. Nine ICWSM papers of the literature study are not cited for the same reason.
16. Every ICWSM reference prints "ICWSM <year>; arXiv version cited" after the arXiv identifier. Check that the venue accepts this form.

**Format**

17. The style files are the AAAI 2025 kit as used by an ICWSM 2026 paper on arXiv. Check them against the kit of the target year, and add the ICWSM paper checklist, which is not in this draft.
18. Length: about 10.3 pages before the references. Two figures (creator pace; speech along the running time) and the calendar figure are in an appendix after the references, numbered A1 to A3. Move them back if the page limit allows.
19. The title is in mixed case, as the AAAI kit asks. Section headings are in sentence case, as the brief asked; the kit's own examples use title case.
20. The two experiment tables are numbered 2 and 4, with the rules table as 3 between them.
21. `\pdfinfo` is left out: the submission option of the style file ignores it. Add it for a camera-ready version.
