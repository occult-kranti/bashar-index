# References report

Written for the second subagent. It describes `refs.bib` (70 entries) and `refs-reserve.bib` (44 entries), both generated by `work/build_bib.py` from metadata fetched in this session, on 2026-11-20.

## What was done

- Every entry was written from a response of the arXiv API (`http://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=...`) or of Crossref (`https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`). The raw responses are in `work/arxiv_raw/` and `work/crossref_raw/`; the parsed copies are `work/arxiv_ids.json` and `work/crossref_doi.json`. Titles and authors in the entries are the ones the service returned. No title or author was typed from memory.
- `refs.bib` holds 70 entries: 47 from the arXiv API and 23 from Crossref. 32 of the arXiv entries are ICWSM papers.
- The digests list 41 ICWSM papers (32 read in full, 9 at abstract level). An arXiv version was found for 32 of them and is in `refs.bib`. None was found for 9; they are listed below and are not in the bib.
- 28 of the ICWSM arXiv ids were already in the digest. Four more were found here: three by the exact-title query `ti:"..."` (`chen2025digital`, `zaman2025disagreement`, `jo2025metaharm`) and one by a query on two title words (`attanasio2025effects`, whose arXiv title differs by one word; see its row).
- The ICWSM proceedings site was not fetched. No paper text was read in this session, only metadata. Numbers quoted from these papers in the catalogue come from machine extraction and must be checked against the PDFs before they are repeated (catalogue rule R16).

## Conventions in the bib files

- Key: first author's family name in lower-case ASCII, then the year, then the first significant title word (hyphenated words joined), for example `russo2022spillover`.
- Year of an arXiv entry: the year of first submission to arXiv (the API's `published` field). It is also the year in the key. It can be earlier than the conference year, which is in `note`.
- Year of a Crossref entry: the print year when Crossref gives one, otherwise the issued year.
- arXiv entries are `@article` with `journal = {arXiv preprint arXiv:<id>}`, so that a style file without `eprint` support still prints the identifier. They also carry `eprint`, `archivePrefix`, `primaryClass` and `url`.
- Every ICWSM entry has `note = {ICWSM <year>; arXiv version cited}`. The extra field `venuesource` says where that venue comes from. It is not a standard field and is not printed. 26 ICWSM entries take the venue from the arXiv record (comment, journal-ref or DOI field). The other 6 read `venuesource = {venue as stated in the digest}`, because the arXiv record names no venue: `attanasio2025effects`, `zhao2023discovering`, `rai2023deep`, `chandio2026examining`, `liaw2023younicon`, `jo2025metaharm`.
- For related-venue entries with an arXiv version, `note` gives the venue where the arXiv record or the digest states one (for example `note = {EMNLP 2020; arXiv version cited}`), and `venuesource` again says which.
- Titles are double-braced, so they print with the capitalisation the service returned. Remove the inner braces if the style should set sentence case; proper nouns then need their own braces.
- The files are pure ASCII; accented letters are LaTeX accent macros. Both files compiled together with BibTeX and `plainnat` in a local harness with no warning. They were not tested with the conference style file.
- `refs-reserve.bib` holds entries verified in the same way and left out of `refs.bib` only to keep it within the 70 entries asked for. Add `refs-reserve` to the `\bibliography` command if one is needed. One reserve DOI contains an underscore (`bender2018data`); a style that prints DOIs needs the `url` or `doi` package for it.
