\subsection{Pipeline}
\label{sec:pipeline}

\paragraph{Decoding.} Audio is cut into contiguous, non-overlapping 30-s windows at 16\,kHz, each decoded alone without
conditioning on earlier text (beam 5, temperature 0 without fallback, word time stamps). In each window a voice detector
(500-ms minimum silence) runs first, so stretches it finds without speech never reach the recognizer. The recorded
settings name Whisper large-v3 \cite{radford2022robust}: its distilled English-only version for English
\cite{gandhi2023distilwhisper}, the multilingual model for other languages, partly by its own translation into
English.

\paragraph{Scope.} Seam findings hold only for this fixed-grid chunked decoding, not for Whisper's sequential
long-form transcription, which moves each window by its predicted time stamps and conditions it on earlier text, nor
for cut-and-merge at detected speech \cite{bain2023whisperx}. Not stated here: implementation, compute type, hardware and
detector settings; the handling of detector-removed audio and of the last, partial window; the shifted grid's start;
and what differed between worker versions. Nobody listened, and no independent voice detector, forced aligner or
speaker separation ran (Appendix~\ref{app:measure}), so voice spans are inferred from text.

\paragraph{Language and models.} \LangCreatorRulePct\% of language decisions follow the creator rule, which assigns
the creator's language without probing the recording; the only guard re-checks recordings whose mean log-probability
falls below \LogpGate{} (seen median \SeenLogpMedian) and would pass fluent English-like text written for other speech.
No language decision was checked by a person. As amended (A10), registered tests, and the seen-derived constants they
use, read only English-only-model transcripts: \NSeenEnglishOnly{} of the \NSeenFiles{} seen ones; the rest form one
sensitivity row, and cross-model comparisons are descriptive (Section~\ref{sec:bridge}).

\paragraph{Text versions and pauses.} Each number uses raw text, filter version 1 (H3 and the two-model bridge of
Section~\ref{sec:bridge}, as run) or
filter version 2, named in captions. Version 2 removes, by position, a thanks phrase starting in a window's last
3\,s, which appears to be inserted (Section~\ref{sec:artefacts}), and also isolated short segments, stock text, repeats
beyond the third, and loops (Table~\ref{tab:artefacts}). The capped seam rule drops a gap under 5\,s that contains a
window boundary and weights any other gap of $g<5$\,s by $1/(1-g/30)$; the literal rule and no rule are sensitivity
rows. The weight assumes gaps independent of grid phase, which fails in part (Section~\ref{sec:seams}).
