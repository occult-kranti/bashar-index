\section{The third-round panel's programme}
\label{sec:panel3}

After the exploratory round, the lead moderated a third panel of twelve model agents playing roles (six method members,
three domain and emic members and three denialists; no practitioner, ethics member or sociologist of religion sat),
which met in four rounds and pooled 106 proposals. The lead adopted its programme on 26 November 2026 with 13 rulings
(Appendix~\ref{app:panel3}). Of the 106, 88 are no-cost work on the seen creators, 9 register for a future wave of fresh
creators, 4 have an unfunded primary part and 5 are set aside. Before any claimed-voice difference is read as a property
of speech, the decoding grid, the recognizer by voice, position and the labeller's own-offer rule are checked with
frozen code. Four public deposits fixed the forecast ledger's hash, the margins, readings and split policy, the blind
word lists and the year roles before any run or count that uses them.

Step~1's instrument checks give readings, not findings: A when the 90\% interval lies wholly beyond the margin in the
named direction, B when it lies wholly within it, otherwise undetermined (all 17 entries in their own words in
Tables~\ref{tab:panel3-step1} and~\ref{tab:panel3-step1b}). The four checks at the head of the programme's order read
as follows. The grid's seam lock and punctuation near seams read undetermined, and some rows' readings are not
printed for privacy. For the recognizer by voice, no row whose reading is printed reads ``differential instability
detected'': confidence, filter removals and transport read ``no differential instability detected among
Whisper-family decodes; shared normalisation untested'', the seam share and both second-decode bases are
undetermined, and some rows' readings are not printed for privacy. Position leaves H1
undetermined and H4 with ``no reference holds size at this count'' (H6's reading is not printed for privacy), while
pay-or-enrol asks read A under its position strata and the placement behind them is undetermined. The own-offer island
gate did not fire. Steps~2 to~5 (the lexicon, forecasts and time, the platform and the audience, and voice, register
and footing) give each entry's reading in its own words in Appendix~\ref{app:panel3-steps25}, and the audio entries and
the audio addendum do so in Appendix~\ref{app:panel3-audio}. New recordings of the seen creators then
entered a descriptive persistence round, not a confirmation (Appendix~\ref{app:newfiles}). The programme has 56 primaries and no family-wise adjustment: if every true effect
sat at half its margin, about 2.8 readings of A would be expected by chance at the deposited one-sided 5\%, and up to
about 6.1 in some interval classes, so a reading A is never called a finding. All of it is exploratory, not a test of
H1--H10, on seen creators only; labels are model-coded, not checked by a person, and compared by agreement. Novelty
statements print only after a second attack pass and a bibliography check (Appendix~\ref{app:panel3-novelty}).
