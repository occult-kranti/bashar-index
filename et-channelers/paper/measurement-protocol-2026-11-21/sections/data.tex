\section{Corpus and pipeline}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Corpus}

\paragraph{Selection.} The authors chose \NCreators{} creators on one video platform who describe their practice as
channelling: they publish recordings presented as messages channelled from non-human sources. The owner wrote this rule down after the
registry freeze and after testing H3, the one label-free hypothesis (Section~\ref{sec:hthree}); the roster grew from
\RosterFirst{} to \NCreators{} on the listing day. A later discovery harvest, closed early after \HarvestQueries{} of
\HarvestTerms{} planned queries, met \HarvestChannels{} channels, \HarvestCohortFound{} of them roster creators, and
fixes no sampling frame. This judgment sample represents no population (Section~\ref{sec:related}): intervals refer
to creators like these, not to all who describe their practice so, a period or a trend.

\paragraph{Quota.} Each creator gives its newest \NPerCreator{} public regular uploads, a quota, not a period: they span
\SpanPtenDays{} days to \SpanPninetyYears{} years from the creators' 10th to 90th percentile (median \SpanMedDays{}
days), so posting rate is entangled with comparisons between creators (Appendix~\ref{app:measure}). Dates are upload
dates.

\medskip
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{\dimexpr\columnwidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule\relax}{\small\textbf{Corpus summary}; counts are
recordings. \textit{Selected:} \NSelected. \textit{Archived:} \NArchived{} with hash-verified audio; \NFailed{} final download failures (\NFailedCreators{} creators).
\textit{Set aside before the run:} \NSetAside, with English text of another kind (\NOtherTextCreators{} creators) or
held for a language review; \NOwnEarlierAsr{} of them hold the project's own earlier recognizer text. \textit{Run:}
\NRunRecordings{} entered (\RunHours{} h); \NTranscribedRecordings{} transcribed (\TranscribedHours{} h, \NLanguages{}
languages); \NNotTranscribed{} not, most for want of speech. \textit{Excluded:} live streams, short videos, paid or
members-only material, other platforms, recordings deleted before the listing (Appendix~\ref{app:measure}).}}
\medskip

\paragraph{Groups.} \textit{Seen} creators (\NSeen{}; \NSeenFiles{} transcripts, \SeenHours{} h) are those whose
transcripts model agents could read while designing instruments; prompts were developed on some
(development creators), gate and verification samples drawn from others (check creators). Every number from text,
word times or the voice detector comes from them. \textit{Hold-out} creators (\NHoldout{}; \NHoldoutFiles{}
transcripts, with all \NNonEnglishFiles{} non-English ones, from \NNonEnglishCreators{} creators) are kept for the
registered tests (Section~\ref{sec:holdout}). The rest, \NOtherCreators{} creators, form an \textit{other} group, for
a reason the registry does not record. Non-seen creators enter only as counts, durations, dates, languages and run
order.

\paragraph{Privacy rule.} No printed cell, bin or figure rests on fewer than 5 creators. Of two nested groups whose
creator counts differ by 1 to 4, only one count is printed, and values recoverable by difference are suppressed (bar
the other group's size, which public counts fix); such places read ``not shown (privacy rule)'' or give a bound.
