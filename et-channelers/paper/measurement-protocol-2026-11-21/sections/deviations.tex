\section{Deviations, and what the protocol teaches}
\label{sec:deviations}

\paragraph{Deviations.} Every departure from the registry is reported \cite{willroth2024best}. The deviation table lists all \NDeviationRows{} deviations (the exploratory round's
\NDevRoundRows{} as one) with their time, scope, the results known then and their direction
(Table~\ref{tab:deviations}): \NDevStricter{} stricter,
\NDevGuarded{} guarded by A10, \NDevNeutral{} neutral, \NDevLooser{} looser, \NDevMixed{} mixed (stricter for some uses
and looser for others, or unknown), \NDevExploratory{} exploratory, \NDevBudget{} budget or operations, and
\NDevOther{} other. The registry does not record who classified the directions, and no one outside the project checked
them. The decisive ones concern H3: its reference held creators fixed, its estimand was off-centre, its interval was
unaudited, and it was withdrawn without its registered wave replication; the cohort's selection rule was written after
its test. The reporting plan's exploratory round, begun after the tests, ran on code written after the analysis
freeze, a deviation declared before any of its outputs was read.

Outside A10's guard lie the looser and mixed deviations: for every label-based test, no person check (A8),
verification by models (A11), an instrument freeze before the known-answer battery finished, and
``uninformative'' undefined at the registry freeze; for single hypotheses, relayed voice merged into claimed voice
after its gate and the pre-declared merge failed (H1, H6), a forecast class of record chosen after a gate failure (H2), instruments below their
floors (H2, H5, H9), revisions inside both guarded versions (H2, H5) and a placeholder smallest effect (H9); for the
first-round inputs, two readings chosen after the hold-out gates were computed (voice gate A per instrument; the title
exception kept); for the hold-out, contacts before the analysis freeze (Section~\ref{sec:limits}) and a loosened release rule for robustness
rows, which were not bought, for budget reasons.

\paragraph{What this protocol teaches.} (a)~Base minimums on a sample-size
analysis: sign-test arithmetic alone committed the registry to tests that, on the cautious projection, a
\NHoldout-creator hold-out could not determine. (b)~Project eligibility on the cautious, creator-level bound; the
announcement made on it held. (c)~Eligibility built on model-coded constructs makes determinability depend on the
labeller (H1, H4). (d)~Re-gate on a hold-out reliability sample: two instruments that passed on the seen gate samples
failed there; selection on those samples, the difference between the groups and chance on a small sample may each
contribute. (e)~Read a design-weighted cross-vendor kappa beside its hard stratum; a reader of the drafting agents'
family is only weakly independent \cite{kim2025correlated}. (f)~Hashes fix content, not time: deposit the registry
externally. (g)~Deviations can raise or lower a test's severity \cite{lakens2024when}; a fixed Holm count with unrun
places at $p = 1$ is conservative but costly with few creators, and a wave of the same creators would show
persistence, not confirmation (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}).
