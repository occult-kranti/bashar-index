\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Long-form recognition and its artefacts.} End-to-end recognizers degrade on long audio \cite{narayanan2019recognizing}, and long-form strategies differ: overlapping segments \cite{chiu2019comparison}, voice-detector cut-and-merge against drifting buffered windows \cite{bain2023whisperx}, or conditioning on earlier text, whose errors can accumulate \cite{ahn2026whispercd}. Non-speech induces recurring Whisper hallucinations \cite{baranski2025investigation,wang2025calmwhisper} that word error rates can hide \cite{frieske2024hallucinations}; recognizer word times are less exact than forced alignment \cite{rousso2024tradition} with an aligner such as MFA \cite{mcauliffe2017montreal}. Benchmarks and social-science audits measure error against human text \cite{delrio2021earnings,proksch2019testing}; reference-free checks estimate or rank error per output \cite{ali2020ewer2,yuksel2023norefer}. This paper measures their size and position on this audio with a shifted and a placebo grid (Section~\ref{sec:seams}).

\paragraph{Labels from language models.} Models are evaluated as codebook-following measurement tools \cite{halterman2026codebook}, but each task needs checks against people \cite{pangakis2024keeping}. Labels move with prompt design and format \cite{atreja2025whats,sclar2024quantifying}; model judges favour their own outputs \cite{panickssery2024evaluators}, and errors correlate more within a provider and, for large models, even across providers \cite{kim2025correlated}. Constructed inputs test capabilities that aggregate scores hide \cite{ribeiro2020beyond}, and reliability minimums come from human coding \cite{krippendorff2004reliability}. Misclassification biases downstream estimates \cite{teblunthuis2023misclassification}, text measures need validation in each use \cite{grimmer2013text,jacobs2019measurement}, and corrections need a person-coded sample \cite{egami2023imperfect,angelopoulos2023prediction}, which this protocol lacks.

\paragraph{Registration and few-cluster tests.} Choices made after seeing data inflate false positives \cite{simmons2011false,gelman2014statistical}. Preregistration fixes the plan before outcomes are seen, also for existing data \cite{nosek2018preregistration,weston2019recommendations,vandenakker2021preregistration}; audits find departures, and guides ask that each be reported \cite{claesen2021comparing,willroth2024best,lakens2024when}. Tests with few clusters need care \cite{canay2017randomization}, and small designs exaggerate effects \cite{card2020little,gelman2014beyond}.

\paragraph{Channelling and the platform.} Trance channelling has been studied ethnographically \cite{hughes1991blending}, mediums' interaction with sitters by conversation analysis \cite{wooffitt2006language}, and channelling by one research group through physiology and message semantics \cite{wahbeh2019physiological,wahbeh2026semantic}. The cohort was not selected for conspirituality \cite{ward2011emergence,asprem2015conspirituality}, and no measure of it is reported. Unlike metadata collections and random samples of the platform \cite{hortaribeiro2020youniverse,mcgrady2023dialing}, a hand-chosen roster represents no population. We claim no first: metadata-index searches found no study of channelling recordings across many speakers (Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}); not found means only that.
