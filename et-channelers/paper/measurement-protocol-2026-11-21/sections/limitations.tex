\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Selection and reference.} The judgment sample has a selection rule written down late, excluded kinds of recording and a quota that entangles posting rate with comparisons between creators (Section~\ref{sec:data}). Nobody listened to the audio; the caption anchor rests on set-aside recordings and a normaliser chosen after the result; errors shared by all model readers go undetected, and the cross-vendor reader shares the drafting agents' model family; corrections that need gold-standard labels cannot be used (Section~\ref{sec:verification}).

\paragraph{Pipeline.} Seam claims hold for fixed-grid chunked decoding only, and some configuration items are not stated (Section~\ref{sec:pipeline}). A creator rule without a language probe decides \LangCreatorRulePct\% of language decisions; the bridge covers English only, and every other language has fewer than five creators, all in the hold-out. Voice spans lack speaker separation, and the own voice concentrates in openings and closings, so voice is partly confounded with position. Without a comparison corpus, nothing is called distinctive of channelling.

\paragraph{Hold-out and registration.} The hold-out is not a random split (Section~\ref{sec:holdout}); before the analysis freeze, hold-out material was touched (Table~\ref{tab:contacts}) by the first panel's snapshot tables (before the hold-out existed) and position pilot (so early hold-out files probably entered the reading that suggested H3), the run report's position displays, H3's registered run, a panel script loading hold-out metadata against the round's rule, a logged time-stamp extraction, and output scripts loading a timing cache with hold-out rows; after it, by a driver outside the freeze running the frozen reliability report, and, after the tests, by the lead's privacy count, the privacy recounts behind the public results file and the exploratory round's logged runs, reads and searches. No contact produced a statistic of a registered hypothesis beyond the registered tests. The registration is internal only.

\paragraph{Programme and reproducibility.} The deviations of Section~\ref{sec:deviations}, gate failures included, limit every label-based statement. Audio and transcripts cannot be shared, and access to identifiers or derived features needs the owner's consent, while code, frozen hashes and the distributions behind each float are planned for release; GPU type and hours are not stated; the labelling models are proprietary aliases that may be retired, and the prompts and codebooks are not released.

\paragraph{Third-round programme.} Its panel members, attackers and search helpers are model agents of one family, so
their agreement is not a check by people, and their literature searches reach the humanities poorly. Preconditions
found by the novelty attacks limit the step-1 entries and bind later registrations: whether the labeller's input
carried decoder segment breaks, window phase against the decoder's earlier text, recurring predicates in shifted
placebos, inclusive against exclusive ``we'', qualifier families disjoint from the forecast detector, control years
matched on topic, register-matched placebos, and declared character kinds fixed before collection. Some leads that may
cover parts of the novelty wordings were not opened. The acoustic rate instrument does not meet its
battery's slope criterion (decision~106.3), and the shifted-grid re-decode of the whole roster was not run
(decision~106.4). Whether a model alias resolved to one snapshot cannot be told from
stored runs (Appendix~\ref{app:panel3-limits}).
