\section*{Ethics statement}
\label{sec:ethics}

\paragraph{Material.} The recordings are public uploads; public availability is not consent to research use. Copies are held privately for non-consumptive analysis. The paper holds no transcript text, quotation, name, code, identifier or title, and no row for one recording or creator. No reported cell holds fewer than five creators: smaller cells are merged, and a creator count that falls fewer than five short of a printed total is stated against it (``all but fewer than 5''), so that no difference of printed counts is such a cell; positional and other timing displays use seen creators only. These rules cover this paper only: the project's public repository still holds named tables of the public channels and their recordings, and earlier per-recording files in its history. This paper does not link to it.

\paragraph{External processing.} The request ledger, read at \ExtStamp, holds \ExtRequests{} requests to the language-model API with \ExtInputTokensM{} million input tokens, about \ExtWordsM{} million words of transcript-derived text with repeats (an estimate); Table~\ref{tab:external} in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol} lists every service that received project data. Transcript text went to the OpenAI API from seen creators and, after freeze 2, from hold-out and other-group creators for the frozen instruments only; it includes speech of guests, clients and audience members (\ThirdPartySharePct\% of words in a model-coded pilot; 95\% \ThirdPartyShareLo--\ThirdPartyShareHi). The other categories of the table are public metadata and web search results of candidate channels for the channeler discovery (\ExtDiscoveryRequests{} requests), constructed known-answer texts (\ExtKnownAnswerRequests), and seen statements sent with the vendor's web search tool (\ExtWebStatementRequests); requests without a tag are listed as such. Requests were sent with \texttt{store=false}, but the vendor keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days unless zero data retention is approved, and no such approval is on file. The retention terms for the Anthropic agents were not read. A rule that keeps client and audience spans out of future calls is proposed for the owner's decision.

\paragraph{Determinations.} No ethics or exemption determination exists. A lawful basis for archiving the audio and for sending participants' speech to a commercial service, a data-protection assessment and a removal route are the owner's acts and do not exist yet.

\paragraph{Agents and claims.} Anthropic model agents made the panels, hypothesis proposals, skeptical review, first-panel transcript reading, literature extraction, novelty searches, cross-vendor reading, the decisions recorded as the lead's under the owner's delegation, and this draft; the records name vendor and role, not a model version per step. Labels came from the OpenAI models fixed in freeze 1. No person checked any label. No statement in the recordings gets a truth label, nothing is said about a creator's sincerity, health or mental state, and for a prediction we report only what was predicted and whether an outcome is observable.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Planned for release: code, frozen hashes and the derived distributions behind each float. Audio and transcripts stay private; access to identifiers or derived features needs the owner's consent.
