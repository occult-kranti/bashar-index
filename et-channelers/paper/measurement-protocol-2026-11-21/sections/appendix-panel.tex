\section{The third-round panel's programme}
\label{app:panel3}

This appendix is the record behind Section~\ref{sec:panel3}: the panel and its programme (\ref{app:panel3-design}),
the deposits (\ref{app:panel3-deposits}), margins, readings, intervals, tiers and multiplicity
(\ref{app:panel3-readings}), step~1's results (\ref{app:panel3-step1}), the results of steps~2 to~5
(\ref{app:panel3-steps25}), the audio entries (\ref{app:panel3-audio}), the new-files round, which
followed the programme (\ref{app:newfiles}), the fourth panel's programme (\ref{app:panel4}), the novelty wordings
(\ref{app:panel3-novelty}) and the limitations (\ref{app:panel3-limits}). The files are public in the research folder
(\texttt{panel-third-round-2026-11-22/}). Everything here is exploratory and not a test of H1--H10. It rests on the seen
creators only; labels are model-coded and not checked by a person, and their comparisons are agreement, never accuracy.
No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these
recordings and how the creators present it. Times are UTC on 26 November 2026.

\subsection{Panel, proposals and classes}
\label{app:panel3-design}

\paragraph{The panel.} Twelve model agents played roles: six method members (a statistician, a computational linguist,
a computational social scientist, a measurement methodologist, a speech and audio researcher and a researcher of
annotation with language models), three domain and emic members (two historians and a reader of conspiracy culture, an
emic reader and not a practitioner) and three denialists (a materialist skeptic, a methodological denialist and a
sociological debunker). None is a person and none speaks for any community. A practitioner, an ethics member and a
sociologist of religion were not seated. The lead moderated; a moderator's assistant, also a model agent, wrote each
round's synthesis.

\paragraph{Four rounds.} Proposals; positions and concessions; final positions with a ranked top ten, vetoes and plot
requests; and novelty, with literature searches and attacks. Round~3's pooled list (r3-P01 to r3-P73) and round~4's
(r4-P01 to r4-P33) give 106 pooled proposals. Ranked by Borda count, six entries were in eleven or twelve of the twelve
top tens and hold about four fifths of all points: the forecast-ledger freeze, the margins and readings register, the
recognizer error by voice, input stability under grid shift, position-matched contrasts, and the labeller's prior on
asks. Rankings were correlated, so rank sums overstate agreement. Two entries were vetoed and set aside: speaker
embeddings, speaker counts and a synthetic-voice screen (biometric processing of people who did not consent; the veto
extends to any per-creator proxy), and feedback contingency in readings for audience members (third parties who did not consent).

\paragraph{Classes and order of work.} Each entry's class is that of its primary part: (a)~do now, no-cost work on the
seen creators with stored fields and local computation, 88 entries (49 needing nothing more, 39 waiting for a ruling or
a stored fact); (b)~register for a future confirmation wave, 9 (a wave of the same roster tests persistence only;
confirmation needs fresh creators); (c)~unfunded primary part, 4; (d)~set aside, 5. The order of work is: step~0, the
deposits; step~1, instrument checks with frozen code; step~2, lexicon preconditions before any lexicon count; steps~3
to~5, descriptions of forecasts and time, of economy, audience and sampling, and of the claimed voice as speech; step~6,
design for any future collection. The lead adopted the programme with 13 rulings under the owner's delegation: among
them, the ledger hash is deposited alone, before any other deposit; listening by a person stays closed until an ethics or exemption
determination exists; word lists are written blind from the literature; a second attack pass precedes any novelty
sentence; and the latest-50+ recordings of the same roster are descriptive material, on which no class-b hypothesis is
tested.

\subsection{Margins, readings, intervals, tiers and multiplicity}
\label{app:panel3-readings}

\paragraph{Margins.} The programme stated no smallest effect for its primaries, so the lead fixed default margins by
measure type before any class-a run (deposit~2): a ratio outside 0.80 to 1.25; a difference in shares of 5 percentage
points; a standardised difference of 0.20 between-creator SD; an AUC 0.10 away from 0.50; a correlation or agreement
coefficient of 0.10; a count of creators or recordings is described only. An entry's own margin holds where it states
one. The floor rule: no reading is looser than ``the near 90\% creator-interval bound lies beyond half the smallest
effect''; reading A also needs every named control row in the same direction; estimators or references that disagree
give undetermined.

\paragraph{Readings.} Reading A (``beyond structure''): the interval lies wholly beyond the margin, in the entry's named
direction, under every admissible reference. Reading B (``accounted for''): the reference-adjusted interval lies wholly
inside the margin under every admissible reference. Undetermined: anything else, including disagreeing references and
a reference that does not hold its size at the count. Not computed: fewer than 5 eligible creators. Descriptions and
acts carry no reading, and several entries use their own words for A and B. A reading is a description, never a test of
H1--H10: readings, not findings.

\paragraph{Intervals and tiers.} Intervals are 90\%, by the method that r3-P30's calibration battery selects for the
design class at the entry's creator count (the row of the largest simulated count at or below it); where r3-P30 did not
simulate a design class, the t interval on creator means is used and the reading carries the words ``interval method
not calibrated by r3-P30 for this design class''. The split policy (r3-P31) assigns tiers before any outcome is read:
lists written blind from the literature run once on all non-development seen creators (L); data-tuned rules with 20 or
more outcome-blind eligible creators are built on an exploration half and run once on a sealed half (S); 10 to 19 use
two-fold cross-fitting (X); fewer are descriptions only (D). The registry's 48 development creators, already exposed to
prompt tuning, form the tuning pool and are never in the sealed half; the 23 non-development seen creators were drawn
once into halves of 11 and 12. The known leak is printed: every member and the lead had seen the exploratory round's
aggregates, so the sealed half guards only against tuning done after the deposit.

\paragraph{Multiplicity.} No family-wise adjustment is applied to exploratory readings. The programme has 56 distinct
primaries. If every true effect sat at half its margin, about 2.8 readings of A would be expected by chance at the
deposited one-sided 5\%, and up to about 6.1 for the share, standardised, AUC and rank-correlation classes (r3-P30's
simulations, not any outcome). This is a disclosure, not family-wise control. A reading A is never called a finding.

The programme's seven audio entries (r3-P12, r3-P08, r3-P11, r3-P09, r4-P03, r4-P15 and r4-P16) ran on 26 November 2026

recordings' stored audio. The audio addendum then served, from the pass's stored per-recording summaries alone, the
registered parts that had waited for audio (the lead's decision~106.4); it read, copied and decoded no audio. Both were
published together by pull request~121 (merged 20:41:45Z) after independent checks; their fix passes (decisions~109
to~114) left values not printed (privacy rule), merged or reworded, with no number changed. Each entry was written by a
model agent working for the lead. No person listened, no model or API call was made, and no GPU work was needed.
Tables~\ref{tab:panel3-audio} and~\ref{tab:panel3-audio-add} give each entry's and each part's question and reading, in

\paragraph{The instrument.} Tier~0 features: level, digital silence, acoustic pauses, syllable nuclei, syllabic
modulation, spectral flatness, bandwidth, background spectrum, a reverberation proxy and rule-based classes of frames
outside recognized speech. Tier~1 features: F0 in semitones relative to the recording's own median (the median itself is
never stored), intensity variability and spectral tilt over voiced frames. Nothing of Tier~2: no absolute pitch, formant,
embedding, speaker count or synthetic-voice score. Every sample outside admitted time is set to zero before any feature is
computed, and conversation and multi-presenter recordings are not copied, so no third party's voice is processed.
Acoustic features describe the sound of these recordings (delivery and recording conditions), never a speaker's state or
a source.

\paragraph{Battery, gate and A8.} A known-answer battery on constructed audio binds the readings; its recovery is an
upper bound. Its criterion~1 is not met for the rate slope (about 0.45, as the audio folder's common README prints it),
so acoustic rate rows never read ``within the margin (B)''; criterion~3 is not met for pitch range, so pitch-range rows
read neither way; and a composite profile reads a direction only if every feature in it may (the battery caps of
decision~106.3). The splice census sees changes of background spectrum only: session joins, music-bed onsets and
spectral changes, not same-session cuts or gain steps. A recording enters a delivery row when its speech-to-background
ratio is 25~dB or more. An entry or part whose primary has fewer than 12 eligible creators after this gate prints ``not
measurable acoustically in this corpus'' and, under decision~109.1 (a rule that does not depend on the data), no
estimate, interval or reading row and no figure (A8). A8 blocks r3-P09 and r4-P15, which stay without a reading in this
round; a run with the tuning pool added would be a new registration, on the fourth panel's agenda (decision~106.2).

\paragraph{Departure from the split policy's wording (decision~106.1).} Deposit~2's split policy builds a data-tuned rule
on exploration half~A and runs it once on sealed half~B (tier~S), or cross-fits it over the two halves (tier~X). Here
every data-tuned part (the instrument's development test, r3-P12's condition adjustment, the standardisation units,
r4-P03's threshold, r4-P15's feature scales, battery criteria 4, 5 and~7, and in the addendum r3-P15's scalings and
r3-P13's exploration) is fitted on the tuning pool~T only, never on either half, and each reading is made once on all
eligible creators. Reason: T sits on the exploration side and is never read for a split reading, so fitting on T alone
keeps both halves out of every fit. Limitation: half~B is no longer sealed for the acoustic measures described here.
Tiers print as letters only (decision~111).

computed and before any acoustic value, the background gate's included, was read for them. Both are published as

\subsection{The new-files round}
\label{app:newfiles}

After the latest-20+ analysis, the owner's next step was a round on the new files, run on the transcripts that existed,
with no further transcription (decisions~117 and~118.1). It is a descriptive persistence check on new
recordings of the seen creators: not a confirmation, not a test of H1--H10 and not a fresh-creator wave. A wave of the
same creators tests persistence only, and agreement is expected more often than it would be on fresh creators (rv-R2).
r3-P20's registered prediction is not tested, and r4-P20's rider on later uploads is not scored. The round's plan was
hashed at 2026-11-26T22:49:35Z, before any measure was computed on the new files and before any new transcript's text
was read; it is published as a redacted copy, and its hash is kept privately. The round was computed on 27 November
2026 by a model agent working for the lead, with no model or API call and no spend, and published by pull
request~124. Everything is rule-based or recognizer-based, not checked by a person, and compared by agreement, never
accuracy.

\paragraph{Scope.} The new uploads, which are later than the latest-20 capture, and the latest-50+ batch, which sits
mostly at older listing positions; each recording counts once, and only seen creators enter. Together they hold 250 to
259 recordings of 65 or more of the 71 seen creators, about 105 to 110 hours of audio (the round's scope table, in the
bands its plan fixed). Hold-out, other-group and unlisted recordings are dropped before any text is read and never
opened. Language was set to English by the transcription jobs, not detected per recording. A creator enters a row with 3
or more new recordings in the measure's base (for the tier-L lexicon rows, a non-development creator with 2 or more),
and creator sets print as bands or not at all. Deposit~2's halves do not
apply as a sealed check, because the new files are new recordings of the same creators; each row runs once, with no
split.

\paragraph{The persistence rule.} For each row, the published value P of the latest-20+ release or programme is fixed,
with a margin M fixed before any new value was read: a margin the output registered itself, otherwise deposit~2's
margin by measure type, and for rows read against a placebo, pseudo-strata or null band, the published band region;
counts and exposure-dependent statistics are described only. The new value's 90\% creator interval, by the original
output's own method with fixed settings, gives ``persists'' when it lies inside P's margin (or band region), ``shifts'' when it lies wholly outside, and ``undetermined'' otherwise. P's own interval does not
enter the rule, and the comparison is not independent of P, because the new recordings come from the same creators.
Before any new transcript was opened, every published value used was reproduced, to its printed decimals, by the
reused code from the latest-20+ inputs, and the reproduction record was hashed; the new transcripts were then opened
through an allow-list.

\paragraph{Readings, in the round's words.} Table~\ref{tab:newfiles} gives the rows by reading as the round's index
prints them. ``No row shifts. The rows that persist are those whose new interval is narrow enough to sit inside the
margin: recognizer and seam measures, stance wording, some topics, three lexicon classes.'' A creator contributes about
3 or 4 new recordings, against about 17 in the latest-20+ corpus, so most new 90\% intervals are wider than the margins
and most rows read undetermined. ``Persists'' is an equivalence description at one-sided 5\% per bound; with no
family-wise adjustment, a few ``shifts'' would have been unsurprising. Within-creator pairs were computed privately and
are not printed (decision~119.1).

\paragraph{Not computed.} The round lists every output it did not compute, with its printed reason: outputs that need
model-coded labels or an audio pass (the label packages, steps~1 to~5's label-based rows, the audio entries and the
addendum); outputs that need a second decode or a reference; the registered hypotheses' statistics (this round tests no
hypothesis); outputs that read spans of dated uploads within a channel, since the new files hold about one dated later
upload per creator; outputs that need 5 or more recordings per creator, depend on how many recordings each creator
contributes, or are counts of uploads a few days old; outputs whose published values are creator quantiles; and planned
rows whose published value was not reproduced before the new files were opened, which the round's order does not allow
adding afterwards.

\subsection{The fourth panel's programme}
\label{app:panel4}

On the owner's order of 26 November 2026, a fourth panel was asked to improve on the experiments, to study esoteric and
ET-contact theories and the documentaries about them, and to deliberate over three rounds toward tier-A layered research
questions. Its eight members are model agents playing roles, not people: three domain roles (esoteric and religious
history; UFO and ET contact studies; documentary and media studies), three method roles (linguistics, speech and
prosody; statistics and measurement; computational social science and language-model labelling), an emic reader who is
not a practitioner, and a denialist and sceptic attacker. All are of one model family, so their agreement is agreement
among agents, not a check by people, and none speaks for any community. In phase~0, three literature reviews with real
searches (esoteric, spiritualist and channelling scholarship; ET and UFO contact theories and their scholarship;
documentaries on channelling, contact and UFO religions) checked every entry under ruling~13 (it exists, can be found,
and says what is claimed) and marked how it was read; no documentary was viewed, and no query carried a creator, channel,
title or corpus text. Three rounds followed, moderated by the lead: proposals, cross-review, and convergence with a vote.
The lead accepted the draft (decision~131) and amended it after an independent check (decision~132);
the lead's decisions 125 to 132 are published in their public wording beside it.

\paragraph{Five tier-A layered research questions,} in the programme's words:
\begin{description}
\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item[RQ-A.] Who speaks, and on whose authority?
\item[RQ-B.] What organises the claimed voice's content: the creator, the source-name class, the topic or the milieu's
dated vocabularies?
\item[RQ-C.] Time and promise in a platform-surviving roster: when these creators speak of the future of contact and of
the world, how much of it could ever be scored as worded, which calendar do its windows keep, how are years once named
as targets spoken of after they pass, and how much can a roster of creators still uploading show at all?
\item[RQ-D.] How far can the instruments speak about voice?
\item[RQ-E.] How is channelled speech packaged for, and addressed to, the platform audience?
\end{description}

\paragraph{Entries and order of work.} The programme's twenty entries (f4-Q01 to f4-Q20: thirteen no-cost entries in
fourteen cards, and seven cards for a later wave or for a person or money) are designs, and none has run. The order of work puts the instruments before one deposit, and that deposit before any count and the readings. The deposit's forecast part,
deposit~5a, went ahead alone, before any count of the two entries it registers (f4-Q07, the frozen ledger, and
f4-Q19, after the window), because the largest group of the frozen ledger's windows becomes checkable from January 2027;
it was published by pull request~129. Everything in the programme is exploratory and not a test of H1--H10; its labels
are model-coded and not checked by a person, and comparisons are agreement, never accuracy. A date is said to have
``passed'', never to have ``failed''.

\subsection{Limitations of the programme}
\label{app:panel3-limits}

\paragraph{Preconditions from the attacks.} They limit the entries already run and bind any later registration (the
lead's decision after the second attack pass). Seam lock (N02, r3-P02): if the labeller's input carried decoder segment breaks or time stamps,
lock to the seams is built in, and the reading must say ``built in'', not ``found''. The grid-shift placebo (N03): a
shift moves both the window phase and the earlier text a decoder conditions on, so it isolates phase-locked marks only
where that text is held fixed or neither decode uses it. Window-shifted placebo
statements (N07) need a covariate for predicates that recur, because a shifted recurring prediction is often fulfilled
by base rate. Plural self-reference (N10) counts inclusive and exclusive ``we'' apart, since a lecturing register raises
the plural for any speaker. Qualifier clauses (N23) are counted only for families disjoint from the forecast detector's
cues. Control years (N24) are matched on topic, not only on anniversary demand. Dated vocabularies (N25) are read
against a register-matched placebo, since the claimed voice is the more formal register. A comparison with declared
characters (N15) fixes the declared kinds of character voice, and a reading per kind, before any collection.

\paragraph{Leads not opened.} Some works may already cover parts of the novelty wordings and were not read for the
claim (decision~96, item~9): reports of educational automated-scoring monitoring, which may cross engine versions with
new cohorts (N06); historical reports of heavy breathing at trance onset (N14); hashed
forecasts of an eruption-forecasting experiment and an on-chain commit-reveal arena for model forecasts (N21); and a
study of disclosure discourse on unidentified anomalous phenomena (N27). The ruling-12 record (\texttt{ruling12-attack/})
names them. They are not counted as searches, and none is cited as support.

\paragraph{Step 1.} The frozen instruments are identified only by alias and no item was coded under both interfaces, so
whether an alias resolved to one snapshot, and whether the interfaces coded alike, cannot be computed from stored runs;
only an anchor re-read (unfunded) could tell. r3-P30's choice rule can flip between neighbouring interval methods
within Monte Carlo error. The centring tolerance of the position references cannot be resolved by the plasmode at this
creator count. Punctuation-stripped labels are not stored at unit level among the seen instruments, so a
punctuation-free relabel stays unfunded.

\paragraph{Steps 2 to 5.} The lexicon counts are cue counts: sense is not resolved and precision is unknown until a
reader is funded. The tiers are small (23 creators for tier~L, 11 voice-eligible creators for tier~X), so many rows are
not computed or are capped; a run on more creators would be a new registration. Step~5 reads recognizer text only, not
audio. Deletions and the regular-upload tab bound every audience and economy reading: those readings describe a
platform-surviving roster's newest regular uploads, and associations at capture are not causal.

\paragraph{Audio entries.} Recovery was checked on constructed audio only, an upper bound, and no person listened. The
acoustic rate instrument does not meet its battery's slope criterion (criterion~1), so no acoustic rate row can read
``within the margin'', and pitch-range rows read neither way (decision~106.3); the splice census cannot see same-session
cuts or gain steps. Two entries and two addendum parts are not measurable acoustically in this corpus (A8). The re-decode
of the seen recordings on a shifted grid over the whole roster was not run (decision~106.4). Half~B is no longer sealed
for the acoustic measures (decision~106.1). The measured condition covariates are not exhaustive, so ``not explained by
measured condition'' is not ``speaker''.

\paragraph{The process.} Every member, attacker and search helper is a model agent of one family; their agreement is
agreement among agents, not a check by people. Members could read only public files, and the round-4 attacks covered
the claims unevenly. No person listened to any recording, and none will before an ethics or exemption determination
exists.
