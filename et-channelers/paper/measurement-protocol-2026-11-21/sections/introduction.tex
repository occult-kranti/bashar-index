\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Studies of podcasts and online video read speech through automatic transcripts \cite{clifton2020podcasts,litterer2024mapping,matos2026misinformation}, and social-science text is increasingly coded with language models \cite{gilardi2023chatgpt,ziems2024can,tornberg2024best}. Both steps leave marks: Whisper-family recognizers \cite{radford2022robust} write unspoken text, more often where the audio holds no voice \cite{koenecke2024careless}, and long recordings decoded in windows drift and repeat \cite{bain2023whisperx}. Where silence and long pauses are part of the content and no human transcript or person-coded label exists, these marks are the first thing to measure.

The creators studied here present recordings as messages channelled from non-human beings, a practice surveyed by \citet{klimo1987channeling} and studied ethnographically \cite{brown1997channeling}; work on religion online studies religious practice performed online \cite{helland2005online,campbell2012understanding}. A creator's move between a claimed voice, which the creator presents as a non-human source's, and their own voice is a change of footing \cite{goffman1979footing} in religious speech, where the sources of words are at issue \cite{keane1997religious}. Such recordings can hold time without speech, several voices and spoken openings and closings; only the first is measured (Section~\ref{sec:nonspeech}), as no speaker separation was run.

We transcribed recordings from the newest \NPerCreator{} public uploads of each of \NCreators{} creators we chose because they describe their practice as channelling, in contiguous 30-s windows decoded without earlier text (Section~\ref{sec:pipeline}), and set the output against every machine text available. We also report an internally registered (hashes and commits, no external deposit), then amended, protocol for model-coded labels, with a hold-out of whole speakers and ten hypotheses on the claimed voice, asks to buy or enrol, forecasts and shared vocabulary (Table~\ref{tab:hypotheses}).

Four points follow. (i)~Fixed-grid decoding leaves seams in segment starts, word onsets and pauses, which a 15-s grid shift with a placebo grid separates from the speech; whether a window-end courtesy phrase that appears to be inserted moves with the grid is not settled (Sections~\ref{sec:seams} and~\ref{sec:artefacts}). (ii)~Machine anchors locate disagreement at a few per cent of words but bound nothing about error (Section~\ref{sec:anchors}). (iii)~Creators carry much of each measure's variance, so inference must be at the creator level, and some word measures may already be off by the pre-set margin at the measured machine disagreement (Section~\ref{sec:variance}). (iv)~Exact creator-level tests under Holm's correction over ten hypotheses need at least \MinCreatorsOneSided{} (one-sided) or \MinCreatorsTwoSided{} (two-sided) eligible creators to pass its first step (0.005). H1, H4, H5, H6 and H9 fell below their minimums on the \NHoldout{}-creator hold-out, as announced for H1, H4 and H6 before any hold-out label, and instruments that passed their gates in design did not all pass on a hold-out reliability sample, so H2 was not run; none passed the Holm step (Section~\ref{sec:tests}). Points (i) to (iii) audit the pipeline without a reference (Section~\ref{sec:measure}); point (iv) and the lessons come from the protocol (Sections~\ref{sec:protocol} to~\ref{sec:deviations}).

Three limits apply throughout. No seen creator (one whose transcripts were read during design; Section~\ref{sec:data}) has a transcript known to be made by people, so no word error rate is reported, and agreement is not a measure of correctness. Labels are model-coded and not checked by a person; the owner declined a small person check (A8). The creators are a judgment sample, so nothing is said of how common the practice is or whether it grows. No measure in this study can tell whether a non-human source speaks; every measure describes the speech in these recordings and how the creators present it. The paper reports no finding about channelling, and \ResIntro; claims in the recordings, including claims about their source, are treated as claims. The owner is the project owner, a person; the lead is an AI agent (an Anthropic model) that took the other recorded decisions under the owner's written delegation, with logged reasons, and was not allowed to weaken the hold-out rule or a registered rule after results were seen.
