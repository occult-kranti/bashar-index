What the badges mean

REC
Recording this source at 1×.
BUF
Recording paused while the source buffers or is paused.
CHECK
Local media and coverage validation is running.
DONE
No eligible job remains for this worker.
ERR
Capture held. Hover the extension button for its reason.

You activate each tab yourself, up to four. The extension can continue through eligible YouTube jobs in those tabs. Ads, source changes, missing coverage, or an interrupted connection hold the result for review. This is re-encoded browser audio; it is separate from native source downloads.

The extension does not read or export cookies, credentials, personal profiles, or signed media URLs. Closing a tab or Chrome stops its recording. Click the extension button again to stop one tab.
