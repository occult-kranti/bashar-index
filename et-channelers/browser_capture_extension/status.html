What the badges mean

LOOK
Reading this tab’s native audio format descriptors.
INFO
Inspection ready here; no media transfer has started.
ERR
Inspection or an earlier capture was held. Its reason appears here and in the toolbar tooltip.

Real-time recording is parked. Earlier incomplete recordings remain held for review and are not counted as complete source downloads. The next native-download step will require a bounded transport proof.

The extension does not read or export cookies, credentials, personal profiles, or signed media URLs. Inspection does not need the local archive service to be running.
